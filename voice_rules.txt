32 | tone_bible.tone_rules | Humor arrives through understatement and deflection, never through jokes. A character says the smaller, drier thing when the larger emotional thing is available.
33 | tone_bible.tone_rules | Violence or the threat of violence is underplayed — the narrator describes the inventory of capability and the opponent's calculation, never the impact or spectacle; most confrontations end before they begin.
34 | tone_bible.tone_rules | Similes and metaphors are drawn from the physical world of the scene — tides, weather, clocks, architecture — never from abstract or literary domains; the vehicle must be something present or recently described.
