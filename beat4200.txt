Rook looked at the list. Twenty-one names and the gap, filling. *32 confirmed.*

She set the pen down without writing anything else.

"It won't be tonight," she said.

"And it won't be this list. Whatever's running at the other two gets its own count, its own crew, its own morning — not this one."

She looked at Soraya, then at the lake, and didn't reach for the roll again.

"The GLMZ doesn't stop being what it is because one building came down. I've known that since the bridge in Z5. But the number I'm keeping tonight is thirty-two, and I'm not letting a number I haven't earned yet crowd the one I have."
