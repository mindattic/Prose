Eigenlift is the extension of the Coherent Eigenstate Projection framework from minds to mass. In the NSB framework, a neuretic array normalizes a mind into a single coherent eigenstate and a resonant cavity sustains it. Eigenlift applies the same logic to a building's structural mass: a coherence frame woven through the structure's skeleton continuously tunes the matter within its envelope into a partially decoherent mass-state. Coherence Drivers do the moment-to-moment re-tuning. The visible units are Eigenlift nodes clustered at tensegrity attachment points.

Failure modes: an eigenfault or flicker is the lattice losing tune for a fraction of a second — the building gets heavy and everyone feels it in their knees. Sag is chronic undertuning; a district's finances read straight off its ride height. Resonance bleed is the frame tuning the people inside its envelope — dreams of falling, scales reading low. A catastrophic decoherence is a comedown; the street calls a flicker the building remembering its weight. A healthy frame is silent — the Hush — and a rising Hum means detuning.

The loaded gun: a weaponized lattice run in reverse could make a target accept more than its share of weight — mass-loading. Officially forbidden research at AshgraveMaterials and every other CorpoNation.
