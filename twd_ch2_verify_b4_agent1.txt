He wasn't thinking about the door. He was thinking about the node two floors down, the one on the service corridor, the one that had just gone quiet. Not dark. Quiet, which was worse. A node went dark when you burned it. It went quiet when someone asked it, very politely, to look the other way.

He'd seen a system die like that exactly once. Three years back, a contractor ran a training scenario for Bear's crew - Bear, the fixer whose channel neither of them had ever had to explain to the other. The contractor was a small man who moved like he was sorry for the space he took up. He'd put seventeen nodes to sleep in eleven minutes and never touched one of them.
