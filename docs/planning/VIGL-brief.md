## 2. Arc Contribution {#SS-BRIEF-VIGL-§2}

Entos-universe arcs this story advances:

- **[x] The Liturgy's Silence** — the Liturgy's suppression of evidence that Scrying damages
  the membrane, and the Canon Immaculate's attempt to use the Oculus Memoriae as leverage.
  How: Canon Prelacze's commission of the theft is the first confirmed act; Lyra's refusal
  to hand over the Relic is the first public counter-move.

- **[x] Scrying as systemic slavery** — the normalization mechanism is made visible through
  Rowe's testimony and Orim Zebulun's complicity. How: Rowe's origin (Sphere 31) establishes
  that the Liturgy maintains a numbered catalogue of harvested Spheres; Lyra as researcher
  confronts her own institutional participation.

- **[x] Transmutation beyond Liturgy control** — the Eaters of Monsters demonstrate that
  Transmutation is not inherently Liturgy-owned. How: they appear in the Schmalburg raid and
  perform unsanctioned Transmutation that produces functional, non-catastrophic results.

- **[x] Membrane biology** — establishes that the membrane is a living organism that can be
  injured and heals slowly. How: Lyra observes the Forge Hearth zone; the Relic demonstrates
  that membrane contact accumulates as memory; Vashe's scry failure at Forge Hearth is the
  first documented instance of a minor scryer being refused.

- **[ ] None** — not applicable; this story advances multiple Entos arcs

## 8. Thematic Complement {#SS-BRIEF-VIGL-§8}

**Theme:** The cost of loyalty to institutions that require you not to see what they do

**Register:** Arthurian high fantasy + cosmic horror; Buehlman (*Between Two Fires*) +
Miyazaki (*Nausicaä*) — formal medieval register with industrial intrusion; combat is physical
and costly; the supernatural is not comfortable

**What this story contributes:**
- The first produced Entos novel — establishes the register, world texture, and character types
  for all future Entos stories
- The normalization of Scrying-as-slavery rendered visible from inside an institutional practitioner's POV
- The membrane as organism, not resource — first direct confrontation with this fact
- A five-person found party with distinct voices and complementary failures

**What would be duplicated if this story didn't exist:** Nothing — it's the foundation document
for the Entos universe's fictional production

## 9. Structural Blueprint Seed {#SS-BRIEF-VIGL-§9}

**Resolution mode:** Mixed — external success (Relic recovered, Canon refused) with
significant cost (Ardea dead, second Myrmidon deserted, Lyra's Liturgy standing now dangerous)

**Moral polarity:** Ambivalent — no House is the villain; the system is the villain; Owain
and Lyra are mirror images at different stages of the same faith

**Ending style:** Avalanche — the Canon confrontation arrives before Lyra has planned it; she
acts from who she has become, not from strategy

**Escalation curve:**
- Beats 1–3: Institutional relic theft → illegal border crossing (low stakes, clear mission)
- Beats 4–6: The deserter found → language acquisition begins → Atrament's scale revealed
- Beats 7–9: Schmalburg raid (death + transformation/fusion) → Forge Hearth (cosmic horror) → Lacerta pirate
- Beats 10–12: Rowe's testimony (personal devastation) → Pallor confrontation (physical climax) → the Relic's perception
- Beats 13–14: The Long Seat's eighth theory → Canon confrontation (moral climax)

**Event-type palette:** Pursuit / combat / revelation / testimony / encounter / confrontation

**Subplot thread:** Kressida Haun's relationship with her rig — she cannot separate herself from
what the system did to her; mirrors Rowe's testimony; both are about the impossibility of
reverting after the extraction

**Form device:** The scrying ritual as recurring structural marker — Vashe scrys at Beats 1, 3,
and 8; each scry shows the membrane in a different state; the third scry is refused, which
marks the book's threshold into the final act

**Intertextual anchors:**
1. *Nausicaä of the Valley of the Wind* (Miyazaki) — protagonist loyal to a world she cannot stop seeing clearly
2. *The Name of the Rose* (Eco) — institutional knowledge as power; the archive as real battlefield
3. *All Quiet on the Western Front* (Remarque) — war's waste seen from inside loyalty
4. *Final Fantasy VI* — found party from the margins; each stop exposes a different institution's failure
