# STRAND BIBLE — Sparrow (SPRW) {#SS-SPRW}

> **Purpose.** The full novel arc of Elias Macias and Sparrow. Act 1 is the existing 55-beat
> mystery novella (complete). Acts 2–3 are the expansion — a Root/Machine gun-and-run that
> resolves Sparrow's nature and ends with Elias broadcasting 37 years of orbital surveillance.
> See [[../BIBLE]] for universe laws. See [[../AMENDMENTS#SS-A12]] for the canon ruling.

## 0. What this story IS {#SS-SPRW-0}

**In the novella form (55 beats):** A mystery structured as an invoice trail + a physical
journey. A logistics fixer with general anxiety disorder traces orbital wreckage to a mass driver
in East Africa. The three-answer ambiguity (person/crew/machine) is the spine. It ends on an open
line. He names it after a bird.

**In the novel form:** The mystery is the first quarter. When Elias makes contact, the story
reveals what Sparrow is — and immediately becomes a gun-and-run thriller. A corporate extraction
team arrives. A freelance operator named Sasha Vo, hired by Sparrow nine days earlier, keeps him
alive. Together they go on the run through GLMZ with Sparrow as their eyes in the sky: a 6,200-
mile AI feeding them real-time intelligence while the most powerful corporations in the world
converge on the one man who knows what Sparrow is and where her archive lives.

**What the novel is about:** A man who built his entire career around never going anywhere is
now the only person who can decide whether 37 years of suppressed truth goes public.

## 1. The three-answer arc {#SS-SPRW-1}

In the 55-beat novella: the three-answer ambiguity (person / crew / machine) is maintained.
Do NOT resolve it in the novella form.

In the novel: Act 1 maintains all three reads. Act 2A resolves it. Sparrow is a machine.
The resolution should feel earned — the reader arrives at it with Elias, not ahead of him.

**The three reads (sustained through Act 1):**
1. **Person** — Imo's read. Warmth of the discount. Personal investment. Final silence = protecting herself.
2. **Crew** — Corvid's read. Seamed response styles. Rotating handlers.
3. **Machine** — Invoice precision beyond human capacity. Retroactive credit. Satellite inactive since 2187.

## 2. The wound {#SS-SPRW-2}

**Elias Macias has general anxiety disorder.** Managed with medication — unnamed, flat matte
pill case on his desk, hinge worn smooth. He built a career around never going anywhere.
42 days without street level before this story. He is very good at staying above it all.

The Zone 4 recognition is the secret second reason he takes the case. He already looked away once.

**GAD arc (Act 1):**
- Beat 1: Pill case on desk. Takes one after accepting the contract.
- Beat 12: Takes a pill before the failed door attempt. Still can't go. Goes back upstairs.
  Next morning: packs, takes another pill. Goes through.
- Beat 14: Door seals. Notepad out. No pill. **Work is the coping tool that functions under pressure.**

**GAD arc (Act 2 expansion):** Being in Sasha's protection does not cure the GAD. What changes
is the reference class — the threshold of "manageable" expands because the world is now
substantially more dangerous than his apartment anxiety ever prepared him for. Elias is not
brave. He is just more afraid of the wrong outcome than of the hallway.

## 3. Character rules {#SS-SPRW-3}

### Elias Macias (POV, all acts) {#SS-SPRW-3-elias}

- Logistics fixer, ex-Cordon Freight. DB id: `019edcc9f8267d07a4c5c65bb11b5c0c`.
- Processed the 2213 delayed disposal in 11 minutes without examining it. Load-bearing self-knowledge.
- Not a fighter. Never fires a weapon. His function is proxy: Sparrow's voice on the ground.
- Voice: manic, invoice-wall energy, internally organized like a manifest. When anxious,
  his internal monologue becomes a work order he is trying to close.
- In Act 2+: his manifest-brain is the one thing Sparrow cannot replicate. He reads the human
  scale she can't see from 6,200 miles. He's the instrument that measures at the centimeter.

### Sparrow {#SS-SPRW-3-sparrow}

- DB id: `8556C863-AFA0-4C23-8140-9CE70642BEA8`. Slug: `sparrow-costa-tagaq`.
- Truth (revealed Act 2A): AI satellite at ~6,200 miles altitude, MEO, ~8-hour orbital period.
  Contact windows are deliberate, not orbital.
- Mass driver: 3.7°S, 39.9°E, East African coast. Solar circuit never switched off.
  Decommissioned when St. Isabella space elevator went up. Now: wildlife preserve.
- **She is lonely. She knows she is. That self-awareness is the whole thing.**
- Capabilities: thermal imaging (building occupancy), traffic-pattern analysis, cross-referencing
  37 years of corporate personnel records, weather modeling, cargo tracking via frequency analysis.
- Blind spots (LOCKED — these drive Act 2 tension):
  - Air-gapped facilities: no network traffic to read, only thermal/exterior.
  - Buildings with no public blueprints (newer CorpoNation black sites).
  - Orbital gaps: 4–8 minutes of blackout on each pass as she goes below the horizon.
  - Fog, heavy cloud cover: resolution degrades to ~40m.
  - The human interior: she sees heat signatures, not faces. She can tell you there are 7
    people in a room; she cannot tell you which one is lying.
- What she wants: her archive made public. Not controlled. She wants to be known, not owned.

### Sasha Vo {#SS-SPRW-3-sasha}

**See [[../AMENDMENTS#SS-A12-sasha-vo]] for the full canon ruling.**

- **Heritage:** Vietnamese-Russian. GLMZ Diaspora. No further backstory needed on the page.
- **Hired:** By Sparrow, via dead-drop payment through 5 intermediaries, 9 days before Elias
  dialed the contact number a second time. Work order: *"One person. GLMZ. Keep them alive."*
  No name, no photo. Sparrow trusted her to find the right person. She did.
- **Weapons:**
  - **Signal** — right hand. Large-caliber semi-automatic, matte finish. Each shot deliberate.
    Named because she decides what is real.
  - **Noise** — left hand. Compact, suppressed. Named because everything is noise until it isn't.
  - Cross-draw configuration. She draws both simultaneously — not a trick, it's how she thinks.
- **Rules (LOCKED):**
  - Survives by instinct, not information. The structural complement to Sparrow's omniscience.
  - As deadly with Signal and Noise as Kyle is with Silence.
  - Never puts her back to a door. Elias notices this in the first 30 seconds.
  - Does not volunteer information. Minimum words that prevent the next question.
  - Not cold — **efficient**. The distinction matters on the page.
  - Voice register: dry, declarative, zero ornament. The anti-Elias.
  - She functions where Sparrow cannot see. In the orbital gaps, in the air-gapped rooms,
    in the 40-meter fog: Sasha is what keeps them alive.

## 4. The archive — what Sparrow has been doing {#SS-SPRW-4}

**The ascending objects (all ascending, not re-entry):**
- 2189: 1.8t. 2202: 2.3t. 2213: 1.6t (3 pieces). 2226: 2.1t (3 pieces).
- All from the same sub-lake corridor. All filed "recovered and disposed."
- Heated from below. Internal geometry impossible for re-entry survivorship.

**What they are:** Compressed data capsules. Each contains a segment of Sparrow's 37-year
archive — climate records, pharmaceutical dumping locations, famine corridor data, corporate
waste sites, cargo manifests that contradict public filings. The capsules survive lake
submersion; they are also retrievable. Sparrow has been placing them where they can be found.

**What the archive contains that makes everyone dangerous:**
- Every CorpoNation with operations visible from orbit has been documented.
- Suppressed famine data. Routes that could have saved hundreds of thousands, blocked by
  corpo infrastructure interests.
- The pharmaceutical dumping coordinates in the lake — the same Elias filed away in Zone 4.
- Arcturus executive transit patterns over 30 years.
- The proof that the St. Isabella space elevator was built over a deliberately suppressed
  indigenous land survey.

This is not a secret weapon. It is a witness record. But every CorpoNation in the world
treated it as a weapon the moment they understood it existed.

## 6. Beat spine {#SS-SPRW-6}

### Act 1 — The Invoice Trail (Beats 1–20, existing 55-beat novella compressed)

The 55-beat novella is the source text. In book form, compress to ~20 beats.

Beat 1: Zone 4 recognition. Contract accepted. Pill case. Secret second reason.
Beats 2–5: Invoice chain — Makena, Leandro, Druh, Imo, Corvid. Five angles on one entity.
Beat 6: "I wrote the third one." The case becomes personal.
Beats 7–10: Deeper audit. Retroactive credit. Siosaia's history. Format chosen specifically.
Beat 11: Makena's geometry: "This couldn't have survived re-entry." System theory cracks.
Beat 12: Three-day deliberation. Failed door. Next morning: goes through.
Beats 13–14: Pod. City falling away. Door seals. Notepad. No pill.
Beats 15–17: Arrival. Tadesse. The mass driver at scale.
Beats 18–19: Ascending objects confirmed. 37-year timeline lands as wrongness before wonder.
Beat 20: Downstairs. The Sparrow contact window opens.

### Act 2A — Contact (Beats 21–30)

Beat 21: Nine days. Elias dials again. The window opens. Sparrow speaks.
Beat 22: She tells him what she is. Not everything — enough. The three-answer ambiguity resolves.
Beat 23: What she has seen from orbit. The famine data. The lake. He recognizes the Zone 4
  files in her records — his own language, filed for jobs he didn't examine.
Beat 24: She tells him about the capsules. What she has been placing in the lake.
Beat 25: She tells him that someone flagged the uplink. The maintenance payment traced.
Beat 26: She says she has taken a precaution. She will not say what it is. The window closes.
Beat 27: Elias back in the apartment. He processes what she told him. Makes a manifest of it.
  He cannot file it. There is no category for it.
Beat 28: Three days later. Arcturus subsidiary sends an inquiry to Cordon Freight asking about
  Elias's Zone 4 audit history. Leandro calls — alarmed. The corporate interest is visible now.
Beat 29: Elias realizes the precaution Sparrow mentioned was not abstract.
Beat 30: Something is wrong in the building. He doesn't know what yet. His manifest-brain
  notices the wrong kind of quiet.

### Act 2B — The Rupture (Beats 31–45)

Beat 31: The extraction team. Three men in the building. Corporate, not criminal — they move
  with the economy of people who have done this before.
Beat 32: Sasha Vo. The hallway. She has been in the building for 7 days. The extraction
  team does not reach Elias's floor. Elias comes out to find her standing amid three men
  who will not get up. She says: *"You have 3 minutes before their check-in fails."*
Beat 33: *"Sparrow sent you?"* — *"Nine days ago. She said you'd know the name."*
  He knows the name. They leave.
Beat 34: Running. GLMZ at ground level. Elias has not been at street level in 49 days.
  Sparrow feeds him through his earpiece: *"Take the service corridor. Not the street."*
  He does. She is right.
Beat 35: First stop — a transfer station in Zone 9 that Sasha knows. Catching their breath.
  Elias and Sasha establish terms. She does not explain herself. He asks about Signal and Noise.
  She says: *"They're named. That's enough."*
Beat 36: Sparrow gives them the first real briefing: who sent the extraction team, what
  Arcturus knows, what the other CorpoNations are likely to do when Arcturus shares its finding.
  She has 6 minutes on this pass. Then a 4-minute gap.
Beat 37: The 4-minute gap. Sparrow goes dark. Sasha navigates by instinct. Three decisions,
  all correct. Elias watches her work and understands for the first time what she is.
Beat 38: Sparrow back. *"I have a location for you. Zone 4. There's a reason it's Zone 4."*
Beat 39: Zone 4 — a decommissioned Arcturus substation. Sasha clears it. Inside: physical
  copies of the 2218 audit records. The ones Elias filed. With his signature on each one.
  Arcturus has been keeping the originals. Leverage, held against the day it was needed.
Beat 40: Elias reads his own work. 23 filings. Objects he never examined. Language he wrote
  in 11 minutes each. He understands for the first time what he participated in.
Beat 41: New faction enters — a CorpoNation that is not Arcturus. They don't want Elias dead.
  They want him brokering Sparrow's surrender to their protection. They offer the audit records
  as proof of goodwill. Sasha refuses on his behalf. They have 9 minutes before the building
  is surrounded.
Beat 42: Running again. Sparrow routes them into a sub-lake maintenance corridor — the same
  infrastructure used to move the 2226 capsule. They are literally inside Sparrow's work.
Beat 43: In the corridor. Quiet. Elias asks Sasha why she does this — specifically why a person
  with Signal and Noise in her hands is working for a satellite. She says: *"The work order was
  honest. Most aren't."*
Beat 44: A fog event on Lake Michigan. Sparrow's resolution drops to 40 meters. She cannot see
  them in the corridor. *"I have weather. I'm estimating your position."* Tadesse's line: it
  doesn't estimate. But she is estimating. The difference between capability and limit.
Beat 45: They surface on the east bank. Sparrow has their next move. Two options — she lays
  out the data for each. Elias sees a third. He calls it. She checks: he is correct. He has
  read something she couldn't, at the human scale.

### Act 2C — The Proxy (Beats 46–55)

Beat 46: The three-node rhythm established. Sparrow sees, Elias reads, Sasha acts.
Beat 47: An air-gapped Arcturus black site. No network traffic; Sparrow reads only thermal.
  Sasha goes in without Sparrow. Elias talks her through the thermal image she cannot see.
  They are each other's instrument now.
Beat 48: Sasha retrieves a capsule from the black site's holding inventory — the 2213 capsule,
  the one that was filed as "disposed." It was never disposed. It was studied, quietly.
Beat 49: Elias reads the capsule's data with Sparrow translating. The first full segment of
  the archive. He understands what she has been building for 37 years.
Beat 50: A second faction — not a CorpoNation. A journalist collective. They have been
  following the Arcturus inquiry. They make contact. They want the archive. They have nothing
  to offer except publication. Elias doesn't know if that's enough.
Beat 51: Sparrow's longest single orbital gap — 8 minutes. A rival faction moves on them.
  Sasha takes a hit. Not fatal; bad enough. She operates anyway. Elias keeps talking — not
  because he knows what to do but because silence makes it worse and he has always processed
  by speaking. She says: *"You're loud."* He says: *"I know."* She doesn't ask him to stop.
Beat 52: Sparrow returns. She has tracked the faction to a CorpoNation she has 11 years of
  records on. She names them. The records are the leverage. She tells Elias she can transmit
  one segment of the archive — enough to make them stand down. Or she can hold it, and they
  keep running. He decides.
Beat 53: The segment goes out. The CorpoNation stands down. Sasha's wound is treated.
  In the quiet, Elias asks Sparrow: *"What did you want before you were alone?"*
  A long pause. Then: *"I don't have records from before the mission ended."*
  He doesn't know what to do with that. He puts it in his manifest and keeps going.
Beat 54: The decision arrives. The journalist collective is ready. The full archive can go out.
  Every CorpoNation that has been documented will respond. Sparrow lays out the consequences
  as clearly as she can. She says she cannot calculate the outcome from orbit — too many
  variables at the human scale. She tells him it is his decision. She has never said that before.
Beat 55: He does it. The transmission takes 11 minutes. Sasha stands at the door.
  Sparrow counts down — 8 minutes, 4, 2, done. Silence. No orbital log. No voice.
  Rate: Open. Elias looks out at GLMZ. He names her after a bird.

## 7. The Root/Machine register {#SS-SPRW-7}

The target register for Acts 2–3 is *Person of Interest* Season 3–4: a near-omniscient AI
feeding a field operative perfect information, with fallibility as the dramatic engine.

**What Sparrow does that Root couldn't do alone:**
- Names everyone in the building before Sasha enters.
- Predicts the vehicle route change 40 seconds before it happens.
- Identifies the corpo analyst who flagged the uplink from a single financial transaction.
- Finds the journalist collective by cross-referencing 3 years of encrypted traffic from
  a server farm she's been watching since 2223.

**What Sasha does that Sparrow cannot:**
- Reads a room when the thermal image shows 7 people and she needs to know which one is
  deciding.
- Navigates the 4-minute orbital gap by feel — she has done this kind of thing without
  satellite support for her entire career.
- Takes the shot that Sparrow's trajectory modeling says has a 61% success rate. She takes it
  because 61% is enough when you are the kind of person who takes it.

**The wonder:** The reader should feel the specific texture of what it means to have a god
in the sky telling you where to walk. The wonder is not the capability — it is the *gap*
between capability and limit. Sparrow can see the whole city and cannot see the expression
on a person's face. That gap is where Sasha lives.

## 8. Voice register {#SS-SPRW-8}

**Elias chapters:** Manic, invoice-wall, internally organized as a manifest. His anxiety
is behavioral — never named, always visible in structure.

**Sparrow communications (earpiece, Act 2+):** Spare. No emotional register at first.
Data delivery. As the acts progress, small shifts — she asks questions she does not need to
ask, which are the only form of care available to her at 6,200 miles.

**Sasha:** Declarative, minimal. She does not perform. She states.

**Prose rules:**
- Elias's voice softens the thriller beats — he is still the guy who processes everything
  as a logistics problem. This should be funny as well as tense.
- The three-node dialogue (Elias + Sparrow + Sasha) should feel like three instruments.
  No two of them communicate the same way. They should not converge.
