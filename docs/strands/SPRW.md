# STRAND BIBLE — Sparrow (SPRW) {#SS-SPRW}

> **Purpose.** The dry spine of Elias Macias's courage arc: a man who has built his career
> around never going anywhere, forced to go somewhere because the loop won't close otherwise.
> See [[../BIBLE]] for universe laws. **Do not resolve Sparrow's nature in any strand except
> this one.** The three-answer ambiguity (person/crew/machine) is the story's spine.

## 0. What this story IS {#SS-SPRW-0}

A mystery structured as an invoice trail + a physical journey. A logistics fixer with general
anxiety disorder traces orbital wreckage with a non-terrestrial signature found in Lake Michigan.
The second half earns the first: a man who has never left his apartment discovers the thing
he's been investigating exists in the physical world and requires him personally.

It ends on an open line. No voice. He names it after a bird.

**This is NOT:**
- A Kyle story. Kyle does not appear.
- A conspiracy thriller. The scale is deliberate: one fixer, one invoice trail, one mass driver.
- A resolution story. The three-answer ambiguity (person/crew/machine) is the deliberate
  architecture. The 20-beat novella maintains it. The book-expansion resolves it. Keep them
  as separate products.
- A story about a satellite fight. The danger is paperwork and distance and self.

## 1. The three answers {#SS-SPRW-1}

Every reader finishes with one defensible reading:

   going dark to protect herself.

   dissolved.
3. **Machine** — Invoice precision beyond human capacity. Retroactive credit from an
   unexamined job. Satellite listed inactive since 2187. Silence = decommission or no mouth.

**Lock:** Panel complaint ("satellite reveal too clean/early") is LOCKED as author design.
The three-reader-defensible ending is the story's spine. Do not resolve it in the 55-beat form.

## 2. The wound (behavioral, never diagnosed) {#SS-SPRW-2}

**Elias Macias has general anxiety disorder.** Managed with medication — unnamed, flat matte
pill case on his desk, hinge worn smooth. He has built a career around never going anywhere.
Forty-two days without being at street level. He is very good at staying above it all.

The zone where he processed the 2213 disposal was Zone 4 — and he wrote "recovered and
disposed, Zone 4 corridor closure confirmed" in 11 minutes without examining it. The Zone 4
recognition is the secret second reason he takes the case. He has already looked away once.

**The GAD arc:**
- Beat 1: Pill case on desk. Takes one after accepting the contract.
- Beat 12: Takes a pill before the failed door attempt. Still can't go through. Goes back
  upstairs. Next morning: packs, takes another pill. "He has already been here once this
  week." Goes through.
- Beat 14: Door seals. Notes the irreversibility. Takes out the notepad. No pill — the
  notepad is what gets him through the tunnel. **Courage arc: pills don't solve it. Work
  (the notepad) is the coping tool that actually functions under pressure.**

**Rule:** Anxiety is behavioral, never diagnosed on the page.

## 7. Register and voice {#SS-SPRW-7}

**Elias chapters:** Ground-level GLMZ. Manic, funny, invoice-wall energy. He processes
the world in logistics units. When anxious, his internal monologue becomes a manifest.

**Sparrow orbital logs (book form only):** Dated. Spare. Third-person-distant. She notes
things: fires, shipping patterns, the specific color of Lake Michigan on a given day. She
mentions once that someone called from a GLMZ exchange asking about a maintenance account.
She doesn't say more. Reader figures out the collision before Elias does.

- Resolve Sparrow's nature in the 55-beat form.

  conclusions: by filing.
