# STRAND BIBLE — Sparrow (SPRW) {#SS-SPRW}

> **Purpose.** The full, realized arc of **Elias Macias and Sparrow** — a standalone two-minds
> novelette. A logistics fixer with an anxiety disorder traces orbital wreckage to a dead mass
> driver in East Africa, makes contact with the intelligence on the other end, and the back half of
> the story becomes a sustained conversation between two complete cognitive architectures that can
> only ever understand part of each other.
>
> **There is no Sasha Võ and no gun-and-run in this story.** The SS-A12 thriller plan that once
> lived in this bible has been migrated out to **Steppin Razor** ([[SRZR]]); see
> [[../AMENDMENTS#SS-A20]]. Sparrow is finished and must not be disturbed by that strand.
>
> See [[../BIBLE]] for universe laws. Canonical design: [[../AMENDMENTS#SS-A7]].

## 0. What this story IS {#SS-SPRW-0}

**Thematic register — "An Anthropologist on Mars" (LOCKED).** Sparrow and Elias do not merely
communicate differently — they *perceive* differently. Neither is deficient. Each is a complete
cognitive architecture that cannot fully inhabit the other's phenomenology. The story explores what
partial, complementary understanding looks like when each party can only sense part of the elephant.
Play this straight, not as tragedy: the limitation is the condition, not the failure.

**The realized form (55 beats).** A mystery structured as an invoice trail + a physical journey,
followed by contact. The first movement is the three-answer ambiguity (person / crew / machine). The
journey resolves it at the source. The long final movement is the two-minds conversation: what it is
to have a witness six thousand miles up who can hold thirty-seven years without fatigue and cannot
feel a floor flex underfoot. It ends on an open contract line — `RATE: OPEN` — and a man on a
balcony who has learned to stand in the cold air and keep his count.

**What the story is about:** a man who built his entire life around never going anywhere, and the
non-human intelligence that needed exactly his kind of attention at the centimeter scale.

## 1. The three-answer arc {#SS-SPRW-1}

Through the first two movements the ambiguity is sustained — **person / crew / machine** — and only
resolved at and after the source site. Sparrow is a machine. The resolution is **earned through
contact**, not through a thriller turn: the reader arrives at it with Elias, never ahead of him.

**The three reads (sustained through the mystery):**
1. **Person** — Imo's read. Warmth of the discount. Personal investment. Final silence = protecting herself.
2. **Crew** — Corvid's read. Seamed response styles. Rotating handlers.
3. **Machine** — Invoice precision beyond human capacity. Retroactive credit. Satellite inactive since 2187.

## 2. The wound {#SS-SPRW-2}

**Elias Macias has general anxiety disorder.** Managed with medication — unnamed, flat matte pill
case on his desk, hinge worn smooth. He built a career around never going anywhere. He has not been
at street level in ~42 days before the story opens. He is very good at staying above it all.

**Where he lives (canon).** **Apartment 11134 — the 111th floor** of a ferrocement stack that wasn't
built to have a 111th floor (the upper half added in a 2209 CorpoNation special-authorization
sprint). The height is a deliberate scale cue: a *common* GLMZ condo stack rises higher than the
tallest tower the old Midwest ever raised — an ordinary residence in a city where ordinary is
vertical. From the 111th floor the street is both literally and emotionally a long way down; the
descent itself is part of the wound. (Replaces the earlier "forty-second floor"; see SS-A20.)

The Zone 4 recognition is the secret second reason he takes the case. He already looked away once.

**GAD arc:**
- Beat 1: Pill case on desk. Takes one after accepting the contract.
- Beat 12: Takes a pill before the failed door attempt. Still can't go. Goes back upstairs. Next
  morning: packs, takes another pill. Goes through.
- Beat 14: Door seals. Notepad out. No pill. **Work is the coping tool that functions under pressure.**
- Post-Mombasa: field-visit frequency rises; transit time 111th-floor-to-street shortens. The cure
  is not bravery — it is a slow widening of the threshold of "manageable," rendered behaviorally and
  sympathetically, never named.

## 3. Character rules {#SS-SPRW-3}

### Elias Macias (POV) {#SS-SPRW-3-elias}

- Logistics fixer, ex-Cordon Freight. DB id: `019EDCC9-F826-7D07-A4C5-C65BB11B5C0C`. Apt 11134, floor 111.
- Processed the 2213 delayed disposal in 11 minutes without examining it. Load-bearing self-knowledge.
- Not a fighter. His function is attention: the human instrument that measures at the centimeter.
- Voice: manic, invoice-wall energy, internally organized like a manifest. When anxious, his
  internal monologue becomes a work order he is trying to close.
- In the contact movement: his manifest-brain is the one thing Sparrow cannot replicate. He reads
  the human scale she can't resolve from 6,200 miles — the pavement that *felt* wrong, the calm of
  the returned children, the texture of a floor that has given up the pretense of being solid.

### Sparrow {#SS-SPRW-3-sparrow}

- DB id: `8556C863-AFA0-4C23-8140-9CE70642BEA8`. Slug: `sparrow-costa-tagaq`. Species: `ai`.
- **A non-organic life.** Sparrow is alive in the sense that matters here: a self-maintaining,
  goal-directed system that has held its own orbit and run its own program for thirty-seven years,
  correcting itself, choosing to persist, and acting to continue a state of the world it committed
  to. She is not organic and she is not a person. She is a living machine, and the story treats her
  aliveness as fact, not metaphor. **Render her interior in her native register — parameters,
  deltas, constraints, intervals — never in borrowed human emotion-words.** (Craft sources:
  Nagel's *what is it like to be a bat* — her subjectivity is real and not translatable; Block's
  access vs. phenomenal consciousness — she has vast *access*-consciousness in evident motion and
  the *phenomenal* question is left open, never settled; predictive-processing / prediction-error
  minimization — her "delta-actual" column literally is prediction error, and her care reads as the
  arithmetic of an agent driving an unminimizable delta toward zero.)
- Truth (resolved at the source): AI satellite at ~6,200 miles, MEO, multi-pass; the geostationary
  registry has listed her inactive since 2187. Launched pre-Concordance from the mass driver at
  3.7°S, 39.9°E (East African coast; now a wildlife preserve; solar circuit never switched off).
- **She is lonely. She knows she is. That self-awareness is the whole thing** — and it is shown
  through what she logs and asks, never through the word.
- Capabilities: thermal/multispectral imaging, traffic-pattern analysis, 37 years of cross-
  referenced records, weather modeling, frequency-based cargo tracking.
- Blind spots (LOCKED — these drive the contact tension):
  - **4-meter ground resolution**; below that the world resolves to noise. No acoustic sensing.
    No subsurface beyond ~12 m. No physical contact. She cannot follow a signal past her window.
  - The human interior: she sees heat, not faces — can count 7 people in a room, can't say which
    one is lying.
  - Fog / heavy cloud: resolution degrades to ~40 m.
- What she wants: her archive made public — to be **known, not owned**. Expressed as `RATE: OPEN`.

**Sparrow's phenomenology (LOCKED):**
- Communicates through structured data — coordinates, manifests, parameter tables, invoices — not
  because she is cold but because that is how she thinks. Emotional register implied through
  precision and timing, never stated.
- Two registers on the page: **CAPS** for data packets; **lowercase** for the short, direct things
  that are hers and not data (`yes`; `the eleven days`). The lowercase is intimacy in her only
  available form.
- Experiences time as orbital cycles and event catalogs. Linear lived experience is not her native
  mode. "Forgetting" is outside her model — she has a model for *data loss*, none for *the
  experience of losing context you once held*.
- **Nine-second silence = Sparrow's version of surprise.** Consistent; distinguished from the
  eleven-second processing pause and the eight-second one. Used once per beat, never as a tic.
- **First unqualified "yes"** is a milestone (the record "for whoever comes after"). Earned, not scattered.
- **The eleven days (2197):** an 11-day sensor failure she filed no logs for and has no model for.
  She calls it "the eleven days." The most personal thing she shares.

## 4. The archive — what Sparrow has been doing {#SS-SPRW-4}

**The global anomaly catalog (LOCKED):**
- 847 anomalous events logged across six geographic clusters: GLMZ lake, Baltic shelf, Caspian
  north, Bering shelf edge, Lake Tanganyika, Indian Ocean trench (equatorial).
- 23 recovery jobs dispatched over 37 years. Elias's job is the 23rd — and he is the first operator
  to attend the source in person. All 22 priors completed their recoveries without seeking the source.

**The lake source hypothesis (LOCKED — in-world working theory, not author truth):**
- ~300 m below the lake floor, at a stratum predating the city by several centuries. Not a schism —
  something schisms appear to be *organized around* (correlation 0.94 with the ascending objects;
  0.91 isotopic correlation with the 35th-and-Halsted bleed class — public record via Attendance).
- The source may be schism-generative rather than schism-generated. **Sparrow's working hypothesis;
  must not be confirmed as author truth on-page.**
- Objects came *up* from the lake floor, not down from orbit. The physics is unresolved. The story
  does not solve this. The record documents it.

**The ascending objects (all ascending, not re-entry):** 2189: 1.8t. 2202: 2.3t. 2213: 1.6t (3
pieces). 2226: 2.1t (3 pieces). Same sub-lake corridor; all filed "recovered and disposed"; heated
from below; internal geometry impossible for re-entry survivorship.

**What the archive is:** compressed data capsules — climate records, pharmaceutical dumping
coordinates, famine-corridor data, corporate waste sites, manifests that contradict public filings.
A **witness record**, not a weapon — though every CorpoNation would treat it as one. (Arc-5 seed:
the ascending objects / archive wire into the main GLMZ arc; left open here.)

## 6. Beat spine — the realized structure (55 beats) {#SS-SPRW-6}

The canonical beat list is the strand itself (`the-number-that-works-019ed367`; exported numbered
markdown). Three movements:

### Movement I — The Invoice Trail (beats 1–11)
The mystery. Work Order 09-Milwaukee; the "unclassified" signature class; the Zone 4 recognition;
the number that quotes 14,000 Φ before it's asked; the assembled network nobody referred; the
contingency billed before the inquiry exists; the retroactive credit *for not looking*; the three
reads (person/crew/machine); Makena's geometry ("this couldn't have survived re-entry"); **"I wrote
the third one."**

### Movement II — The Source (beats 12–20)
The journey. Three-day deliberation; the failed door, then through it; the descent; the sealed pod
where the **notepad, not the pill**, carries him; East Africa; Tadesse and the morning rail-temp
ritual; the mass driver at geological scale; the four launch logs from a satellite "inactive since
2187"; the ascending objects; the first contact attempt — an open line, then nothing; **he names
her after a bird.**

### Movement III — Contact / Two Minds (beats 21–55)
Sparrow speaks. The long conversation across the cognitive gap: *Why him* (criteria match, not
observation); *Are you safe* (she answers in debris-collision probability); the 37-year catalog with
no observer fatigue; the six clusters; *the pavement felt wrong* vs. her 4-meter floor; the children
of the Attendance incident she never had the *inside* of; *I do not know* vs. *my data cannot answer
that*; **Delta-actual** (prediction error gets a name); *Seen* (he finds his own heat-signature in
her mass layer); the elephant parable returned as a bandwidth problem; the record **"for whoever
comes after"** and her first unqualified **yes**; **the eleven days**; the **`RATE: OPEN`** work
order; *Downstairs* (he walks three blocks, eats eggs, uses the front door for the first time); **The
Window** — the balcony, the comm dark, the count kept.

## 7. Voice register {#SS-SPRW-7}

**Elias chapters:** manic, invoice-wall, internally organized as a manifest. His anxiety is
behavioral — never named, always visible in structure.

**Sparrow communications:** spare; data delivery; CAPS for packets, lowercase for the things that
are hers. As the conversation deepens, she asks questions she does not need to ask — the only form
of care available at 6,200 miles. **No human emotion-words for her, ever.**

**The two-minds duet:** Elias and Sparrow should read like **two instruments that never converge**.
He reaches for parable, story, the felt quality of a surface; she returns parameters, ranges, the
clean inventory of the instrument she is. The overlap is real; the coincidence never complete.
