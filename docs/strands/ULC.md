# STRAND BIBLE — Underlying Connection (ULC) {#SS-ULC}

> **Purpose.** The arc and rules of the Amara + Seto investigation: a neuretic bleed that
> reveals what neither protagonist can see alone, and a CorpoNation that has been erasing
> the evidence. See [[../BIBLE]] for universe laws. Codeword **DUST** = quality campaign.

## 0. What this story IS {#SS-ULC-0}

A dual-POV GLMZ novel (~80k words, 3 acts, ~28 chapters). Two strangers — Amara Osei
(neuretic tech, inside Orison) and Seto Banda (data courier, Gray Zones) — whose neuretics
harmonize by accident, trading fragments of each other's lives until the combined signal
reveals what neither could see alone:

**Orison Neuretics contaminated Batch 44-C (847 clients), running a suppression
sub-protocol during calibration visits that erases degradation events rather than fixing
them. Visible only as absence — a constellation-shaped black spot in the gray-zone route map.**

**This is NOT:**
- A Kyle story. Kyle does not appear.
- A thriller with a gunfight climax. The antagonist's weapon is administrative erasure.
- A romance (the bleed is involuntary neuretic resonance — not love, though it coexists with
  developing trust).

## 1. The bleed {#SS-ULC-1}

**Neuretic harmonic resonance.** Identity contamination: Amara receives Seto's cognitive
states, emotions, proprioceptive memories. Physical and specific — not literary. The phantom
weight in exact tendons, not "emotional heaviness."

**Seto's neuretics are carrier-grade, sealed, zero-leakage.** The bleed should be
impossible. That impossibility is the first evidence that something is wrong with the batch.

**The bleed must be PHYSICAL and SPECIFIC in prose.** It lands in the body before the mind.

**What the bleed is NOT:** A metaphor. A bond. A love signal. It is a malfunction that
happens to reveal the truth.

## 2. The conspiracy {#SS-ULC-2}

**Orison Neuretics** — premium neuretic cultivation; CorpoNation.
**Batch 44-C** — 847 recipients. Carrier-grade devices. A sub-protocol runs during each
calibration visit: it flags degradation events as "nominal variance" and routes them to
suppression rather than correction. The client's device records "maintenance complete."
The error propagates.

**Visible only as absence:** the black spot. Seto's route map has a gap — the gray-zone
corridor through the Pilsen Veil where no courier goes twice. The clients there don't
report problems. They stop reporting anything.

**Ciro Fonseca** — Orison internal fixer / Account Liaison. Portuguese-Brazilian. Straight
razor with a bone handle. He observes, threatens, but does not land a blow until Act 3.
**CRITICAL WEAKNESS (current editorial gap):** Act 2 Ciro watches without costing either
protagonist anything concrete. Reviewers consistently flag: "consequences abstract." Each
Act 2 chapter needs one hard, irreversible Ciro action per chapter — access revoked, route
terminated, a contact disappears, Nuru gets a visit.

**Nuru Banda** — Seto's sister. Zone 6, Facility C-9. Nutritionist at a community clinic.
In the Batch 44-C manifest at row 19 (Zone 6 block). Seto has known for 8 days before Ch11.
She is the personal cost.

## 3. World rules for this story {#SS-ULC-3}

**Seams = gray zones** between CorpoNation jurisdictions. Real air, honest commerce, relay
beacons nobody updates. Seto's world.

**CorpoNations = arcologies.** No visible clocks, no exit signs, curated news about horrors
outside, managed air you feel in your sinuses. Amara's world.

**The bleed is medical, not metaphysical.** Any prose that makes it sound mystical or
romantic is wrong. Keep it in the body. Keep it specific.

## 4. Character rules {#SS-ULC-4}

**Amara Osei (POV — Amara chapters):**
- Cellvault maintenance tech, Batch 44-C recipient, Lagos-Chicago. Entity `019ee11c`.
- Inside Orison — the one who has access to the calibration records.
- Her POV reads the CorpoNation's management of information from inside.
- Gender: she/her. Never use "they" for Amara.

**Seto Banda (POV — Seto chapters):**
- Gray Zone data courier, sealed carrier-grade neuretics, Japanese-Kenyan. Entity `019ee11c`.
- Gender: **he/him throughout**. No pronoun errors.
- His POV reads the absence — the missing couriers, the black spot on the route map.
- Outside Orison — sees the shape of the conspiracy from the seams.

**Ciro Fonseca:**
- Orison internal fixer. Straight razor, bone handle.
- His weapon is administrative. He watches; he threatens; he closes routes.
- Must land at least one irreversible blow per Act 2 chapter to drive score above 79.

## 5. Narrative locks {#SS-ULC-5}

1. **The bleed is physical and specific.** Phantom weight in exact tendons. Not "emotional
   heaviness." Never literary.
2. **The black spot is visible only as absence.** No dramatic discovery scene — Seto
   notices what's NOT there on his route map. The absence is the clue.
3. **Seto = he/him throughout.** No pronoun errors.
4. **Act 2 Ciro must land blows.** Observers without consequence cap scores at ~79.
   Each chapter: one irreversible antagonist action.
5. **The bleed should be impossible for carrier-grade neuretics.** That impossibility
   is the first proof something is wrong.

## 7. Structural diagnosis (why 85% requires overhaul) {#SS-ULC-7}

2. **"Consequences abstract"** — Ciro watches/threatens but never costs the protagonist
   something tangible.
3. **"Cyberpunk underdressed"** — mood without tactile images.

**What score peaks correspond to:** Ch15 +5.6 (Seto meeting added — actual plot event).

score; texture-only additions flatline or hurt.

antagonist action — Ciro does something that costs Amara/Seto something concrete.

## 8. What works / what kills scores {#SS-ULC-8}

**What works:**
- AntagonistCost that BLOCKS an action (proxy alert stops the query; compliance counter
  blocks the cancellation; broker infrastructure vanishes).
- Physical involuntary details > literary description (jaw tightens, hand flat on terminal,
  left shoulder forward).
- The bleed must be PHYSICAL and SPECIFIC (phantom weight in exact tendons).
- Quiet cyberpunk chapters max at ~81.5; action-adjacent confrontations can reach 82+.

**What kills scores:**
- "Not its physical weight, which was negligible, but its informational weight" — recycled
  affectation.
- "She thought about X. She thought about Y" — mechanical enumeration.
- Over-explaining the bleed mechanics.
- Seto pronoun errors (he/him throughout).
- Adding description without plot events.
