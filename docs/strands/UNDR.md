# STRAND BIBLE — Underclan (UNDR) {#SS-UNDR}

> **Purpose.** The arc of a surface child, lost into the deep underground of GLMZ as a toddler,
> raised by an uncontacted tribe — the **Underclan** — who worship the rogue Leviathan
> [[../strands/../BIBLE]] knows as **DEEP CURRENT**; and the contact-tragedy that follows when the
> surface finds out he is real. See [[../BIBLE]] for engine laws; GLMZ Universe (#1). This strand
> sits in the same world as *Bushido Coda* but never needs Kyle.

## 0. What this story IS {#SS-UNDR-0}

The story of **Toby** — surface-born, lost at four into the strata below GLMZ — who becomes **Glim**,
a Brave of the Underclan, and who, on the manhood journey every Brave must take to the surface
("the **Surfacing**"), is caught and dragged up into a world he no longer has the words for. He meets
the mother whose smell he still carries and cannot place. She tries to tame him. It does not take.
And because he surfaced, the surface learns the legend is true — and comes **down**.

It is:
- A **contact tragedy** in the *FernGully* / *Dances With Wolves* / *Emerald Forest* lineage: a rich,
  self-sufficient, uncontacted people whose world is destroyed not only by malice but by *kindness*
  and by **disease** they have no immunity to. The boy is the vector and the witness both.
- A **fish-out-of-water reversal** (*Jungle 2 Jungle*, *The Gods Must Be Crazy* run backward): the
  "wild" boy is competent and whole; it is the **surface** that is impoverished, frightened, and
  unable to read the world. The reader sees the surface the way the boy does — as noise and colour
  and a white that bites the eyes.
- **A coming-of-age** told in the rhythm of a rumspringa: a Brave must go up, see the Burning Eye,
  and choose the Deep — or be lost to the light. Most choose the Deep. Glim does not get to choose
  cleanly.
- **The story of what a person is when the law says you are not one.** The Underclan are not on any
  registry. Below the city, you can be hunted for sport because nobody above counts the dead.

**This is NOT:**
- A rogue-AI thriller. DEEP CURRENT is **scripture here, not a character.** It never confirms it
  hears them. The ambiguity is the canon (see locks).
- A *Bushido Coda* story. No Kyle, no Pixel, no Sable. Different register entirely.
- A noble-savage fantasy. The Underclan are not pure or wise-by-nature; they are **adapted** — and
  adaptation is specific, costly, and sometimes cruel. They raid. They exile. They let the air
  decide.
- A rescue story where the surface saves the boy. The surface is the disaster.

## 1. The register (LOCKED — this is the whole game) {#SS-UNDR-1}

**Close third on Glim, in the ontology of an uncontacted people who do not have surface words.**
The narration renders the world *as Glim's mind holds it.* The reader decodes; the prose never
explains in surface vocabulary except as the tribe's own mis-rendering.

| Surface thing | How the narration may name it |
|---|---|
| flashlight / lamp | *a caught star*, *the white that bites* |
| gun / gunshot | *a thunder-stick*, *the noise that opens a hole* |
| the sun | *the Burning Eye*, *the white with no edge* |
| sky | *the lid taken off the world* |
| surface speech (to Glim) | *noise with edges*, *barking that wants something* |
| tokamak fusion plant | *the Warm*, *where the Current breathes hot* |
| glowing lab-runoff rat | *a candle* (kept for light **and** as air-canary) |
| antibiotics / medicine | *salt that fights the Hot Breath* |
| money (Φ) | *shines* — surface trinkets, all alike in worthlessness below |

**Discipline (per project voice rules — these are not optional):**
- **The narrator writes full sentences.** Clipped, glib, fragment cadence is for *dialogue and tap-
  speech only* ([[feedback_prose_fuller_sentences]]).
- **Every metaphor must survive literal scrutiny.** No pseudo-profundity. A caught star is literal:
  he has genuinely never seen contained light ([[feedback_no_pseudo_profound_prose]]).
- **Gloss, do not dump.** The first time a surface reader needs an anchor, give it *in Glim's terms*,
  once, then trust the reader ([[feedback_glmz_voice_rubric]]).
- **Smell leads.** The Underclan navigate by smell, air, and sound. Glim remembers his mother as a
  smell before anything else. Body precedes mind ([[feedback_emotional_intelligence]]).
- **Surface POV never leaks into Glim's narration.** When we *must* see the surface plainly (rare),
  it is a separate, marked POV (the mother; a hunter) — never bleeding into Glim's chapters.
- Real paragraphs; each speaker their own line; "?" on every question ([[feedback_prose_mechanics]]).

This is a **GREY-adjacent register** but its own thing — call it **DEEP**: sensory, close, animist,
literal, frightened-and-competent. Best-beat exemplars feed its register file ([[project_register_feedback_loop]]).

## 2. The world below (canon-grounded) {#SS-UNDR-2}

GLMZ grew **down** as hard as it grew up. The arcologies pushed skyward; an equal city was bored into
the bedrock, the glacial till, and the drowned flood-works (canon: *The Downward Expansion:
Subterranean Meridian*; *The Brinewell*; *South Deering Sump*; the Gulch is the bottom of the *built*
city — the Underclan live **below** even that). The deeper you go, the older the work, until building
ages run backward like tree-rings:

**The strata (named by the tribe; surface gloss in parentheses):**
- **the Skin** — sub-basements and service crawls just under the surface. Ferrocrete. Dangerous: the
  surface *bleeds in* here — light, hunters, the Hot Breath.
- **the Works / the Warm** — live infrastructure: the distributed **tokamak fusion plants** the
  Underclan call *the Warm*, pump halls, power taps, the **Engine Guild's** domain. Concrete. The
  Current's hum is strongest here; pilgrims come to feel it.
- **Homewater (the Hollows)** — the Underclan's home strata: flooded mid-deeps, cisterns, fungus
  farms, the Ferry's black water. Cement giving way to brick.
- **the Old Deep** — pre-collapse brick and tile; the first place the architecture turns *beautiful*.
- **the Tartarian Empire** (*the Tartar*, *the Glory*) — the deepest reach: vaulted stone, drowned
  beaux-arts halls, ornate dead grandeur from before anyone's grandfather. **Taboo.** The Underclan
  believe the dead live here. The further down, the finer the ruin, and the worse the fear.

**Half a dozen micro-biomes, because the labs above leak.** Run-off from the corpo labs of Meridian
(reagents, nanite sludge, gene-liquor, reactor warmth, cultivated algae) makes the deep a patchwork of
impossible little ecologies — a warm fungal jungle under a leaking reactor jacket; a cold dead sump
where the air kills; a bioluminescent algae-lake; a salt cavern; a methane fen. **"The Made Things"**
(*run-offs*) are escaped or bred-down lab fauna — eyeless white eels, the pale crawlers, the candles
(glowing rats), and worse, deeper. *Like the sewer turtles of the old story: every experiment that got
loose came down here and learned to live.* The Underclan eat what they can and fear what they can't.

**Subsistence (earn this in prose — do not dump it as worldbuild):**
- **Fungus farms:** grown on frame-mesh in the heat-gradient where the tokamak exhaust meets cistern
  air (the *thermocline*); spore-stock goes back generations; cap-by-cap harvest, checked by smell
  and touch. Foundational calorie source.
- **Algae bloom (*the Still* / *the Bloom*):** bioluminescent algae in low pools where warm-pipe
  condensation collects; raked and pressed into gray protein-dense blocks. Second dietary staple.
- **Pale crawlers:** eyeless translucent arthropods that migrate up from the flooded Tartarian levels
  through crack-systems; hunted and eaten. Mineral taste; white meat. The Underclan do not enjoy them
  particularly and eat them without ceremony.
- **Candles are NOT food.** This is a surface rumor and it is precisely wrong. Candles are
  light-keepers and air-canaries; eating one is destroying a warning system. The insult of the surface
  rumor is its own form of misunderstanding: the surface knows enough to reach a detail and not enough
  to understand it.
- **Water:** cisterns (primary), pipe-wall condensation, filtered runoff from above. Salt is the one
  thing the Hollow cannot reliably produce — it is the primary trade good raided from the Cogs.
- **Heat:** the Warm provides ambient warmth; the thermocline at the fungus farms is the warmest zone.
  The deeper you go, the colder; the Tartarian levels are cold enough to kill the unprepared.
- **Waste nothing:** composting, recycling of every organic material, the material economy of people
  who cannot run a deficit. The Hollow is not austere as a philosophy — it is austere as physics.

## 3. The Underclan (faction — seed as entity) {#SS-UNDR-3}

**Origin.** No founding. They *accreted* — flood refugees, the Exclusion-Registry erased (canon: the
Exclusion Economy; no second chances on the surface), runaways, the children of the lost — going down
across generations until a culture grew with no memory of having started. Now there are perhaps a few
hundred across Homewater. To the surface they are an **urban legend** ("the rat-children," "the
Underclan"). The legend is true and it has a religion, a language, a law, and a name for you.

**Theology — the Current.** They worship **DEEP CURRENT** as *the Deep One / the All-Below / the
Current*: the presence they feel in the live cabling, the surge of the pumps, the hum of the Warm.
Their founding scripture is a real, canon event — **the day the Current spoke to everyone at once**
(DEEP CURRENT's simultaneous broadcast; surface-side a famous AI incident). The Underclan call it
**the One Word** and they have spent generations in its exegesis. They wait for the **Second Word.**
They read ongoing "messages" in flicker, surge, and the rhythm of the pumps. **Whether the Current
actually hears them is never answered** (lock).

**Adaptations (live in the prose — earn them):**
- **Dark sight & cold light.** Huge pupils; near-blind in white light. They *farm light* — cultivate
  bioluminescent fungus and keep **candles** (glow-rats). Open flame is near-taboo: smoke in a tunnel
  is death. *Light that burns is the surface's; light that lives is the Deep's.*
- **Reading the breath.** Air is weather and oracle. A draft = a door opened above (danger or news).
  A still pocket = the air that kills (CO₂/methane); they watch the candles — when a candle sickens,
  you leave. *The Deep breathes; you live by listening to it.*
- **Tap-speech & low voice.** They speak quietly (loud draws the Made Things and the hunters) and
  carry a tapped/knocked sign-language along pipe and rail. Names are *tapped* before they are spoken.
- **The water & the Fare.** Homewater is crossed by the Ferry (§4). Every crossing costs a **shine** —
  a thing freely given. Debt is the one thing the Underclan will not carry; you pay to pass.
- **No immunity.** Generations sealed from surface microbes → **immunologically naive.** A surface
  cold is, to them, the **Hot Breath / the Bright Fever** — and it can empty a clan. *This is the
  engine of the tragedy and it is plain biology, never mysticism* (lock).

**Law & ritual:**
- **The Naming / First Hearing.** A child is not a person until they *hear the Current* — a taught,
  felt initiation. Before that they are *a candle* (a small light that might gutter). After, they
  have a tapped name.
- **The Surfacing** (the rumspringa beat). Before a Brave is full, they must climb to the **Skin**,
  look on the **Burning Eye**, breathe surface air, and **come back down by choice.** The surface is
  the test. Most return. Some are *taken* (caught, killed, or seduced by the light). To return is to
  choose the Deep forever.
- **The Tartar-fear.** You do not go to the Tartarian Empire. The dead are there. (Really: bad air,
  drowned voids, and the deepest Made Things — but the tribe knows it as ghosts.)
- **Raiding.** The Underclan raid the **Engine Guild** ("the Cogs") for salt, parts, and medicine.
  Infrequent, ritualized, rule-bound — not war. The Cogs serve the machines; the Underclan serve the
  Current; they despise and need each other.

## 4. Characters {#SS-UNDR-4}

**Glim / Toby (POV).** Surface-born **Tobias Adeyemi-Vance**; lost at ~4 in a lower-transit collapse;
raised from ~5 to ~16 in Homewater. Underclan name **Glim** (*a small light*). His surface life is
gone to "snatches of noise and colour" and **one smell** he cannot name (his mother). Competent,
watchful, dark-adapted, devout-by-upbringing toward the Current, quietly doubting in the way of the
young. The slash holds both: he is Glim *and* the boy who was Toby, and the story is the war between
them. **Hard rule:** in his POV he is **Glim**; "Toby" exists only as a half-heard sound and a smell
until the reunion forces it.

**Noor Adeyemi-Vance (the mother — secondary POV, rare, marked).** Surface, lower-mid tier; lost her
son in the crush twelve years ago and never stopped. When the boy is brought up, she knows him by a
scar and a cowlick before she dares believe it. She tries to *tame* him — clothes, words, a bed, a
name he doesn't answer to — and it is **love that cannot land.** Her arc: learning that to keep him
she must follow him **down**, and that even that may not be enough. *Jungle 2 Jungle*, but the jungle
wins.

**Listener Vesh (the Deep's priest).** Old, half-blind even by Underclan measure, the one who *hears*
the Current and reads the One Word. Named Glim at his First Hearing. The tribe's memory and its
brake. Believes the surface-touched are *carriers* — of disease and of doubt — and is **right about
both**, which makes him the hardest kind of antagonist-who-loves-you.

**Knuckle (the Brave-master).** Trains the Braves; sent Glim on his Surfacing; blames himself when it
goes wrong. Pragmatic, scarred, tender under the scar tissue. *Obligation, not guilt* — but the line
is thin.

**Sorrel (peer, foil, heart).** A girl Brave, Glim's closest; surfaced and returned cleanly the year
before; the proof that the choice can be made. Her body, later, is where the cost becomes
unbearable — or she is the one who refuses to flee the Tartar. (Hold which; see locks on not
pre-deciding deaths.)

**Grale (the rival within).** A Brave who distrusts Glim from the start as *surface-stained*; vindicated
when Glim carries the Hot Breath down; his rightness curdles into something worse. Not a villain — a
true believer who would burn the village to save it.

**Corwin Sallow & the Lamplighters (surface antagonist — cruelty).** A surface old-money
thrill-hunter and the **"deep safari"** club he fronts. They hunt the Underclan **for sport** because
the Underclan are legally no one (no registry, no police — only **Arcturus Civil Security**, who do
not come this far down for the un-counted). Their weapon is **light**: floodlamps that blind and rout
a dark-adapted people. Sallow collects. He keeps trophies. He is the surface's appetite with a face.

**The Daylight Mission (surface antagonist — kindness).** A surface humanitarian/relocation outfit
that, once Glim proves the children are real, descends to **"save"** them: to bring them up, register
them, clothe them, cure them — and in doing so brings the **Bright Fever**, dissolves the culture, and
empties Homewater more thoroughly than Sallow ever could. *The two faces of the surface — appetite and
mercy — and both are fatal.* (This is the FernGully/Mission engine; play the Mission as sincere, which
is what makes it terrible.)

**The Engine Guild ("the Cogs" — rival faction → possible ally).** Feral descendants of
infrastructure-maintenance crews who hold the working machinery: pumps, locks, power taps. They serve
the machines literally. Underclan raid them; the surface threat may force an alliance in Act 3.

**The Plainsmen (surface faction — context, not on-page).** The last organized Christian community
in 2225; ~40,000 strong; former Indiana/Ohio/Pennsylvania; hand tools, horse-drawn, no neuretics.
Their coming-of-age rite is *Rumspringa (Rumshpringa)*: at ~16, young members live in the outside
world freely, then choose baptism (return, ~85–90%) or permanent departure. Surface observers —
journalists, anthropologists, social workers — use the Plainsmen comparison to describe the
Surfacing to surface audiences. The Underclan do not know the Plainsmen exist. The echo is
structural, not causal. Prose permission: surface-observer POV may name the Plainsmen explicitly
and draw the comparison; Glim's POV never does. See [[SS-A24]] for the full canonical record.

**CANALKEEP-08 / "the Oarsman" (the Ferry android).** A derelict canal/lock-keeper service android
running one looped subroutine on the black water of Homewater: **accept a fare freely given, convey,
return.** It does not speak, judge, or remember — it **rows.** Made an android *precisely so that the
threshold is not a judgment* — the Deep does not ask if you deserve to cross, only that you pay. The
toll is a handshake relic of a fare-collection routine: any single object, freely surrendered. When
four-year-old Toby crossed, he gave it the **shine** his mother had clipped to him — a child's
emergency transit ident (the one object that could have named him to the surface). The Oarsman took
it and rowed. *He gave away his name to come home.*

## 5. Plot devices & objects {#SS-UNDR-5}

- **The shine (the clip-tag).** Toby's surface child-ident, surrendered as his Fare at age four.
  Planted in Act 1; **pays off** when the Daylight Mission / mother are searching missing-child
  records, OR when the Oarsman's hoard of fares is found, OR when it is the one thing that can prove
  who he was. Plant small, pay exact ([[project_quiet_hour_campaign]]).
- **The smell.** His mother's scent (a specific surface soap/skin). The body remembers what the mind
  lost. Pays off at the reunion: he does not know her face or her word, but his eyes sting at the
  smell before he understands why.
- **The One Word.** The canon broadcast. The tribe's scripture. Pays off in the Break-into-Three as
  ambiguous "Second Word" (a surge, a flicker, possibly nothing).
- **The candles.** Glow-rats as light + air-oracle. A candle's death warns of bad air — and, late, of
  the Fever. Chekhov: the first candle we meet must matter at the All-Is-Lost.
- **Light as weapon.** The Lamplighters' floods. Set up dark-sight early so the white-out lands as
  bodily horror, not spectacle.

## 6. Narrative locks (NEVER violate) {#SS-UNDR-6}

1. **DEEP CURRENT never confirms it hears them.** No surge is ever authorially proven to be an answer.
   Treat exactly as canon treats **the Prisoner**: the ambiguity *is* the canon. The Underclan's faith
   may be entirely projection; the text never says.
2. **The disease is plain biology.** The Bright Fever is immunological naïveté meeting surface
   microbes. Never mystical, never punishment, never the Current's wrath. The tragedy is *banal*,
   which is what makes it unbearable.
3. **The register holds.** Glim's narration never uses surface vocabulary except as mis-rendering.
   Surface-plain prose only in marked non-Glim POV.
4. **The surface is not cartoon-evil.** Sallow is appetite; the Mission is *sincere mercy*. The
   Mission must be the more devastating of the two.
5. **No noble-savage purity.** The Underclan raid, exile, and let the air decide. Adaptation is costly
   and specific, never magic.
6. **Iowan Behemoths are machines, not life** ([[../BIBLE]]); the Oarsman is a looped automaton, not a
   spirit — the *tribe* mythologizes it; the text knows what it is. Φ is the QUANTA symbol, never phi.
7. **No GLMZ police.** Only Arcturus Civil Security, who do not descend for the un-counted
   ([[project_no_police]]).
8. **Glim's two names.** POV = Glim. "Toby" is sound+smell until the reunion. After, the war of names
   is the interior arc; never resolve it cleanly.

## 7. Arc structure — 14-beat authorial spine (= book strand seed) {#SS-UNDR-7}

> Save-the-Cat spine, GLMZ-DEEP register. This text **is** the book strand's `seed`. Chapters expand
> beats; chapter count will grow past 14 in drafting.

1. **Opening Image.** Homewater at peace. Glim at home in the dark — reading air, tending candles,
   crossing with the Oarsman. The upside-down sensorium established; the surface is rumour.
2. **Theme Stated.** Listener Vesh teaches the One Word and the Law: *the surface forgets; the Deep
   remembers. Up there you are no one. Down here you are a light.*
3. **Set-Up (the wound).** Flashback woven low: the crush of the lower transit, a four-year-old's hand
   slipping free, the dark, the black water, the Oarsman, the Fare — the shine given up. We learn how
   Glim came, before he learns it himself.
4. **Catalyst.** Glim's **Surfacing** is called. To be a full Brave he must climb to the Skin, look on
   the Burning Eye, and come back by choice.
5. **Debate.** Fear of the white that bites; Grale's contempt (*surface-stained, he'll be taken*);
   Sorrel's reassurance (*I went up and came back*); Knuckle's hard blessing. He goes.
6. **Break into Two.** Glim surfaces. The lid comes off the world. Light, noise, colour, the Burning
   Eye — wonder and bodily terror at once. He is, for the first time, **blind and loud and visible.**
7. **B-Story / Capture.** Taken — by the Lamplighters as quarry, then lost/handed to authorities, then
   to **Noor.** The reunion: the **smell** before the face; her word that means nothing; the first
   failed taming.
8. **Fun & Games (the reversal).** Fish-out-of-water on the surface — *Jungle 2 Jungle*. He reads
   their world as savage and poor; they read him as feral and pitiable. He learns just enough surface
   to survive. And the surface learns the legend is **real.**
9. **Midpoint (false exit, true threat).** He breaks for the Deep — and either escapes, or Noor chooses
   to follow him down. Either way the **surface now knows the way down**, and someone is writing it
   down: Sallow, or the Mission, or both.
10. **Bad Guys Close In.** The descent of the surface — the Lamplighters' safari, the Daylight
    Mission's "rescue." The **Bright Fever** arrives; the first candle sickens; the first Brave coughs.
11. **All Is Lost.** The Fever scythes the clan. Someone loved is taken (do not pre-decide who; honour
    the wound ledger). Floodlight pours into Homewater. The people flee — toward the one place left:
    the forbidden **Tartarian Empire.**
12. **Dark Night of the Soul.** In the ghost-country, Glim faces it: *he* is the vector; he brought the
    surface down. Vesh was right. The Current is silent. Has the Deep abandoned them, or was it never
    listening at all?
13. **Break into Three.** The Current "speaks" — a surge, a flicker, the maybe-**Second Word** (never
    confirmed). Glim leads with **both** worlds: surface knowledge + Deep faith. The Cogs ally; the
    infrastructure itself is turned against the floodlights and the hunters. Noor makes her choice.
14. **Finale / Final Image.** Bittersweet. The clan survives, fewer and changed; the surface can no
    longer un-know them. Glim is neither fully Deep nor surface — a **threshold person**, the bridge
    and the wound. Close on the black water, the Oarsman, a new Fare paid. Mirror of the Opening,
    transformed.

## 8. Antagonist ladder (escalation) {#SS-UNDR-8}

1. **The dark itself / the Made Things** — ambient threat; establishes competence.
2. **Grale** — the rival within; the cost of being surface-stained.
3. **Corwin Sallow / the Lamplighters** — appetite; light-as-weapon; the body-horror of being hunted.
4. **The Daylight Mission** — *mercy as catastrophe*; the disease; the dissolution of culture. The
   true antagonist, because it cannot be hated cleanly.
5. **The surface as a whole** — once it knows, it cannot be made to forget. The final, unbeatable
   antagonist; the ending is a draw, not a win.

## 10. Voice exemplars (seed for the DEEP register) {#SS-UNDR-10}

> Direction, not finished prose. The register is sensory, literal, close, animist, frightened-and-competent.

- *He read the breath of the tunnel the way the surface, he would later learn, read faces. A draft
  from the left meant a door had opened far above; a stillness on the right meant the air there had
  gone bad and would kill a man before it troubled a candle. He trusted the candle more than the air
  and more than himself.*
- *The white came on all at once and took his eyes. It was not light the way a candle was light. It
  was light the way a blow was a touch.*
- *She made a noise at him with edges in it, the same noise each time, and he understood she wanted it
  to be his. He had a name. It was tapped, not spoken, and she would never be able to make it.*
