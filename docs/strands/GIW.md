# STRAND BIBLE — Grafted Into War (GIW) {#SS-GIW}

> **Purpose.** The arc of M-101/Soren becoming a Champion: the alchemist (Dame Lyra), the prima
> materia (Soren), the Magnum Opus (each Transmutation). See [[../BIBLE]] for engine laws;
> [[../BIBLE#SS-LAW-15]] for Universe isolation. Fantasy Universe (Universe #2).

## 0. What this story IS {#SS-GIW-0}

The story of Soren becoming a Champion. Across all iterations: ordinary soldier (M-009) →
anomalous spark (Dame Lyra notices) → cultivated iteration by iteration → growing toward Champion.

It is:
- An alchemical frame: Lyra is the alchemist, Soren the prima materia, each Transmutation
  one stage of the Magnum Opus toward the Philosopher's Stone (the Champion).
- A long arc across 60+ iterations, told at two points: the first grafting (Arc One, M-009)
  and the hundred-and-first (Arc Two, M-101), with the Ashgrave Anomaly as Arc Three.
- **The story of what it costs to accumulate self without continuity** — deaths blurring
  together until the distant ones lose resolution like looking through water.

**This is NOT:**
- A redemption story. Soren has not done wrong; he has been deployed.
- A chosen-one prophecy story. Lyra chose him at a restraint table, early in the war, for
  reasons she has seven theories about and hasn't confirmed. No prophecy. No destiny.
- A love story between Soren and Lyra (not explicitly).
- A Kyle story. Different universe. Different laws.

## 1. The naming convention (LOCKED) {#SS-GIW-1}

**Designation / Name. Not interchangeable. The slash holds both simultaneously.**

- Soren — the man, unTransmuted. No designation.
- M-009/Soren — first iteration. First designation. Same name.
- M-101/Soren — hundred-and-first iteration. Same name. Designation climbs. Name is the constant.
- [Champion name] — eventually the designation fades. A name emerges outside the numbering
  system. That's how you know someone became a Champion: they stopped being assignable.

**Maewen the Threader has no slash.** Champions are named, not designated.

**Hard ban:** "Jon Murphy" — the character's name is Soren from the beginning. Always.

## 2. The alchemy frame {#SS-GIW-2}

- **Dame Lyra** = the alchemist. Has been calling this spark back since at least iteration 41.
- **Soren / M-101** = the Prima Materia (the base substance worth refining).
- **The Champion** = the Philosopher's Stone (perfected, indestructible, refined self).
- **Each Transmutation** = one stage of the Magnum Opus.

The Transmutation isn't just the procedure. It IS the alchemical refinement. What he was in
iteration 1 and what he is in iteration 101 are not the same thing. The self is accumulating
toward something.

**Why Lyra keeps choosing him:**
Something in the Loose State notices M-101 specifically. Four recorded instances of awareness
in the dark between iterations. She doesn't know what it means. Neither does he. That's why
she keeps calling him back. The answer is worth more than whatever the war costs to get there.

**The arc endpoint:**
He will choose a vessel that isn't human-shaped. He will stop fitting in ordinary flesh. He
will become one of the old things — a Champion. Whether he considers this a victory is the
open question of the series.

## 3. Memory doctrine (LOCKED) {#SS-GIW-3}

- **M-101 does NOT have amnesia.** Memory accumulates and BLURS. Deaths blur together.
  The distant ones (near Soren) lose resolution like looking through water. Recent ones are
  clearer but bleed into each other.
- **The smell of yggdra smoke** is his most stable carried image. Present across all iterations.
- He knows about Soren from documentation, not clean memory. He cannot confirm what is his
  vs constructed.
- **He does NOT ask existential questions.** He has accepted what he is. The doing is a
  condition, not an action.

## 4. Character rules {#SS-GIW-4}

**M-101/Soren (POV — Arc Two):**
- Long view, not detached — small things hit harder because the big things don't.
- Death is not dramatic. Specific moments are.
- Cost shown, never stated: "the cost is the left sensor array" not "it was painful."
- Dame Lyra is the anchor. Not romantic necessarily — the one constant across a hundred deaths.
- The orientation wiggle (chassis awakening tell) is universal. He knows it from the inside.

**Dame Lyra:**
- The alchemist. Reads him like her own document.
- She has seven theories and hasn't confirmed any.
- Her Interview ritual: established protocol across many iterations. Yggdra smoke. Old Rite
  hands. Questions about what he carried through the Loose State.
- She does not sentimentalize the relationship. He is her most important experiment.
  That doesn't preclude care.

**Sergeant Osei:**
- Executes Ungrafted bandits. "The third army."
- The moment establishes that Houses do not recognize combatants outside the system.

## 5. World mechanics for this universe {#SS-GIW-5}

**Pneumagration** = formal term for consciousness transfer. **Reshelled** = grunt term.
(Title pun: "Drafted" + "Grafted.")

**Scrying** = observe a parallel Sphere's technology, copy the design, build it locally.
Nothing crosses. The engineers of the source Sphere never know. Like pressing a key into a mold.

**QE Link** = neural bond; severing = death; refusal = war criminal.

**The Infusion** = conductive fluid, ~80% washout rate, prerequisite for QE grafting.

**The spark state** = purgatory between grafts. Hold one thing to carry something through.
**The orientation wiggle** = universal awakening tell for every chassis.

**Dame Lyra's standing order** = she has been calling this spark back since at least iteration 41.

**Burnt ozone** = QE link severance smell.

**Yggdra smoke** = M-101's most persistent cross-iteration sensory anchor.

**The Ungrafted** = third army; former soldiers outside Houses; permanent feature of every conflict.

**Lanternkin** = locked souls; Guttering = locked body failing. Three tiers: poor die → Bheur,
wealthy transfer to new shell, useful are Locked. "Don't waste good alive."

**Bheur** = real. Monsters are native. Monster Meat = governing failure metric.

**Anachronism is the mechanism:** Scrying = pull future into present. Transmutation = past
selves in present body. Champions = history refusing to resolve.

## 6. Narrative locks {#SS-GIW-6}

1. **The Prisoner thread.** Four recorded instances of awareness in the Loose State. Beat 18
   is the fourth. Lyra says "it implies something." M-101 says "it implies the Prisoner."
   Neither confirms. The Prisoner is NEVER confirmed in-universe; no House theology is correct.
2. **Scrying data blocks = cold bureaucratic notation interrupting medieval prose.** The
   uncanny made mundane. Never dramatize the data blocks.
3. **M-101 does not ask existential questions.** The doing is a condition, not an action.
4. **Yggdra smoke follows him.** Beat 20 ends with it. It is his through-line the way
   Silence is Kyle's through-line.
5. **The orientation wiggle.** Universal. Every chassis. He knows it from the inside.

## 7. Arc structure {#SS-GIW-7}

### Arc One — The First Grafting (M-009) — 9 beats

Soren → Infusion → M-009/Soren (Turret #33 chassis) → Mossland battle.
Establishes: Infusion procedure, QE Link, turret operations, the orientation wiggle, Dame
Lyra's standing order, the spark state.

1. The Infusion — Murphy/Soren strapped to table, 80% washout rate
2. Training — robotic claw, QE neural control
3. Turret #33 goes live — grafted to underbelly cannon, a mile up
4. #34's moral crisis — "they're people, I can't" from shared channel; he fires anyway
5. #34 dies — command severs QE link, burnt ozone smell
6. #34 reborn — new spark wiggles, opens fire without a word
7. RPG impact / the fall — airship hit, housing tears free, a mile of sky
8. The spark — infinite black sea from inside
9. M-009 awakens — first Myrmidon graft; orders: find Dame Lyra

### Arc Two — The Hundred and First (M-101) — 20 beats

Buehlman voice (Between Two Fires). Set many decades and iterations later.

1. What He Wakes To — orientation wiggle, repair bay, new chassis after Mossland
2. The Briefing — Commander Vassar, Mossland objective, thin-membrane secondary concern
3. The March — infantry column, deserters at tree line, yggdra smoke smell
4. Camp — burned orchard, Old Rite prayer, Soren's documentation
5. Scrying Intelligence — Sphere 47 data block, gas-operated rifle design
6. The Battle Opens — dawn, causeway crossing, first shot
7. The Fight in the Mire — three rounds taken, bridge assault, chassis pragmatism
8. The Deserters — Sergeant Osei executes Ungrafted bandits; the third army
9. The Incursion — bone-powder smell, bird-things come through, both sides stop
10. The Membrane Site — M-101 enters breach, QE closure at cost of sensor array + arm
11. After — House Skarr truce, two reports (official + separate to Dame Lyra)
12. The Order — standing order arrives; he has known it for forty iterations
13. The Road — nine thin-spots in forty miles, Lanternkin Vigil watcher (sixty-year post)
14. Ships Rock — the facility, the conductive fluid smell = Dame Lyra is here
15. Dame Lyra — the reunion, she reads him like her own document, sensor array noted
16. The Interview — their ritual; yggdra smoke, Old Rite hands, rifle sound, bridge
17. What She Knows — sixty-two iterations of self-continuity, anomalous
18. What He Gives Her — sound in the Loose State dark; something large noticing him (4th time)
19. New Orders — Ashgrave Corridor; a sphere that shouldn't exist; parallax mapping anomaly
20. What Remains — dawn departure, yggdra smoke follows; "he keeps moving, that is what he has"

### Arc Three — The Ashgrave Anomaly (pending)

A sphere outside the catalogue sequence. The Ashgrave Range thin-spots multiplying. M-101
sent because of his breach experience. This is where The Prisoner thread develops.
