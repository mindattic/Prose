## 0. What This Story IS {#SS-LSSS-0}

A standalone short story — one complete hunting sequence. Dame Lyra T-044 tracks a harrower
and its kin-pack to a creek ford, kills them all, and returns to garrison with the harrower's
head. No subtext, no arc. A deed. The kind that gets told by firelight afterward.

**Primary function:** Register exemplar for VIGL. Every beat in Vigil's End should feel
like the event that becomes this story — immediate, physical, specific, deadpan at the return.

**What the prose establishes:**
- Lyra's combat register: observational, precise, unsentimental. She notes things (group instinct,
  the reach problem) rather than naming emotions.
- Harrower anatomy and behavior as established canon: membrane-grey, too many joints, yellow eyes
  with membrane-glow, the low-frequency sound that lives under hearing, the smell of iron and rot.
- The kin-pack structure: smaller creatures that test and circle; the harrower holds until the
  pack has softened the target.
- The kill method: go inside the reach. Pommel to the shoulder joint to bring it down; blade
  reversed up through the jaw into the skull. Braced feet for leverage.
- The return register: absurdist logistics (rope through the nostrils, dragging on frozen ground),
  garrison soldiers, the question nobody should have to ask, the one-line ending.

## 1. Canon Established {#SS-LSSS-1}

**Harrower (Sinterspawn classification):**
- Size: up to 14+ feet at the shoulder; quadruped with modified forelegs
- Hide: membrane-grey/brown, camouflage-effective in Caul autumn terrain
- Joints: extra joints in the forelegs doing work the observer cannot immediately account for
- Eyes: pale yellow, larger than any eye has a right to be; membrane-glow in the pupils pulses
  like a heartbeat independent of other body movement; this is the worst part
- Sound: not loud — low. Below conscious hearing, felt in the back teeth and spine. Fog forms
  on cold water surfaces before the creature moves. This is the warning.
- Smell: old iron and something beneath iron; membrane-heavy tissue rot; sits in the throat
- Movement: each foot placed deliberately; cracks stone and ice that a human crossing did not crack
- Pack: travels with kin (same species, smaller, juvenile or variant); kin test and circle;
  harrower engages after the kin have run the target
- Kill method: inside the reach — pommel to shoulder joint to drop it; blade reversed up through
  the jaw palate into the skull. Brace against the neck for leverage.
- What it does when it dies: makes a sound with no name; then nothing

**The Old Watch manuals** mention the reach problem once and refuse to elaborate. This is
because the elaboration is: go inside. The elaboration kills people who don't understand it.

**The garrison crows:** Soldiers stationed at the Watch-adjacent garrisons. The literal crows
follow blood trails. Both kinds are present at the return.
