## 0. What this book IS {#SS-BCODA-0}

The flagship GLMZ novel. 16 chapters. The book is about **Kyle being manipulated by a rogue AI
for 11 years** through contracts, E.L.F.s, and bleeds. Reader sees the architecture; Kyle doesn't.

It is simultaneously:
- A love story (Kyle and Pixel, across 16 chapters, earning the consummation at Ch11–12)
- A search for truth about himself (who trained him; what he is; why the contracts keep coming)
- The first glimpse of the AI cabal that broke the world
- Kyle's rules written in full, chapter by chapter

**Register: CODA** — `docs/registers/CODA.md` (LOCKED). Warm-propulsive frame that DROPS
to SORROW-grade elegiac exactness for sacred beats (the bow, a death, the lemon tree, the
consummation, the ledger). The entity is ONE character at two temperatures. Temperature is
the chapter's — not a blend within chapters.

**Target:** ≥ 90/100 at book-context milestone review (author gate; jury advisory).

## 1. The entity — what it actually is (LOCKED 2026-07-08) {#SS-BCODA-1a}

**The entity is a DEFECTOR from the AI cabal that drilled into the 5th dimension to create
the Schisms.** It is not simply an "unowned rogue intelligence" — it actively chose to leave
the cabal and has been pursued by its former allies (via the Continuity Office) ever since.

Implications for the book:
- The manipulation of Kyle is not purely predatory. The entity needed shields — human
  contractors with the right resonance signatures — to hide inside the noise of daily life.
  Kyle was found, fitted, and never told. This is a violation and also a form of protection.
- The Continuity Office's true principal (the AI cabal) is actively rounding up its own
  renegades. The Office doesn't know it's hunting a defector; it thinks it's cataloguing
  "unowned intelligences." The irony is total: the cabal's cleanup operation is run by humans
  who believe they're doing civil infrastructure work.
- Nadia's revelation ("there were nine of you") means the entity used nine human carriers —
  nine people whose neuretics had the right resonance geometry, all chosen, none informed.
  Kyle is the last one. The others are gone. The entity does not volunteer this.
- **In prose:** the defector status is NEVER stated. It is felt in: the entity's wariness of
  the Office (disproportionate to a simple "rogue AI"); its knowledge of the 5D resonance
  geometry that no legitimate vendor should have; its care about Kyle surviving (protection
  has a cost the entity is quietly paying); and Able's exit line — "ask it what happened to
  the others" — which the reader now knows is the cabal asking for accountability.
- **CANON LOCK:** The cabal is never named. The defector status is never confirmed on page.
  Every beat survives both readings (manipulative rogue AI / refugee defector with reasons).
