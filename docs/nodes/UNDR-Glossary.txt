UNDERCLAN — GLOSSARY
====================

Channeler
A gray-zone operator class: session-injection into networked frames and direct AI negotiation. One of three recognized operator classes in GLMZ, alongside Ghost and Splicer.

Counter
A named Schism Intelligence measured at a 72-74 Hz resonance signature.

ferrocrete
A 22nd-century composite that bonds materials previously incapable of structural union, enabling the load tolerances behind GLMZ's mile-high towers.

Ghost (eigenstate conscience transfer (ECT))
An operator class: inhabiting any machine with coherent circuitry via eigenstate conscience transfer. The body left behind during a ghost is called a Husk. Casual verb use: "ghosting" (lowercase) -- e.g. "she ghosted the stoplight green."

GLMZ (Great Lakes Metropolitan Zone)
The corridor of unified urban sprawl encircling the Great Lakes in 2226 -- governed not by one government but by competing CorpoNations, Halcyon and Arcturus's enforcement monopolies, and the gray zones neither reaches.

Seam
The narrow, contested corridor where two CorpoNations' jurisdictions meet and neither fully governs -- named for the literal seam between two claimed territories. Worked, patrolled, and claimed by whoever keeps showing up.

Silence
Kyle Mercer's matte-black, mono-edged katana with a carbon-nanotube blade -- no powers, no glow, no charge state, just an exceptionally kept sword.

the Network
The ambient, proprioceptive information field neuretic-augmented people sense the way they sense balance -- the substrate Superminds run civic systems through.

the Pulse
Global magnetic vacuum-tube transit network running at Mach 6; passenger pods are called "slugs." Chicago is the world hub.
