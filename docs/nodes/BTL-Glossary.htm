Between the Lines — Glossary

14 terms.

ARC — Aerostatic Regulatory Command
The checkpoint-enforcement arm governing crossings between corporate interior blocs and the Grey Zone ground — customs, biometric processing, and dispatch of Direct Response Units. Distinct from Arcturus Defense Solutions (ArcSec), which handles physical/military security; ARC is a civil processing and customs authority.
Arcturus — Arcturus Defense Solutions
The CorpoNation holding GLMZ's physical-security monopoly, operating under the name ArcSec: sovereign perimeter defense, PMC contracting, corporate military, autonomous weapons.
Bleed
Data leakage between psionic minds -- an unwanted crossover of thought or sensation between nearby Reads. Not a synonym for a Schism.
Cordero Desk
A trafficking operation run out of the Grey Zone ground by Mateo Cordero, structured and staffed like a logistics business rather than a criminal syndicate with a public identity. Operates through cutouts — Cordero himself has never personally met most of the checkpoint officers who keep his routes open.
Counter
A named Schism Intelligence measured at a 72-74 Hz resonance signature.
DRU — Direct Response Unit
An ARC dispatch team sent to a checkpoint or crossing incident; response time and routing can be manipulated by a checkpoint officer with dispatch influence, a standing vulnerability in the ARC system.
ferrocrete
A 22nd-century composite that bonds materials previously incapable of structural union, enabling the load tolerances behind GLMZ's mile-high towers.
GLMZ — Great Lakes Metropolitan Zone
The corridor of unified urban sprawl encircling the Great Lakes in 2226 -- governed not by one government but by competing CorpoNations, Halcyon and Arcturus's enforcement monopolies, and the gray zones neither reaches.
neuretics
Thought-operated in-head compute that everyone in GLMZ has at some tier -- grown, not implanted. Checkpoints read the wearer biometrically through it. A common noun, lowercase, like "sword" or "hand."
Operator
General GLMZ term for a gray-zone specialist working outside CorpoNation-sanctioned channels. Three recognized classes exist: Channeler, Ghost, and Splicer.
Seam
The narrow, contested corridor where two CorpoNations' jurisdictions meet and neither fully governs -- named for the literal seam between two claimed territories. Worked, patrolled, and claimed by whoever keeps showing up.
Silence
Kyle Mercer's matte-black, mono-edged katana with a carbon-nanotube blade -- no powers, no glow, no charge state, just an exceptionally kept sword.
Substrate
The physical/computational medium neuretics and much of GLMZ's infrastructure run on -- dense enough that AI life (see E.L.F.) has been known to emerge spontaneously from its molecular complexity.
the Loop
GLMZ's wealthiest, highest-status territory -- prestige tier 5 in the city's informal territory hierarchy.
