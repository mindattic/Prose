## LOGLINE

When a massive unregistered Behemoth wanders into GLMZ's outer tiers, four people with competing interests race to decode its intention before ArcSec fires — and discover the machine was following orders no human alive remembers giving.

---

## PREMISE

An Iowan Behemoth — a harvest automation system worth billions — crosses into Zone-12 with no manufacturer signature and no kill-switch response. ArcSec reads it as incursion; AgriCore reads it as theft; the surveyor reads it as impossible. A maintenance worker from the machine's origin site knows the real problem: the Behemoth is looking for a marker that no longer exists in corporate records. The team has hours before Kaden Vũ's battalion vaporizes a machine that is simply trying to finish a job older than the current territorial map.

---

## REGISTER

Procedural and precise. Maris POV anchors the story — she documents everything, names distances in metres, does not trail off. The Behemoth moves like a proof: one step follows the next, no deviation, no ambiguity. The human panic happens around this certainty, not against it. The resolution is quiet. No explosion. No chase. A machine finishes a job.

Second register note: this is a **warm story**. The ending is genuinely warm — the Behemoth was never a threat. The last image is a seed-marker in the soil, the machine dormant, and Maris writing a report that describes something remarkable as routine. The warmth is earned by not telegraphing it.

## THE BEHEMOTH: CROWFOOT

Iowan Behemoth. No registered manufacturer. No CorpoNation transponder. No override beacon.  
Forty-two metres tall. Eighteen hundred tonnes. Six articulated harvest-limbs the width of train carriages.  
Runs warm from bioreactor heat. Smells of turned soil and oxidised metal.  
Hull: dark agricultural steel, decades of weathering, no corporate livery.

**What it is doing:** Running a crop-cycle protocol. Specifically: planting a territory-definition marker at the endpoint of its assigned northern route — a GPS coordinate that is now inside GLMZ's Zone-11 residential tier. The boundary moved in 2219. The machine's route did not update. It has been walking the same circuit for seven years trying to finish a job that is now geometrically impossible.

**What it is NOT doing:** Attacking. Invading. Demonstrating hostility. Every "threatening" behaviour (pausing at intersections, pivoting, retracing steps) is territorial confusion: it is searching for landmarks that should be there and are not.

**Resolution mechanic:** Yusuf feeds it a corrected endpoint — the Zone-12 outer boundary, where it entered GLMZ. The machine plants its seed-marker there instead. Cycle complete. It powers down.

## LOCKS

- The Behemoth is **NOT alive**. It is NOT a character. It does not have feelings, intentions, or experiences. It has a protocol. The alien-contact resonance comes entirely from the humans projecting onto and learning to read a non-sentient system.
- No one fires a weapon at the Behemoth. The resolution is not about combat. The climax is a coordinate input and a machine that turns around.
- The ending is warm. Unambiguously warm. The Behemoth did its job. Everyone goes home.
- Yusuf is the emotional core but Maris is the POV anchor. Keep the camera with Maris.
- Priya starts as comic-corporate and ends as genuinely useful. Do not let her stay a joke.
- Kaden is not a villain. He is a man with a doctrine and a problem that does not fit it. He stands down because he was looking for a reason to.
