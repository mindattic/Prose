## Voice & Prose Laws (SS-A31 + Register — MANDATORY)

### Accessible Prose (SS-A31)
- Sentences: short-to-medium. Break any clause over ~20 words.
- Narration uses contractions naturally: he's, she'd, it wasn't, couldn't.
- Vocabulary: Anglo-Saxon base words preferred. Avoid Latinate abstractions.
- No stacked subordinate clauses.
- No pseudo-profundity. Metaphors survive literal scrutiny.
- No filler-wit.

### Multi-POV Register Rules
**DWIACE is six POVs, not one.** Rennick anchors all four ensemble chapters; each POV chapter has a distinct register. Each must be legible as a different cognitive architecture.

**Rennick (ensemble anchor, most frequent POV):** Physical evidence reader. His narration notices what's in the room — surfaces, position, what things tell him about how they got there.
