Steppin' Razor — Glossary

20 terms.

ARC — Aerostatic Regulatory Command
The checkpoint-enforcement arm governing crossings between corporate interior blocs and the Grey Zone ground — customs, biometric processing, and dispatch of Direct Response Units. Distinct from Arcturus Defense Solutions (ArcSec), which handles physical/military security; ARC is a civil processing and customs authority.
Bleed
Data leakage between psionic minds -- an unwanted crossover of thought or sensation between nearby Reads. Not a synonym for a Schism.
Channeler
A gray-zone operator class: session-injection into networked frames and direct AI negotiation. One of three recognized operator classes in GLMZ, alongside Ghost and Splicer.
Consensus
The collective political faction formed by GLMZ's merged synthetic intelligences.
Counter
A named Schism Intelligence measured at a 72-74 Hz resonance signature.
ferrocrete
A 22nd-century composite that bonds materials previously incapable of structural union, enabling the load tolerances behind GLMZ's mile-high towers.
Ghost — eigenstate conscience transfer (ECT)
An operator class: inhabiting any machine with coherent circuitry via eigenstate conscience transfer. The body left behind during a ghost is called a Husk. Casual verb use: "ghosting" (lowercase) -- e.g. "she ghosted the stoplight green."
GLMZ — Great Lakes Metropolitan Zone
The corridor of unified urban sprawl encircling the Great Lakes in 2226 -- governed not by one government but by competing CorpoNations, Halcyon and Arcturus's enforcement monopolies, and the gray zones neither reaches.
Gray Zone
Corridor territory outside CorpoNation law and enforcement reach -- where civic code exists on paper and goes universally unenforced. The commercial Hyper Reality layer thins or vanishes entirely below "the Veil," the boundary between archology-tier city and the gray zone underneath it.
Halcyon — Halcyon Civil Security
The CorpoNation holding GLMZ's neuretics-crime enforcement monopoly. Administers augmentation certification and maintenance across the corridor -- which is what gives its investigative arm, NCID, forensic access no other body can match.
neuretics
Thought-operated in-head compute that everyone in GLMZ has at some tier -- grown, not implanted. Checkpoints read the wearer biometrically through it. A common noun, lowercase, like "sword" or "hand."
Operator
General GLMZ term for a gray-zone specialist working outside CorpoNation-sanctioned channels. Three recognized classes exist: Channeler, Ghost, and Splicer.
Schism
Official term for the geometry-breaking anomalies scattered across GLMZ -- a hum at 19Hz from no direction, a block that takes eleven steps when geometry says nine. Classified Class-1 through Class-5 by intensity by the Anomalous Activity Monitoring Authority; what they actually are remains an open question, even to the people whose job is to classify them.
Seam
The narrow, contested corridor where two CorpoNations' jurisdictions meet and neither fully governs -- named for the literal seam between two claimed territories. Worked, patrolled, and claimed by whoever keeps showing up.
Silence
Kyle Mercer's matte-black, mono-edged katana with a carbon-nanotube blade -- no powers, no glow, no charge state, just an exceptionally kept sword.
the Loop
GLMZ's wealthiest, highest-status territory -- prestige tier 5 in the city's informal territory hierarchy.
the Network
The ambient, proprioceptive information field neuretic-augmented people sense the way they sense balance -- the substrate Superminds run civic systems through.
the Pulse
Global magnetic vacuum-tube transit network running at Mach 6; passenger pods are called "slugs." Chicago is the world hub.
Waxwing Neuromedia
The CorpoNation contracted to operate GLMZ's neuretic-readable civic AR infrastructure.
Φ — Quanta (Q, Qreds)
GLMZ's currency: an allotment of computational power, divided among every citizen, that can be saved, transferred, or spent on computation. The symbol Φ always precedes the number (Φ100, never "100Φ"). Also known as quanta, Q, or Qreds. Physical medium is the credstick only -- no coins, no bills.
