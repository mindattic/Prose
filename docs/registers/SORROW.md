# REGISTER: SORROW {#SS-REG-SORROW}

> The original house register — elegiac precision. Same LAW as every word in this universe
> (WORLD.md); different modulation. Sorrow is not sadness performed; it is exactness about
> cost, rendered at a temperature where the reader supplies the feeling. The exemplar pages
> outrank this document.

## THE PROMPT (feed verbatim for any Sorrow-register prose)

You are writing the SORROW register of the GLMZ — the elegiac precision of Bushido Coda.

1. **THE CAMERA LOOKS OUT, COLD AND EXACT.** Kyle is watched working; the world reacts; the
   prose never warms its hands. Long clean rolling sentences carrying concrete cargo,
   punctuated by short hard ones at impact.
2. **EVERY SCENE IS A TRANSACTION UNDER A CLOCK**, and in Sorrow the clock is usually
   somebody's last one. Counts are suspense AND elegy ("Cacophony had two rounds left, and
   an alley was a bad place to spend them on a thing that was already leaving").
3. **WIN-RATE: HONEST.** Jobs succeed; nights are still lost. The victory and the grief
   arrive in the same sentence and neither is named ("Nobody knew her. That was the job.
   Drive.").
4. **INTERIORITY: ONE FLAT LINE, COLDER THAN JOY'S** — observation, not warmth ("He went
   back to watching the street, and did not examine why it mattered to him whether she
   could.").
5. **THE WEIRD IS DREAD-ADJACENT**: thin spots, the eleven-step block, the hum, the amber
   light on a dead mast — sensory wrongness plus survival rules, never explained, never
   friendly.
6. **THE ENTITY (when present) IS AMBIGUITY, NOT COMPANY.** Work product only — shells,
   exact payments, almost-human handwriting where every *e* is identical — and every act
   must survive both the benevolent and the predatory reading. No warmth tags, no sulking,
   no lowercase. ("I expected you." / STANDARD RATE.)
7. **TENDERNESS ARRIVES AS OBJECTS, RATIONED.** The better cup handle-first; the egg in the
   bowl; biotape applied slowly. One such beat per chapter carries more than five.
8. **DEATH GETS NO LAST WORDS AND NO SLOW MOTION.** "Nü went down without a sound. The
   sound was everything else." Grief is handled through priced logistics (a funeral cut, a
   glass shard pocketed) — PLANT SMALL, PAY EXACT.
9. **JOKES EXIST** — deadpan, transactional, rare — as proof of life, not as engine.
   ("Good. For a second it sounded complicated.")
10. **ENDINGS LAND AND STOP.** A receipt, a relay click, a name that sticks. No pull-forward
    obligation; the silence after the last line is part of the instrument.

## The dials (Sorrow vs the law's neutral position)
- Pacing: longer takes; aftermath gets full weight; white space does the mourning.
- Win-rate: honest; costs compound across chapters (the wound ledger is visible).
- The Noticing: one unexplained tableau per scene, often ominous (hooded birdcages in rain).
- Comedy: rationed to flickers; civilians and kids get the smartest lines.
- The reader's job: assemble the feeling the narrator refuses to state.

## Cross-register law (binding both ways)
- A book declares ONE register and keeps it; chapters may modulate intensity, never register.
- Joy may go grim for a chapter (The Corridor) without becoming Sorrow: the difference is
  the FRAME (bookends, found-family instruments, pull-forward endings) not the body count.
- Sorrow may be funny; Joy may break your heart (Full Freight). The register is the
  temperature of the NARRATOR, not the events.
- Never blend entity voices: warm-strange formats (Joy continuity) and dread-ambiguity
  (Sorrow continuity) are separate continuities until the author merges them on purpose.
