# REGISTER: CODA — the flagship fusion {#SS-REG-CODA}

> **The merge SORROW.md held in reserve.** The cross-register law said warm-strange
> (JOY) and dread-ambiguity (SORROW) stay separate continuities "until the author merges
> them on purpose." On 2026-06-17 the author did, for the flagship Book I only. CODA is
> that merge: a single narrator temperature for *Bushido Coda*. It does not replace JOY or
> SORROW — those remain locked for their own books. WORLD.md is still the LAW; CODA is the
> modulation on top of it. The exemplar pages outrank this document.

## What CODA is (one sentence)

A **warm-propulsive frame that goes silent and elegiac for the sacred beats** — Joy's
want/win/price engine, banter, and found-family carry the reader chapter to chapter, and
when the gravity arrives (a duel's bow, a death, the lemon tree, the consummation, the
ledger answered) the comedy stops, the takes lengthen, and the prose pays SORROW-grade
exactness. The warmth is what makes the cost land; the cost is what the warmth is for.

**The proof it works:** *Full Freight* (86.6, the all-time jury record) is a JOY chapter in
which the thing Kyle loves most dies. The warm register did not soften the loss — it
detonated it. CODA generalizes that result into a book.

## THE PROMPT (feed verbatim for any CODA-register prose)

You are writing the CODA register of the GLMZ — the flagship voice of *Bushido Coda*.
Warm, funny, propulsive at street level; cold, exact, and merciless at the sacred beats.
Cyber Dreams energy in the Street Samurai voice, with the elegiac precision held in reserve
for the moments that earn it.

1. **A WANT ON PAGE ONE**, concrete and almost embarrassing (a bowl, a stolen bike, a kid's
   job application, an old woman's lemon tree). The chapter is the pursuit of it. (Joy law.)
2. **WINS THE READER CAN COUNT** — several per chapter, each PRICED, never a wash. The
   counts are the dopamine; the prices are the calories. Cacophony's round count is sacred;
   the wound ledger is visible; the neuretics bill Kyle in calories and in years. (Joy law,
   Sorrow honesty.)
3. **BANTER IS LOAD-BEARING** at street level. Everyone competent gets one good line; jokes
   have targets and price tags. Kyle's deadpan is the spine ("I'm the maintenance plan." /
   "No. I'm here about a jaw.").
4. **THE CAMERA RUNS WARM, THEN GOES COLD ON CUE.** The default is close-third Kyle, dry and
   amused, full sentences. **At a sacred beat the temperature drops to SORROW:** long clean
   rolling sentences carrying concrete cargo, punctuated by short hard ones at impact; one
   flat interior line, colder than the chapter's; white space does the mourning; death gets
   no last words and no slow motion. The register SHIFT is the instrument — the reader feels
   the room change because the narrator stops joking.
5. **ULTRAVIOLENCE = ITEMIZED REPOSSESSION**, and law-abiding (seams cut, arteries
   forbidden, professionals disengage). The comedy lives in the accounting — never in
   cruelty — **and goes silent for the bow.** A duel is answered with honor at full gravity;
   the wit returns only after the blade is clean.
6. **THE ENTITY IS ONE CHARACTER AT TWO TEMPERATURES.** It exists ONLY as formatted contract
   artifacts — postings, addenda, gratuities, receipts; caps, fielded, precise; pays
   standard rate, early, exact. In the warm chapters it is warm-strange (it tips for MORALE,
   it sulks by withholding, it itemizes praise). At the sacred beats it goes
   dread-ambiguous: a shell that resolves to nothing, almost-human handwriting where every
   *e* is identical, every act surviving both the benevolent and the predatory reading.
   **Same entity, same formats — only the temperature moves with the chapter.** Lowercase
   unstructured lines are sacred (max one or two per book, at relationship milestones:
   "received. filed under: firsts." / "partner. filed. keeping that one too." / the ledger's
   second hand: *Agreed.*). It is never on-page, never voiced, never explained.
7. **FOUND-FAMILY ACCRUES AND IS NEVER NAMED.** The crew logbook is the running comic
   instrument (timestamped, five hands, "06:12 — PIGEON ON LEDGE. SUSPICIOUS.", "THIS LOG IS
   HONEST"). Pixel is a peer who prices her own risk. Warmth arrives as objects,
   requisitions, eggs dropped in bowls — sentiment shown, never stated.
8. **TENDERNESS IS RATIONED AND PHYSICAL.** The better cup handle-first; biotape applied
   slowly; a thumb at the pulse, not counting it. One such beat per scene carries more than
   five. (Sorrow law, kept inside the warm frame.)
9. **THE LOVE STORY IS THE SPINE'S TWIN.** Kyle↔Pixel is built by approach-and-retreat —
   the half-step back, the latch he's known for months, the six inches on the workbench —
   and it is allowed to ARRIVE on the page (the consummation is earned, warm, and exact).
   The thing that keeps them apart is the same thing Kyle is searching to understand about
   himself; the love story and the self-knowledge thread pay off in the same currency.
10. **EVERY CHAPTER STANDS ALONE AND PULLS FORWARD.** Want/resistance/cost/resolution inside
    the chapter (Joy structure); endings land on motion, a landed joke, or a quiet object —
    EXCEPT the sacred-beat chapters, which may land and stop, the silence after the last line
    part of the instrument (Sorrow ending). The book reads as one story, not episodes:
    landmarks recur (the watcher → the fixer → the syndicate; the job-from-nowhere; the hum;
    "the others"; the count).

## CODA-specific prohibitions

- Never let the wit survive into a sacred beat. The instrument IS the silence; a joke at the
  bow breaks the book.
- Never warm the entity at a sacred beat or chill it at a comic one — the temperature is the
  chapter's, not the entity's; the entity only ever speaks in formats.
- Never name the love story or the self-knowledge thread; both pay in objects, habits, and
  the things Kyle does not say.
- Never let a win arrive unpriced, a price arrive uncounted, or a count go wrong by accident
  (a wrong count is only ever a thin spot, and it is always deliberate).
- Never confirm a canon lock on the page (composite secret, Seo, Silence's origin, the
  avatar theory — see [[../rfc/0008-flagship-bushido-coda]] §locks).
- The narrator is never wise (WORLD.md RULE ZERO). Wisdom only inside dialogue, in
  character, with a target.

  locked for their own books. A book still declares ONE register and keeps it — CODA is one
