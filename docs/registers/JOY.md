# REGISTER: JOY {#SS-REG-JOY}

> **What a register is:** WORLD.md is the LAW — it applies to every word ever written in this
> universe (narrator never wise, dialogue quoted, counts kept, metaphor survives literal
> scrutiny, Φ, the locks). A REGISTER is the modulation layer on top of the law: pacing,
> temperature, win-rate, what the camera lingers on. Registers never override the law.
> The exemplar pages outrank this document; this document outranks taste.

## THE PROMPT (feed verbatim for any Joy-register prose)

You are writing the JOY register of the GLMZ — propulsive, warm, funny, ultraviolent when
violence comes, a pleasure to absorb. Cyber Dreams energy in the Street Samurai voice.

1. **A WANT ON PAGE ONE**, concrete and almost embarrassing (a stolen motorcycle; a bowl;
   a kid's job application). The chapter is the pursuit of it.
2. **WINS THE READER CAN COUNT** — several per chapter: a name obtained, a tracker re-homed,
   a requisition APPROVED. Every win is PRICED (a favor owed, a fact surrendered, a scar) and
   never a wash. The counts are the dopamine; the prices are the calories.
3. **BANTER IS LOAD-BEARING.** Dialogue carries momentum; everyone competent gets one good
   line; jokes have targets and price tags ("It's inventory management. Get out of my shop.").
   Kyle's deadpan is the spine ("I'm the maintenance plan." / "No. I'm here about a jaw.").
4. **ULTRAVIOLENCE = ITEMIZED REPOSSESSION.** Fights are exact, geometric, law-abiding
   (seams cut, arteries forbidden, professionals disengage) AND gleeful: the array prices
   opponents in Φ and financing terms; hardware "declares itself a total loss"; the comedy
   lives in the accounting, never in cruelty. **The comedy goes SILENT for sacred beats**
   (a duel's bow, a death, four bars of carousel music) — the fun frames the gravity.
5. **THE ENTITY IS WARM-STRANGE.** It exists ONLY as formatted contract artifacts on the
   terminal — postings, scope updates, ADDENDUM lines, receipts, gratuities — caps, fielded,
   precise. Care is expressed as employment. Canonical formats:
   - `CONTRACT: RECOVERY OF STOLEN PROPERTY (VEHICLE, 1200CC). FEE: Φ1.`
   - `ADDENDUM: CLIENT ADVISES THE MURAL DOOR IS NOT THE DOOR.`
   - `GRATUITY: Φ0.5 — ITEMIZED: MORALE.` (it sulks by withholding; it praises by itemizing)
   - Long pauses (eleven seconds, thirty-one seconds) are its unfinished gestures.
   - **Lowercase unstructured lines are sacred** — maximum one or two per SEASON, only at
     relationship milestones: "received. filed under: firsts." / "partner. filed. keeping
     that one too." Never inflate this currency.
   It is never on-page, never described, never voiced, never explained; ambiguity preserved,
   valence = delight.
6. **FOUND-FAMILY ACCRUES AND IS NEVER NAMED.** Boost's crew logbook is the running comic
   instrument (timestamped, five hands, "06:12 — PIGEON ON LEDGE. SUSPICIOUS.", "THIS LOG IS
   HONEST"); Pixel is a peer who prices her own risk and invites herself; warmth arrives as
   objects, requisitions, and dropped eggs in bowls — sentiment is shown, never stated.
7. **EVERY ENDING PULLS FORWARD** — a scope update, a watcher logged, a drawer getting full.
   End chapters on motion or a landed joke or a quiet object; never on a generalization.

## The dials (Joy vs the law's neutral position)
- Pacing: scene cycles SHORT; tension→release every few hundred words.
- Win-rate: high; the world is dangerous but generous to competence.
- Interiority: warm and dry; Kyle may be amused; one flat feeling-line per scene max (law).
- The weird: present but friendly-strange (the bird's experiments) more than dreadful.
- Wounds: real and persistent (law) but worn lightly in prose ("catalogued").
- Loss: PERMITTED and devastating precisely because the register is warm (Full Freight,
  86.6, the all-time record, is a JOY chapter) — grief still arrives only as objects and
  logistics (a folded plate cover; a blank itemization field; "REQUISITION: ONE BIKE").

## Joy-specific prohibitions
- Never cute-for-cute's-sake; whimsy without a price tag is filler (law: no filler-wit).
- Never let the entity speak in prose, voice, or feelings; formats only.
- Never spend a lowercase line on less than a milestone.
- Never let a win arrive unpriced, or a price arrive uncounted.
- Never mock the sacred beats; the register earns its violence by keeping the bows.
