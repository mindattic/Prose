# SCRY — Universe Craft Rules {#SS-SCRY}

> **Scope: SCRY universe stories only.** Universal prose rules live in **docs/CRAFT.md**

> **docs/universes/CAUL.md** (generated; run `ss --generate-canon-md --type UniverseCanon`).
> This file records SCRY-specific craft additions and overrides to the Base layer.
> When this file and CRAFT.md disagree, this file wins for SCRY stories.

## 1. Naming Canon (SCRY world facts — always observe)

- **The universe is SCRY.** The world within it is **The Caul**. These are not
  interchangeable: SCRY names the cosmological system (Spheres, Amnios, the Oculus);
  The Caul names the inhabited world Lyra, Soren, and the rest live in.
- Write "The Caul" — always capitalized, always with the article. Never "the Caul" (lowercase
  t) in narration. Never "Caul" alone unless a character uses it in dialogue as shorthand.
- Characters call their world "the Caul" in speech; narration writes "The Caul."

---

## 2. Death and Consequence (SCRY addition — overrides any CRAFT default)

**Death is permanent. SS-LAW-19.** No resurrection, no soul recall, no restoration of
the dead. A character who dies is gone. Do not write around this — do not imply a path back,
do not render death as reversible through any mechanism, including:
- Relics
- Accumulation
- Keeper intervention
- Divine will

The **Accumulation Doctrine**: abilities and power accumulate across campaigns through
experience and earned knowledge — NEVER through memory restoration or recovery of a past
self. "Restores memory" is banned phrasing. Death cost is paid once; it is not refunded.

---

## 3. Tone and Visual Language (SCRY addition to CRAFT §1–2)

**Visual register**: FFVI-palette + Scottish Hebrides. Stone, slate, low light, earned color.
Not high fantasy spectacle — weight, texture, cold. The castles are real castles (mass,
murder-holes, damp). The landscape is post-glacial and indifferent.

**Military register** (for war stories: M101, GIW): 20th-century deadpan. The prose
describes logistics, mud, and arithmetic — not glory. Misfires and procedural failures are
the comedy engine; heroism is quiet, late, and expensive.

**Vigil watchers** — not Lanternkin (RETIRED). The Bheur: an afterlife that cannot be
confirmed, only hoped for. Never write the Bheur as real on the page; write the belief.

---

## 4. The Weird (SCRY addition to CRAFT §4 The Noticing)

The cosmological uncanny in SCRY:
- **Amnios**: the golden substrate between Spheres — NEVER rendered as visible to
  un-Scryed characters; render as pressure, wrongness, a sound with no direction.
- **The Oculus**: a scry that never closed. Characters do not explain it; they work around
  it or are worked through by it.
- **Sinterspawn**: write the bodies first — the physical fact of them — then the wrongness.
  Never render them as supernatural until the physical fact is exhausted.

---

## 5. Hard Prohibitions (SCRY world facts)

- Death is permanent: no resurrection, no memory restoration, no exceptions (SS-LAW-19).
- The Bheur is unconfirmable: never affirm its existence as fact in narration.
- "Lanternkin" RETIRED — write "vigil watcher."
- Myrmidon bodies house stolen MINDS (Verlaine's program) — never treat them as alive;
  never reveal Soren Rowe's M-number (M-1018883) except in designated story moments.
- Dame Lyra has NO M-number — she was taken by Ocipheus outside Verlaine's program.
- The Accumulation Doctrine: accumulated power, never restored memory. "Restores memory"
  is banned.
