## Open method note: miracle claims as a class

A recurring "Mixed" pattern above (healings, nature miracles) raises a question this ledger
defers rather than resolves ad hoc per pericope: **what does "checkable" even mean for a
reported miracle?** No amount of independent evidence can confirm or refute that a specific
1st-century healing was supernatural in origin. What *can* be checked, where evidence exists,
is the surrounding claim — was there a real place, a real crowd, a plausible illness — while the
miracle attribution itself sits outside any evidence's reach in either direction. When this
campaign returns to draft the individual healing-narrative pericopes, they should get a shared
short method note (likely appended to this file or split into its own
`docs/gospel/miracle-claims-as-a-class.md`) rather than re-litigating this in every entry.
