# RFC 0005 — BALANCE: the merge map (Book Three) — WITHDRAWN {#SS-RFC-0005}

> **WITHDRAWN 2026-06-11 by author ("cancel this, it's not the right way").** The mechanical
> merge of two existing books into a third was the wrong approach. The Balance book + its 24
> assembled chapters were deleted; Joy and Bushido Coda remain intact. This RFC is kept only
> as a record of the rejected approach. Do not resurrect without a new author directive.

> **Mandate (author, 2026-06-11):** a THIRD book that merges the best of *Joy* and *Bushido
> Coda* (Sorrow) into one complete, satisfying, true arc. NOT one injected into the other —
> a third best-of-everything assembly. Keep Sorrow's **will-they-won't-they** (Kyle/Pixel
> restraint) and **visual brutalism**; keep Joy's **banter** and **clean-cut goals + rhythm**.
> Assemble in order: **MAP → CHAPTERS → seamless STRANDS**. No Legion voting. Parent strand:
> **Balance** (`balance-019eba1d`). This RFC is the MAP.

## 0. The core insight {#SS-RFC-0005-0}

Joy and Sorrow are not two stories — they are **one story told twice, at two temperatures.**
Five titles exist in BOTH books (Work Order, The Interview, Year Three, Street Meat, The
Quiet Hour). The merge is therefore not collage; it is **choosing the truer telling of each
beat and building one spine that carries both registers without seams.**

The register law for Balance (the synthesis the author asked for):
- **Camera & violence = Sorrow.** Brutalist, exact, the vertical drowned city, chrome priced
  in Φ, arteries as forbidden lines, the duel's bow, wounds that persist. The set-pieces look
  like Teeth.
- **Goals, pacing, dialogue = Joy.** A want on page one, countable priced wins, banter that
  carries scenes, the entity as warm-strange employer in formatted contract-speak, found-
  family logbook texture, chapters that pull forward.
- **The heart = Sorrow's restraint + Joy's warmth, fused.** Pixel is the will-they-won't-they
  (Sorrow's "stop cataloguing me," the handle-first cup, the step-back he always takes) AND
  the peer who prices her own risk and gets the funny lines (Joy). The two are not in
  tension; restraint is what makes the banter land.
- **One rule:** the comedy goes SILENT for the sacred beats (a duel, a death, a cut line, a
  confession). The fun frames the gravity; it never undercuts it. (Proven in the With-Teeth
  Joy-register experiment.)

## 1. The unified arc (one complete story) {#SS-RFC-0005-1}

Kyle — street samurai, Atlas-grade neuretics quietly killing him, a code that prices
everything at standard rate — has worked for eleven years for an employer he has never seen.
Book Three is the story of that employer becoming a **partner**, the corponation that comes to
**own** it, the people Kyle assembles into a life despite himself, and the cost of doing the
right thing in a city that bills you for it.

**Three movements:**

- **ACT I — THE WORK.** Who Kyle is, what the code costs, the world that runs on invoices.
  The entity is a mystery (perfect paper, no face). Pixel established as the wall he won't
  climb. Ends when the work stops being random: he answers the ledger.
- **ACT II — THE QUESTION.** The entity arc detonates (the cage, STANDARD RATE), the city
  widens (Lotus, the Vultures, the Atlas thread, the machines), the Continuity Office emerges
  to hunt the employer's footprint, found-family accretes (Boost's crew), and the Pixel
  thread tightens toward the thing neither will say.
- **ACT III — THE BILL.** The trap, the loss (the bike between Boost and the truck), the
  answer (witness, paid at standard rate), and the partnership sealed. Corponations unbeaten
  but the district held; the question — *what are you willing to do* — answered by a man who
  can still live with himself. Coda hooks the series ("the others").

## 3. The two threads, woven {#SS-RFC-0005-3}

**The entity (plot spine):** mystery (ch1–6) → partner (ch9 Carousel) → revealed-as-employer
(ch10 Year Three, STANDARD RATE) → hunted (ch13 Glove, ch15 Offer) → goes dark (ch20) →
returns to settle accounts (ch23) → first true conversation (ch24). One letter per act,
escalating, every line surviving both the warm and the dread reading until the end.

**Pixel (heart, will-they-won't-they):** the wall (ch2) → the convention that works because
neither names it (ch2, 8, 17) → the peak where it's nearly said and he steps back (ch16 Sexy
Time) → she runs his crew when the world takes everything (ch20 Dark) → the rebuilt bike, the
thing done not said (ch24 Firsts). Sorrow restraint; Joy warmth; never resolved, always
present.

**Found family (Joy's gift to the merge):** Boost's crew + logbook accrete from ch2 onward;
the bike dies for Boost (ch21); the crew rebuilds it (ch24). This is what Sorrow lacked and
Joy supplies.

## 4. Build order (the author's three phases) {#SS-RFC-0005-4}

1. **MAP** (this RFC) — the arc + the 24-chapter source→Balance plan. ← DONE on approval.
2. **CHAPTERS** — for each Balance chapter, assemble the base strand's beats into the Balance
   book, then apply the fuse (donated lines/scenes from the other version) + the register
   note. Duplicates resolved to one. Work chapter by chapter, in order, keeping the prior
   chapter's last beats in mind so prose flows.
3. **STRANDS** — a continuity + seam pass across the whole assembled book: names, wound
   ledger, timeline, the two threads' through-lines, register consistency, so it reads as one
   seamless novel, not a merge.

No Legion voting at any phase (author directive); the gate is the read.

## 5. Continuity reconciliations to make during phase 3 {#SS-RFC-0005-5}

- **Two continuities, one timeline.** Joy (entity-as-partner, Boost, Continuity Office) and
  Sorrow (Year Three cage, Atlas/Nadia) must become ONE history. Year Three (the cage reveal)
  is the load-bearing event; the Joy standing-contract is its consequence, not a separate
  thread. Reconcile: the entity's warm contracts (Joy) BEGIN after STANDARD RATE (Year Three).
- **The entity's voice** unifies on the warm-strange formatted register (Joy), but its
  letters retain Sorrow's both-readings discipline (never confirm benevolence).
- **Wounds persist** (Sorrow's ledger): the corridor rib, the Glove burn, the Full-Freight
  spend carry across chapters.
- **Mrs. Chen, Sable, Vey, the Vultures, Lotus** are single canonical entities across both —
  already true in the DB; the merge just stops telling their intros twice.
- **"neuretics" lowercase; Φ; Silence/Cacophony; arteries forbidden; machines not alive** —
  WORLD.md law holds throughout.
