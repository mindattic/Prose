SCRY — Master Glossary

66 terms.

Age of Scrying
The calendar era from Year 1 (the discovery of Scrying) to Year 1312, ending with the Bolide; its first three centuries are separately remembered as the Age of Innocence, before Piercing existed.
Alloy 41
Entos's in-world designation for steel, manufactured locally from Scried metallurgical designs.
Amnios
The womb of an impossibly large biological creature -- the Expectant -- inside which every Sphere (including Entos and Sphere 31/Earth) floats as a single cell in a dense cluster, separated from its neighbors by thin films of Amnios fluid.
Anima Core
The component that makes a Shell or machine inhabitable by a Relayed mind -- without one, a Shell is just metal. Installed in Myrmidon Shells, Templar Station terminals, airship turrets and navigation systems, and certain war-beast constructs.
Anima Lantern
A portable containment vessel -- not a Shell, grants no body or motion -- that holds a captured mind the way a Shell holds an operating one. Used as a prison, a storage device, or a means of transport for a mind with nowhere else to go.
AoV — Age of Vigil
Entos's calendar epoch counted from the Bolide/Fall of Sinter (1312 absolute = 0 AoV). The current story year, 1371, is 59 AoV. Pre-Bolide dates belong to the preceding Age of Scrying.
Bheur
What House theology says waits for the dead. Because death is permanent and no one returns to report, whether it exists is unknowable; if real, it is held to process rather than punish -- no kings, no hierarchy.
Bolide, The
The impact that struck Sinter in 1312 absolute (0 AoV) -- the event Entos's calendar counts forward from. Source of the Cavity, the Ichor, and the quarantine line the Vigil holds at the Pass.
Canon — senior Liturgy rank
A senior Liturgy office above Lector: interprets doctrine, oversees Lector assignments, and adjudicates the most complex worthiness cases escalated from the field.
Catalyst — Gifted matter class; Xerum 525 primary
Infusion of alien Gifted matter into a living person, producing controlled physical change over time -- not consciousness transfer. The body is changed; the person who entered the procedure is the same person afterward, altered rather than replaced. Xerum 525 ("Red Mercury") is the primary, most-studied Catalyst; field slang is "the dose."
Cavity, The — the Sinter Cavity
The crater-like descent site within the Sinter quarantine region, where cluster-stage organisms complete and Sinterkin detach. "Sinter" names the wider quarantined region; "the Cavity" is the pit itself.
Champion
A soldier Transmuted so far past ordinary House rank structure that they are identified by name rather than designation. No House owns or commands a Champion -- only courts them.
cluster-stage organism — unformed Sinterkin
The pre-birth stage of a Sinterkin: an unfinished body pressed against the wet interior wall of the Sinter Cavity, joints and features still incomplete, that eventually detaches and falls to the cavity floor before it first moves.
Colchester naming — the Colchester naming convention
The informal practice of naming a Pierced object for the place and date it was taken from (e.g. "Colchester 1648"); nothing is catalogued or filed -- the name simply sticks because no one has a better one.
Compact, The
The series of operational agreements -- beginning as an eleven-line truce between three exhausted Basin institutions -- that ended the Blood Century and formalized the transition from warring tribes into the Houses.
Eaters of Monsters
Fjorheim wildmen who conduct self-directed, unsanctioned Transmutation outside Liturgy control, hunting Wall-breach creatures for the effect their meat produces -- three generations of empirical practice, founded after their predecessors were publicly executed for the same act.
Eirene's Gate
The primary Vigil checkpoint on the Entos-side bank of the Pass, named for Eirene, the last Champion of the vanished House Sinter, who died at that location in Year 1314.
Entos
The Sphere -- one cell among many adrift in the Amnios -- where every story in this setting takes place. Feudal, Liturgy-administered, and still counting its years from the Bolide.
Expectant, The
The vast organism whose body the Amnios and every Sphere inside it belong to -- worshipped by the Liturgy as the source of all Gifted matter, and the entity its doctrine holds gifted humanity the Catalyst in the first place.
Flood
A battle-Scryer's weaponized tearing of the membrane mid-engagement, dumping whatever creatures or atmosphere happen to be adjacent on the other Sphere's side into the fight; the crossers are random and short-lived in Entos's air, and the Liturgy treats the act as a war crime for the lasting damage it does to the membrane.
Gifted — Gifted matter / Gifted Ceremony
The alien substance (Catalyst class) the Liturgy Pierces from other Spheres, and the rite -- the Gifted Ceremony -- by which it is administered to the loyal. An "Infused" person or Templar has undergone it at least once; rank tracks how many times.
Harrower
Soldiers' and villagers' name for the body/behavior of the larger, pack-anchoring creature at a Sinterkin/Sinterspawn encounter -- a silhouette, not a taxonomic claim. Almost always a Sinterspawn wearing that shape; exceedingly rarely, a true Sinterkin wears the same silhouette, and nothing about the body alone tells you which.
Heloth
Two-legged steam-chemical machines, roughly 3m tall -- the dominant labor and transport technology of Entos, named for an extinct draft animal they replaced before horses ever could. Entos has no horse analogue; Heloth variants cover freight, courier, and battle-rig roles.
House
One of Entos's noble ruling families -- feudal, religiously bound to the Liturgy, and dependent on it for Gifted Ceremony access and protection. Houses field their own soldiers and Myrmidons but answer to Liturgy doctrine.
Ichor
The caustic, faintly luminous fluid that runs in Sinterspawn instead of blood -- reacts with untreated metal and blisters unprotected skin within seconds. Not venom, not sprayed offensively: simply what fills a body that came out of, or was made wrong by, the Sinter overflow. Handlers who work Sinterspawn at close range wear Ichor-proof plate.
Keeper, The
The person who runs a House's Scrying installation -- the technical authority whose control of the apparatus determines what the House can manufacture.
Knight — Dame (female form); formal Transmutation rank
The formal rank earned through multiple Gifted-Ceremony infusions and distinguished service, marking visibly ascendant physical change; the female form is "Dame." Continued infusion can carry a Knight toward Paladin and, rarely, Champion status.
Lector
A Liturgy field practitioner who travels between Houses administering the Gifted Ceremony, distributing Relics, and independently assessing worthiness -- a judgment no House can override.
Liturgy, The
The religious-scientific sect that administers the Gifted Ceremony and exists outside House politics, predating the current Houses. Does not fight wars or take territory; its doctrine and rites are the foundation the Houses' own legitimacy is built on.
Living War, The
The thousand-year, two-front war that defines Entos: Houses fighting Houses over Scrying territory and coalition advantage, and all Houses fighting the native monsters the war disturbs. No one alive remembers how or why it began.
Loera — House Loera — the Cutting Seat
The western cliff-coast House of the Entos, styled "the Cutting Seat" and headed by Imara Loera, "the Unwitnessed." Loera governs by charter rather than by parade: covert companies, corsair hulls, and licensed tradecraft crews — the Blink Thieves' trade among them — all operate under Cutting Seat charters, and the head of House is by design the person the record never catches. Because Loera issues its own service kit, "Loera" functions in the prose as a tradecraft signature rather than a place name: Loera boot-prints (the narrow heel and non-standard toe-cap of the coast tanneries) mark a wearer as Loera service rather than a contractor, and Loera hulls do not change custody mid-crossing whatever harbour they finally put in at.
M-101 — Myrmidon Shell designation
The short, spoken designation given to an activated Myrmidon (e.g. "M-101"), distinct from its longer catalog/intake record number (e.g. "M-1018883"). A Myrmidon may insist on the short form as its name and reject the longer number as "paperwork."
Monster Meat
The flesh of Entos's native monsters. Eating it triggers the same biological transformation mechanism as an unguided Transmutation, gradually turning the eater into a humanoid monster; the Oathless, left to starve by the Houses, have the highest exposure rate.
Myrmidon
A Shell (a Liturgy-built android construct) with a human mind Relayed into it and operating it -- the mind always Pierced from another Sphere, never Entos-born, never Transmutation-eligible. Death is permanent: if a Myrmidon's Shell is destroyed while its mind is still inside, the mind is gone -- no shell-cycle, no return.
Oathless, The
People outside House structure -- deserters and civilians who never swore, or broke, a House oath. Hunted by the Houses as unlicensed combatants, they scavenge Scried equipment from battlefields and have the highest rate of eating Monster Meat.
Ocipheus
The House of the island nation (Britain analog); seat of the Canon Immaculate and the oldest continuously documented Scrying installation in Entos, staffed by the longest-tenured vigil operators in any House.
Oculus Lacrimans Vigilantiae — The Weeping Eye of Vigilance
Sinter work, Year 630 -- not a pulled object but a Scry that never closed: an aperture struck onto the core of Sinter and never sealed, so it has held seven hundred and forty-one years of unbroken sight rather than the look-and-loss of an ordinary Scry. Held by House Ocipheus since Year 1330 (forty-one years' custody at the time of Vigil's End). Weeps a slow gold bead (Amnios) at the rim; paired with the Orbis Omnividens. Short form: the Oculus, or the Weeping Eye.
Orbis Omnividens — The All-Seeing Orb
The Oculus Lacrimans Vigilantiae's paired half -- the registry name for a Relic nobody at Ocipheus had catalogued as separate from the eye that housed it until the two were split apart at the crater. Left to fall back into the depths it had always watched, to keep seeing whether or not anyone remains to read what it sees.
Order of the Watch
The Vigil's formal administrative body: draws mixed-House personnel under Liturgy charter to staff the quarantine line at the Pass, administer the Descent Corps' expeditions into the Sinter zone, and issue crossing permits -- answerable to the Canon Immaculate, not any Warrior King.
Paladin
The Transmutation rank above Knight, marked by significant, rare visible ascendance; a Knight closing on Paladin status is considered exceptional.
Pallor
The northern fjord House (Scandinavia analog), the oldest surviving martial succession tradition in Entos -- ruled by a Warrior King confirmed by the Long Seat rather than by heredity -- which absorbed the failing House Fjorheim three centuries ago.
Pass, The
The narrow strait (the Bosphorus analog) separating Entos's eastern boundary from the quarantined Sinter territory -- the only practical crossing between the two, held by fortified towers, naval patrols, and Eirene's Gate.
Piercing
The harder, rarer act of reaching through a Sphere's membrane and drawing something out of another Sphere -- a rupture, not a stretch, that leaves scar tissue behind.
Prisoner, The
Entos's other central, unsolved mystery alongside the Expectant -- an entity or presence of unknown nature, intent, or even reality, whose possible relationship to the Expectant is deliberately never confirmed in-world.
Received Tongue — also called Liturgin
The Liturgy's dead, Scried language, used for writs, rites, and rank-names -- commoners call it High Speech, cathedral-talk, or the Silence-tongue. Rendered on Templar Station terminal screens as "Liturgin."
Relay
A living Myrmidon moving its mind from its current Anima-Core-bearing Shell or machine into another one -- another Myrmidon Shell, a Templar Station terminal, an airship turret, a war-beast left standing on a battlefield. Requires a clear line of sight AND close range -- roughly a mile, the distance at which the destination's detail is visible, not just its silhouette; past that, even with sightline, the Anima Core signature is too faint to lock onto. The vacated Shell goes inert, not dead. Never a way to survive death: if the Shell a mind occupies is destroyed before it Relays out, that mind is gone, same as any other death.
Relic
A physical object wrought from Gifted matter and distributed by the Liturgy to the loyal. Unlike Scried technology, no Relic has ever been manufactured or reverse-engineered, and each has one narrow, consistent function; a Relic goes cold and inert in a disloyal carrier's hands.
Scrying
Pulling two adjacent cell walls (Spheres) tight against each other so a practitioner can look through -- without rupturing or damaging either membrane.
Scrying Rod
The most common focus instrument for a minor (personal) Scryer -- a dense, membrane-adjacent stone set in a grip -- that extends a practitioner's Scrying range and depth, at the cost of picking up whatever the membrane happens to be carrying rather than only what was asked for.
Second Discovery, The
The first Piercing, in Year 331, which ended the Age of Innocence by turning membrane-work from pure observation into taking.
Shell
An android construct built by the Liturgy, carrying an Anima Core. Uninhabited, it is just a Shell -- inert, no different from any other piece of equipment. The moment a mind Relays into it, it is a Myrmidon.
Silence, The
The Liturgy's highest governing body. It never appears publicly, speaks only through Canon-level intermediaries, and its decisions are final and unexplained.
Sinter
The quarantined region where the Bolide struck in 1312 (0 AoV) -- source of the Cavity, the Ichor, and everything that comes out of it. "Sinter" names the place; what emerges from it is called Sinterkin or Sinterspawn.
Sinterkin
What a completed organism from the Sinter Cavity is called once it moves -- coated permanently in black, oil-thick residue, its proportions never fully finished. The rarest and most dangerous free-roaming threat past the quarantine line, because there is no native biology in it to predict or wound familiarly.
Sinterspawn
What a Sinterkin makes of something else. A free-roaming Sinterkin infects or "mates with" any living Entos creature it encounters -- deer, hound, person -- and the result is neither Sinterkin nor the original animal, but a hijacked, warped hybrid. Dangerous, but killing one only stops one corrupted thing; killing the Sinterkin that made it stops the source.
Sphere
What the Liturgy calls each of the countless worlds floating in the Amnios, Entos among them -- a single cell in a vast biological cluster. Scrying looks between two Spheres without damaging either membrane; Piercing tears through one to take something out.
Templar
The Liturgy's own guard order, founded generations before the current Houses existed to argue with the Liturgy -- its "Vatican Guard." An "Infused Templar" has undergone the Gifted Ceremony, which is what opens a sealed Templar Station. Rank tracks the number of Gifted infusions a Templar carries.
Templar Station
A sealed, self-contained structure at Vigil/Templar checkpoints across Entos -- locals describe them as impenetrable geometric eggs. Opens only for an Infused Templar. Holds supplies and a permanent relay terminal carrying Logs between Stations, rendered in the Received Tongue.
thin-membrane site
A location where the boundary between Spheres is thin enough to make Scrying -- and sometimes Piercing -- practical. Such sites carry incursion risk and unease, and a minority of people can perceive things at the boundary that no apparatus captures.
Transmutation — the Gifted Ceremony
The Liturgy's rite of infusing a person with Catalyst-class Gifted matter (chiefly Xerum 525), producing controlled physical ascendance over years of guided doses; framed as sacred and consented to, though the first infusion is only 80% survivable. Soldiers in the field call it simply "the dose" or "being seen."
Verlaine
The central House (France analog) that wrote the Compact and holds the broadest Sphere catalogue in Entos -- the Deep Archive -- governed by catalogue-regency under the First Archivist.
vigil operator — vigil watcher
A long-tenured practitioner stationed for decades at a thin-membrane site to monitor boundary behavior -- distinct from the Vigil holy-warrior order despite the shared word. Some privately describe the membrane as something that "breathes" and responds to them personally, an observation never entered into any formal record.
Vigil, The
The order that holds the quarantine line at the Pass, watching the Sinter border so whatever comes out of it doesn't reach the rest of Entos.
Warrior King
A House's most experienced military commander -- a rank accumulated through decades of campaigns rather than inherited by blood.
Xerum 525 — Red Mercury (alt. House name)
The primary, most-studied Catalyst used in the Gifted Ceremony. Administered correctly by a certified Lector it produces ascendance; administered wrong, or without sanction, it produces catastrophic uncontrolled mutation instead.
Yggdra-wood
A common Entos hardwood, as unremarkable as oak, used for weapon stocks and furniture -- it carries no cosmological significance despite the evocative name.
