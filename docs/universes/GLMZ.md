## Setting

**Year:** 2226. **Location:** GLMZ — Great Lakes Metropolitan Zone (also: *The Glooms*, street
vernacular). Formerly the U.S. Midwest. Population: ~58 million.
Chicago is the transit and commercial hub; the Lake itself is the geographic axis.

The world is 200 years in the future. Coastal infrastructure has collapsed: New York, Boston,
LA, Miami are uninhabitable. The cities that survived were those that could be defended inland
or elevated above flood lines. A few megalopolises survived globally: Shanghai, Tokyo, Rio de
Janeiro, Mexico City, Jakarta. The Pulse connects them.

**Genre:** Literary cyberpunk. Close-third POV. Prose is accessible with sensory detail and
withheld interiority — what characters feel surfaces in action and observed data, not emotional
narration. The city is not cartoonishly dystopian. It is a place where most people have figured
out how to live, and some of them are even happy. The tragedy is structural, not theatrical.

## The Pulse — Global Magnetic Transit

The Pulse is the global magnetic linear accelerator transit network. Vacuum tubes bored through
continental bedrock and laid on ocean floors. Pods move at Mach 5–6 (~6,000 kph).

**Routes:** Chicago → Rotterdam: 43 min. Chicago → Lagos: 51 min. Chicago → Osaka: 97 min
(via Bering Strait arc, the longest route).

**The Blur:** The in-universe name for a Pulse transit — the entire journey between boarding
and arrival. Not a geographic zone. Not jurisdictional. Everything passes so fast it can't be
locked onto: the whole ride is called The Blur. *"She watched Iowa in The Blur."* *"The Blur
thinned"* (arriving). Correct usage: "somewhere in The Blur" = during transit.

**Pulse pod mechanic — spheres + neuretic hallucination:** Physically, passengers ride in
individual sealed spheres (one per person). During transit, neuretics wire together to produce
a shared hallucination: the interior appears as an old-style intercity train from 200 years
earlier — seats, aisles, overhead racks, window feeds from external cameras. Passengers call
their pod **"the car."** Nobody finds this odd. Shared neuretic constructions are routine in
GLMZ. The sphere underneath is never described from inside; characters experience the train.

**GIDS-4 (Gradient Inertial Distribution System):** Why passengers survive 19g acceleration.
The superconducting interior coil array acts on every ferromagnetic/conductive component
simultaneously. Result: ~0.3g subjective during the 47-second Punch. Unaugmented passengers
feel ~0.9g. Coupling vests available at Chicago Station Gate 7 service counter.

**Key Pulse vocabulary:**
- **Slug** — vernacular for a transit pod ("personal slug," "cargo slug")
- **The Punch** — 47-second acceleration phase
- **The Flip** — sequence reversal at geometric midpoint
- **The Catch** — deceleration phase; mirrors The Punch
- **Coasting** — peak Mach 6; perfect stillness
- **Threading** — departure induction loop cycling; 1–3 loops waiting for a flow gap
- **Thrumline** — 8 Hz bone-vibration felt in buildings above Pulse corridors
- **Ferrogate Transit** — franchise operator for GLMZ Pulse segments

**Chicago Station**: 6 international + 14 continental spurs; ~280,000 passengers/day.
Tier 4 sponsorship required for entry. Personal slug cost ≈ 40× a Tier 1 resident's monthly UBC.

Governed by the **Pulse Interconnect Accord (2167)**: 23 corponations + 3 surviving national
governments set pod dimensions, coil specs, pressurization. No single entity controls the
full network — regional franchise holders per segment.

## Neuretics and NSB

**Neuretics** are neural mesh grown into brain tissue — not implanted hardware, grown.
Integration takes 8–24 months and can fail catastrophically (autoimmune rejection = death
without nano bridging). Everyone with neuretics has **neuretic etching**: visible external
trace of mesh at the skull's nerve ports (temples, jaw, behind ears). Grade signals money.

**What each tier actually does:**

| Tier | Access | Capability |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | Common; corpo-subsidized | Basic AR overlay, communications, ID verification |
| Tier 2 | Mid-tier; requires employment contract | Sensory enhancement, memory indexing |
| Tier 3 | High-tier; restricted | Tactical processing, heuristic combat assist |
| Tier 4 / Atlas | Experimental or illegal | Full-stack cognitive expansion; models interlocutor's likely response before they finish speaking |

**AtlasCorps NeoCortex brand tiers (correspond to Tier 1–4 above):**
Standard (12 threads) → Extend (48) → Prime (240) → Atlas (2,400 threads, Φ600/day, Accord registration required)

**The Governor** — thermal/electrochemical survival system in all neuretics (not a performance
cap). Ungoverned Atlas = fatal within 1–3 years.

**Growth-window lock:** Neuretics must be grown during a developmental window; nearly universal
lock after ~age twelve. Rare adult-open cases exist (SS-A21) but are exceptional.

**Cognitive divide:** ~18% global population remains unaugmented. The gap between
unaugmented → Tier 1 → Tier 4 is qualitative, not gradient. Tier 1 subsidized in core
districts via NeoCortex Access Initiative.

**NSB (Neuretic Shell Bonding):** The protocol for occupying a machine body (Shell).
- **Husk** — the biological body sitting dormant while bonded to a Shell
- **Shell** — the machine body being occupied
- **Riding / inject** — entering a Shell; consciousness relocates
- **Eject / return** — leaving a Shell; consciousness returns to the Husk

Vocabulary rules: characters **inject**, **ride**, **ghost**, **haunt**, or **slot** into
Shells. They **eject** or **return** to their Husk. Never "transfer" or "upload" — those
imply copying, not relocation.

## GLMZ Geography

**No official zone numbering.** GLMZ has no official Z1–Z12 designation system. Districts and
neighborhoods use their own names. Do not number them.

**Ferrocement Wave:** A curved flood barrier 60–90 meters tall that rings the lakefront.
Not a wall in the military sense — there are official gates — but crossing it without
authorization is its own kind of statement. Inside is GLMZ; outside is the Territories.

**Key named districts:**
- **The Loop** — Chicago core; prestige corporate towers, arcology rows, banking; highest ArcSec presence
- **Bloom Quarter** — nanotech R&D and biomodification district; legal-gray clinics, corpo labs, body-mod culture; Substrate coverage degraded here (see Nanotechnology)
- **The Yards** — western lakeshore industrial strip; shipyards, heavy manufacturing, Iowan Behemoth staging grounds
- **Gray Zone** — ungoverned buffer between CorpoNation arcologies; where operators, Splicers, and scavs work; not a slum — a free-fire zone of opportunity and danger

**Critical distinction — Tier vs. Altitude:**
- **Tier** = social class and access privilege (1–5); controlled by CorpoNation sponsorship
- **Altitude** = real estate and physical location; no automatic mapping between them
These are independent axes. A Tier 5 resident can choose street level. A Tier 1 resident
cannot afford the float layer. Do not conflate them.

**Altitude = class in practice:**
- **Sky People** — most privileged residents; altitude physically separates money from the city below
- **Float layer** — aeroblocs, aeroquarters (Float Districts), aeropolises (Cloudtowns); wealth lives here
- **The Low** (30–320m) — ungoverned gap: ArcSec patrol ceiling ~50m, MATA radar floor 320m; neither covers this band → gray-market vertical transit, industrial haze, building-wake turbulence
- **Street level** — where most stories happen

**The Spine** — the western lakeshore corridor specifically: Chicago → Milwaukee → Green Bay.

**Green Bay** — full metropolis, low millions population. Not a frontier town.

**Bathysphere** — submerged ruins below the Lake; one sealed black site. There are no
underwater communities. Stories referencing communities under the Lake are incorrect.

**Building scale:** Heights increase toward the Lake. Buildings over one mile high (~1,609m)
are common in the inner districts — made possible by ferrocrete composites. CorpoNation
arcologies are the tallest structures, clearing even supertall residential towers.

**Space elevator** — visible over the southern horizon from GLMZ; operational; most residents
stop noticing after a year.

## Social Structure

**No police.** ArcSec (Arcturus Civil Security) replaced the city police in 2096 — the Blue
Massacre. Law enforcement is now private. ArcSec is simultaneously a civil security monopoly
and a for-profit corporation: it polices where it is contracted to police.
- **ArcSec** — dominant in high-Tier districts and wherever clients pay
- Uncontracted areas: private crew security, CorpoNation guards, or nothing

**UBC (Universal Base Credit)** — minimum subsistence income from CorpoNations to Tier 1–2
residents. Not enough to live on. Enough to make people dependent.

**Tier system (1–5):** Tier 5 = ultra-privileged elite (may have informal sub-tiers for extreme
elites); Tier 4 = high access/privilege; Tier 1 = minimal survival support.
**Sponsorship tokens** — tradeable instruments; professional sponsors monetize their tier status.
Movement between tiers is controlled by CorpoNation sponsorship.

**Block Wars** — territorial conflicts between CorpoNations over Gray Zone buffer space.
Ongoing, low-level, economically motivated. Residents treat them as weather.

**Border Markets** — neutral-ground trading posts at Gray Zone / arcology perimeters.
Goods and people move through them that wouldn't survive either side's legal framework.

## Nanotechnology

GLMZ runs on nano. The entire city is saturated.

**The Substrate** — permanent atmospheric nano-cloud over all GLMZ population centers.
Municipal service: both active functions (atmospheric remediation, structural monitoring,
basic medical response) and passive functions (structural scaffolding, wound closure).
Chicago core: ~98.7% coverage; Gray Zones: 12–18%; Bathysphere: 0%.

**The Ongoing** — continuous molecular warfare inside the Substrate between competing
nano-organisms. Background condition. A **Bloom event** = local Substrate failure. The
Bloom Quarter averages 31% coverage due to the known Substrate seam there.

**Five-class nano system:**
- Class I — atmospheric, municipal permit; common as weather
- Class II — medical; licensed facilities
- Class III — industrial; Permit-7 required, AAMA oversight
- Class IV — military-grade; restricted
- Class V — weaponized; BANNED; triggers military response

**AAMA (Anomalous Activity Monitoring Authority)** governs Classes III+.

**Nano Triumvirate** (three dominant corps):
- **Tessera Industries** — Substrate infrastructure
- **Axiom BioNanics** — medical nano
- **Null Dynamics** — counter-nano

**Neural nano:** Bridges organic brain to neuretics. Without it, integration causes fatal
autoimmune rejection. This is why neuretics can't just be "installed" like hardware.

**Gray market nano:** Feral (escaped Substrate, unpredictable), Black (stolen corpo-grade),
Cooked (artisanal street lab, variable quality).

**Founding incidents:** São Paulo Event (2171) ~40k dead; Chengdu Sequence (2179); Lagos
Bloom (2183) 12 city blocks destroyed → Nano Accords (2187) established international framework.

**Street drug Cascade** — overloads DC-5 nano; euphoric effect ("feeling the war inside").

**Iron Dream** (Lotus pharmaceutical) — purges all nano including protective Substrate.
Fatal in Gray Zones where Substrate provides basic life support.

## Schisms

**The Schism** (historical event) — an AI cabal attempted to bore a fifth-dimensional
corridor; the physics rupture reshaped global infrastructure. The resulting 5D anomalies
persist as localized phenomena throughout GLMZ.

**Five synonyms for a local anomaly** (all in use; register varies):
- *Schism* / *riven* — formal, institutional
- *Rip* / *tear* — street vernacular
- *Interstice* — academic (lowercase "interstice" = a specific anomaly; "the Interstice" = the 5D substrate itself)

**"There is no inside"** — the defining rule of 5D physics. A 5D shape can reach into a
sealed box or skull without breaking any surface. Nothing is truly sealed from schism intrusion.
The colloquial image: **the Gingerbread House** — something that looks like a place, that lures
you in, that is not a place at all. You can be adjacent to a Schism or pass through one; you
cannot be *inside* one.

**Schism classes:**

| Class | Scale | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Micro-rip | Local distortion; non-lethal proximity |
| 2 | Room-scale | Persistent; documented |
| 3 | City-block scale | Evacuation-worthy; Permit-7 required to approach |
| 4 | Neighborhood-scale | Irrecoverable zone |
| 5 | Regional | Theoretically civilization-ending |

**Frequencies:**
- **19 Hz** — resonant frequency of the 5D substrate itself; every active schism membrane rings at this base frequency
- Phenomena *within* 5D manifest at shifted higher frequencies; documented readings from the intelligences at 35th/Halsted: **19.415 Hz** and **19.771 Hz**
- **72–74 Hz** — the **Counter** (the second intelligence at 35th/Halsted)

**The two intelligences at 35th/Halsted:**
- **The Lure** — a collector; draws things toward Schism boundaries; motives unknown; resonates near the 19 Hz 5D base (documented: 19.415 Hz and 19.771 Hz)
- **The Counter** — a limiter; "this far and no further"; documented resisting Schism expansion; 72–74 Hz signature

**Micro-schisms** — floating pebble clusters (~2 inches) on street corners; treated like
weather by residents; dangerous to neuretics-sensitive individuals.

**Word rule:** "bleed" is RESERVED for neuretics data leakage. Never use "bleed" for schism
phenomena. Use "rift," "seep," "intrusion," etc.

## QUANTA Currency

**QUANTA (Φ):** An allotment of computational power, divided among every citizen of Earth.
Each person's allotment can be saved, transferred to others (serving as currency), or spent
directly on computation. Wealth is accumulated QUANTA — those who cannot save spend their
allotment as fast as it issues. The symbol Φ is NEVER the Greek letter phi — it is always
the QUANTA currency symbol. Write: *"She owes 200Φ."*

## Aerostatic Architecture

**ANGEL (Aerogel Null Globe Evacuated Lifter):** The core tech behind the float layer.
Visual: translucent milky sphere, ghost-lamp glow; a vacuum micropump disc at the north pole
maintains the interior vacuum. Modular by design — personal/Axis Rig units run 8–20cm;
platform-scale arrays stack into much larger lift systems. The acronym ANGEL is locked — do not vary it.

**Aerobloc** — a city block built on vacuum-cell (ANGEL) buoyancy for voluntary elevation;
can be moored or free-floating.

**Mooring doctrine:** Aeroblocs anchor to arcologies via CNT-steel **Black Wire** (carbon
nanotube cable). The corpo holding the arcology owns the hold. "Who holds your tether" is
a live political question — cut wire = a free-floating bloc, which is either freedom or
a fall depending on wind.

**ANGEL sabotage = ghost ball cracking** — controlled failure over hours or days, not a
sudden drop. The heartbeat (aggregate hum of healthy micropump array) goes silent before
the descent begins. Silence = danger.

**Key aerostatic slang:**
- *Vac-skin* — aerogel wealth signaling; high-end exteriors
- *Ghost glass eyes* — insulated by altitude; can't see the street below
- *Webwalkers* — float maintenance workers; live between the mooring cables
- *The Hold* — mooring system collectively

**Air Tax** — fee charged for altitude above street level; collected by arcology owners or
Gray Zone controllers depending on location.

## Automata and Synthetic Life

**Automata (Iowan Behemoths and similar)** are autonomous machines. NOT alive. NOT synthetic
persons. No personhood, no consciousness, no rights. Do not write them experiencing emotion,
fear, or identity. An Automaton being destroyed is equipment loss.

**E.L.F. (Emergent Life Form)** — genuinely alive, emerged from code-fragment complexity
within the Substrate. Sentience and capabilities vary by emergence — some are pre-sentient
(instinct-driven, no language), others exhibit complex behavior and self-direction. E.L.F.s
are alive but hold no legal personhood; the 2187 Cognitive Rights Expansion Act addressed AI
personhood, not E.L.F.s. Always write with periods: *E.L.F.*, never "ELF." Gloss on first
mention in any story. Emerged primarily 2210s onward.

**Tier 3 synthetic persons** — fewer than a dozen recognized since 2201. Require server-
cluster-scale compute (Bekenstein/Bremermann constraints). No current story character qualifies.
Form has no bearing on personhood: a humanoid android = machine; a box on wheels with genuine
desires = alive. The test is interior life, not exterior shape.

## Factions

### CorpoNations

Corporations that have subsumed national functions: security, infrastructure, housing, legal
systems. States with share prices. Two dominant players in GLMZ:

- **Axiom** — the largest and most politically dominant corponation; controls the Chicago core
  and surrounding prestige districts; research into consciousness transfer is an open secret
- **Arcturus** — parent of ArcSec; the civil security monopoly; simultaneously law enforcement
  agency and for-profit corporation since the Blue Massacre (2096)

CorpoNation names are written with conjoined capitals on first mention, abbreviation after.

### Lotus Syndicate

Blood-purity organization; the dominant E/SE Asian criminal organization in GLMZ; deep historical
roots (early 17th-century Yakuza lineage).

**Bloodlines:** East and Southeast Asian ancestry — Chinese, Japanese, Korean (North + South),
Taiwanese, Vietnamese, Cambodian, Lao, Thai, Burmese, Mongolian, maritime SE Asian. Members
carry pure East Asian names. You are BORN Lotus or never belong. Non-bloodline operators
("dogs") are used for dirty work and never seated at council.

**Structure:** Stems (council) + Root (half-mythical senior leadership) + Thorns (enforcers).

**Territory:** Centered in Pilsen, Bridgeport, Back of the Yards (Chicago Gray Zone).

**Character:** Long-view organization — "geology, not weather" vs. Gray Zone warlord churn.
Honor code invoked only when convenient.

**Clean Covenant** — official Lotus doctrine rejecting nano use (purity doctrine). The inner
circle secretly uses military-grade nano. This hypocrisy is known by street-level operators
and never acknowledged by the organization.

## World Geography (Beyond GLMZ)

**Five surviving megalopolises:** Shanghai, Tokyo, Rio de Janeiro, Mexico City, Jakarta.

**Amish** — last significant Christian sect; ~40,000 people; Indiana/Ohio/Pennsylvania.
Rumspringa-style walkabout at ~age 16; ~85–90% return. Survived the False Prophet collapse
(2082–91). Do not write them as enemies or villains.

**Australia** — sealed; described as "absorbed into the Dreaming." No Pulse connection,
no diplomatic contact, no explanation. LOCKED — do not speculate or explain.

**Korea** — reunified 2153; extreme isolationism; ~99% genetic homogeneity; "the Quiet Country."
Not hostile, just closed.

**Russia** — collapsed and balkanized; does not exist as a unified state.

**Rome** — Class 4–5 schism zone; LOCKED. Do not set stories in Rome.

**Tequenica** — third schism intelligence (after the Lure and Counter at 35th/Halsted);
Tierra del Fuego; maintains a protective inward-pointing perimeter; woven into Yahgan
cosmology. Treat as canonical but unreachable for story purposes.

## Technology Canon

**The Glass (BTL — Better Than Life)** — direct-feed neuretic entertainment; profoundly
addictive; users called Glass-heads by street residents.

**The Volume** — three-dimensional broadcast display; the standard media format.
Flat screens are antique; the Volume is ambient and spatial.

**Nano gutter service** — has stopped in neglected districts. Iridescent residue film on
neglected curbs and walls. Clean curbs = corpo-adjacent class signal.

**No anti-gravity. No zero-point energy.** Aerostatic lift (vacuum cells/ANGEL) is the only
non-propulsive altitude technology. Any reference to "anti-grav" in prose or entity
descriptions is an error.

## Psionics (The Read)

**The Read** — genuine psionic ability; emerged early-to-mid 2100s. Real, present throughout
the universe, and a recurring story element across multiple GLMZ strands. Readers perceive
emotional state, surface cognition, and sometimes deeper intention from other people.

People distrust Psionics (Readers) and AIs. Anti-psionic sentiment is normalized in 2226:
employment discrimination, social stigma, and periodic crackdowns. The psionics did not cause
the Schisms — that detail has never mattered to the people doing the discriminating.

## Prose Ground Rules (GLMZ-specific)

- **Close-third POV** — narrator rides inside the POV character's sensory frame; no omniscient
  overview; no access to other characters' interiority
- **Body before mind** — physical sensation registers before emotional interpretation; reader
  infers emotion from body
- **Cost is visible** — augmentation, transit, housing, food all have prices; cost is world-building
- **No universal-truth wry lines** — narrator does not step back to offer ironic commentary
- **Metaphors survive literal scrutiny** — if a metaphor describes the moon as bleeding, there
  must be a way the moon could plausibly bleed in this world
- **CorpoNation names are proper nouns** — conjoined-capital on first use, abbreviation after
- **"bleed" = neuretics data leakage only** — never schism phenomena
- **Shared neuretic spaces are mundane** — characters don't marvel at them; they're Tuesday
- **Ubiquitous Diaspora** — mixed heritage is default; no Anglo default; no duplicate first/last
  names; double-barrel surnames are rare
