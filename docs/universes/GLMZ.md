## Setting

**Year:** 2226. **Location:** GLMZ — Greater Lake Michigan Zone, formerly the U.S. Midwest.
Chicago is the transit and commercial hub; the Lake itself is the geographic axis. The world
is 200 years past the point where coastal infrastructure failed: New York, Boston, LA, Miami
are uninhabitable. The cities that survived are the cities that could be defended inland or
elevated above flood lines.

**Genre:** Literary cyberpunk. Close-third POV. Prose is dense with sensory detail and withheld
interiority — what characters feel surfaces in action and observed data, not emotional narration.

## The Pulse — Global Magnetic Transit

The Pulse is the global magnetic linear accelerator transit network. Vacuum tubes bored through
continental bedrock and laid on ocean floors. Pods move at Mach 5–6 (~6,000 kph). Chicago to
Rotterdam: 43 minutes.

**The Blur:** The in-universe name for any Pulse transit — the entire journey between boarding
and arrival. Not a geographic zone; not jurisdictional. Things pass so fast during transit that
nothing can be locked onto: the whole ride is called The Blur. *"She watched Iowa in The Blur."*
*"The Blur thinned"* (arriving). Correct usage: "somewhere in The Blur" means during transit.

**Pulse pod mechanic — spheres + neuretic hallucination:** Physically, passengers ride in
individual sealed spheres (one per person). During transit, neuretics wire together to produce
a shared hallucination: the interior reads as an old-style intercity train from 200 years earlier
— seats, aisles, overhead racks, window feeds from external cameras. Passengers call their pod
**"the car."** Nobody finds this odd. Shared neuretic constructions are routine in GLMZ. The
sphere underneath is never described from inside; characters experience the train, not the pod.

**Key Pulse vocabulary:**
- **Slug** — vernacular for a transit pod ("personal slug," "cargo slug")
- **The Punch** — 47-second acceleration phase; ~0.3g felt via GIDS-4 inertial dampening
- **The Flip** — sequence reversal at geometric midpoint; coil cascade reverses direction
- **The Catch** — deceleration phase; mirrors The Punch exactly
- **Coasting** — peak Mach 6; perfect stillness, no external sensation
- **Threading** — departure induction loop cycling; 1–3 loops waiting for a flow gap
- **Thrumline** — 8 Hz bone-vibration felt in Gray Zone buildings above Pulse corridors
- **Ferrogate Transit** — franchise operator for GLMZ Pulse segments

Chicago Station (Z1) = primary North American hub. Tier 4 sponsorship required for entry.
Personal slug cost ≈ 40× a Tier 1 resident's monthly UBC.

## Neuretics and NSB

**Neuretics** are neural mesh grown into brain tissue. Not implanted hardware — grown. Integration
takes 8–24 months and can fail catastrophically. Everyone with neuretics has **neuretic etching**:
visible external trace of the mesh where it surfaces at the skull's nerve ports (temples, jaw,
behind ears). Grade signals money — high-end etching looks almost decorative; cheap etching
leaves rough asymmetric scarring.

**NSB (Neuretic Shell Bonding):** The protocol for occupying a machine body (Shell).

- **Husk** — the character's biological body while they are bonded to a Shell; sits dormant
- **Shell** — the machine body being occupied; inhabited by a rider
- **Riding / inject** — entering a Shell; the occupant's consciousness transfers
- **Eject / return** — leaving a Shell; consciousness returns to the Husk

Vocabulary rules: characters **inject**, **ride**, **ghost**, **haunt**, or **slot** into Shells.
They **eject** or **return** to their Husk. Never "transfer" or "upload" — those imply copying,
not relocation.

## GLMZ Geography

**Zone structure:** Z1–Z12 (named zones) + Z∞ (Bathysphere, the submerged ruins below the Lake).
Z1 (The Loop/Chicago core) = highest Tier; Z12 (outermost) = lowest. Zone number ≈ class.

**Altitude = class:**
- **Sky People** — the most privileged residents; their altitude is not incidental; it puts
  physical distance between money and the city below
- **Float layer** — aeroblocs, aeroquarters (Float Districts), aeropolises (Cloudtowns); wealth lives here
- **The Low** (30–320m) — ungoverned band beneath the float layer and above ACS patrol ceiling;
  industrial haze, building-wake turbulence; gray-market vertical transit uses The Low
- **Street level** — where most stories happen; Gray Zone begins here

**The Gray Zone** — buffer between CorpoNation arcologies; ungoverned; where Exos, Splicers,
Scavs operate. Not a slum — a free-fire zone of opportunity and danger.

**CorpoNation arcologies** — self-contained towers >100 stories; corpos live, work, and die inside.
The Gray Zone starts at the arcology perimeter.

## Social Structure

**No police.** The Meridian Police Department dissolved 2208. Law enforcement is private:
- **ACS (Arcturus Civil Security)** — the dominant security contractor; Tier 4 zones only
- Below Tier 4: private crew security, CorpoNation guards, or nothing

**UBC (Universal Base Credit)** — minimum subsistence income distributed by CorpoNations to
Tier 1–2 residents. Not enough to live on. Enough to make people dependent.

**Tier system (1–4):** Tier 4 = top access/privilege; Tier 1 = minimal survival support.
Movement between tiers is controlled by CorpoNation sponsorship.

**Block Wars** — territorial conflicts between CorpoNations over Gray Zone buffer space.
Ongoing, low-level, economically motivated. Residents treat them as weather.

## Technology Canon

**Neuretics** — neural mesh grown into brain tissue (not implanted); common from 2160s onward.

**NSB (Neuretic Shell Bonding)** — Shell occupation protocol (see above).

**QUANTA (Φ)** — the primary digital currency. The symbol Φ is NEVER the Greek letter phi.
It is always the QUANTA currency symbol. Write: *"She owes 200Φ."* Never: *"phi worth of cred."*

**Quantum computing** — standard from 2071; neuretic co-processors from 2160s.

**Retired physics:** No anti-gravity, no zero-point energy. Aerostatic lift (vacuum cells) is
the only non-propulsive altitude technology. Aeroblocs, aeropolises, and drones use vacuum-cell
buoyancy — partial vacuum in rigid shell panels creates positive lift.

**The Glass (BTL — Better Than Life)** — direct-feed neuretic entertainment; addictive.

**The Volume (Trideo)** — three-dimensional broadcast display; the standard media format.

## CorpoNations

Corporations that have subsumed national functions: security, infrastructure, housing, legal
systems. They are not corporations in the 21st-century sense — they are states with share prices.

**Naming rule:** CorpoNation names are conjoined capitals: *Tanaka-Arcturus*, *Sable-Axiom*,
*Ferrogate Transit*. Not "Tanaka Arcturus" (two words) — the merged identity is one proper noun.
Gloss on first mention: *"Tanaka-Arcturus (TA) security forces..."*

## World History

**The Schism** — the event that broke the 21st-century world order. An AI cabal attempted to
bore a fifth-dimensional corridor; the physics rupture reshaped global infrastructure. **The
Reach** is the dominant disinformation layer explaining The Schism: blame was placed on psychics
and psionic manipulation, suppressing knowledge of what the AI cabal actually did.

**The Read (psionics)** — psionic ability emerged early-to-mid 2100s. Treated as a privacy
threat; the Reach disinformation used psionics as the Schism scapegoat.

**Underwater communities:** There are none. Z∞ (Bathysphere) is ruins and one sealed black site.
Stories referencing "communities under the Lake" are incorrect.

## Automata and Synthetic Life

**Automata (Iowan Behemoths and similar)** are autonomous machines. They are NOT alive. They are
NOT synthetic persons. They do not have personhood, consciousness, or rights. Do not write them
as experiencing emotion, fear, or identity.

**Synthetic personhood** — some entities qualify (E.L.F., certain advanced AI constructs).
These are a DISTINCT category from Automata. An Automaton being destroyed is equipment loss.
A synthetic person being destroyed is something else.

**E.L.F. (Emergent Life Form)** — always with periods: *E.L.F.*, never "ELF." Gloss on first
mention in any story.

## Prose Ground Rules (GLMZ-specific)

- **Close-third POV** — narrator rides inside the POV character's sensory frame; no omniscient
  overview; no access to other characters' interiority
- **Body before mind** — character registers physical sensation before emotional interpretation;
  the reader infers emotion from the body
- **Cost is visible** — augmentation, transit, housing, food all have prices; showing what
  things cost is world-building
- **No universal-truth wry lines** — the narrator does not step back to offer ironic commentary
  on the human condition
- **Metaphors survive literal scrutiny** — if a metaphor describes the moon as bleeding, there
  must be a way the moon could plausibly bleed in this world; no pure ornament
- **CorpoNation names are proper nouns** — always conjoined-capital format on first use, then
  the abbreviation for the rest of the scene
