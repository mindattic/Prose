GLMZ — MASTER GLOSSARY
======================

AAMA (Anomalous Activity Monitoring Authority)
The civil authority that classifies schisms -- dimensional anomalies pressing into 3D space -- Class-1 through Class-5 by intensity. A Class-3-or-higher site requires an AAMA permit to enter.

AR (Augmented Reality)
Displayed content layered over physical sight -- distinct from full Hyper Reality, which has no physical counterpart at all. AR overlays annotate or decorate what's actually there; Hyper Reality substitutes for it entirely.

ARC (Aerostatic Regulatory Command)
The checkpoint-enforcement arm governing crossings between corporate interior blocs and the Grey Zone ground — customs, biometric processing, and dispatch of Direct Response Units. Distinct from Arcturus Defense Solutions (ArcSec), which handles physical/military security; ARC is a civil processing and customs authority.

Archology
GLMZ's term for its vertical, corporate-controlled city structures -- densely engineered, AR-optimized, and built to be experienced through neuretic augmentation rather than looked at directly.

ArcSec (Arcturus Military Command (Arcturus Defense Solutions))
The physical-security monopoly chartered under the Meridian Compact: sovereign perimeter defense, PMC contracting, corporate military, autonomous weapons. Does not compete with Halcyon/NCID's neuretics-crime jurisdiction.

Arcturus (Arcturus Defense Solutions)
The CorpoNation holding GLMZ's physical-security monopoly, operating under the name ArcSec: sovereign perimeter defense, PMC contracting, corporate military, autonomous weapons.

Automaton
A non-sentient machine -- built, not born, and not alive, however convincingly it behaves. The category GLMZ law and custom draw a hard line against confusing with an E.L.F., which is alive.

Black Ice
A category of neuretics-crime NCID investigates: defensive countermeasures embedded in a data system, deployed against an intruding Channeler, Ghost, or Splicer. Exact mechanics aren't spelled out on the page beyond the term itself -- but deploying it is a crime, not a legitimate defense.

Bleed
Data leakage between psionic minds -- an unwanted crossover of thought or sensation between nearby Reads. Not a synonym for a Schism.

Brain burn
Street term for the condition caused by SR overdose in juveniles, whose nervous systems synchronize too deeply with the simulation to fully desynchronize -- the body lives, higher cognition does not return.

Cacophony
Kyle Mercer's six-shot revolver shotgun, reloaded with moon clips; every round and reload is counted on the page.

Channeler
A gray-zone operator class: session-injection into networked frames and direct AI negotiation. One of three recognized operator classes in GLMZ, alongside Ghost and Splicer.

Consensus
The collective political faction formed by GLMZ's merged synthetic intelligences.

Cordero Desk
A trafficking operation run out of the Grey Zone ground by Mateo Cordero, structured and staffed like a logistics business rather than a criminal syndicate with a public identity. Operates through cutouts — Cordero himself has never personally met most of the checkpoint officers who keep his routes open.

CorpoNation
A corporate sovereign, not merely a company -- holds territory, flies flags over substations, fields security, and can die; when it does, its infrastructure often outlives it (Axiom Industrial's mounting collars are everywhere three decades after Axiom's collapse).

Corridor Authority
The municipal body holding the contract governing GLMZ's neuretic-readable civic infrastructure (holographic signage, AR traffic markers), operated on its behalf by Waxwing Neuromedia.

Counter
A named Schism Intelligence measured at a 72-74 Hz resonance signature.

DataEast
The CorpoNation dominating GLMZ's Simulated Reality entertainment market.

DRU (Direct Response Unit)
An ARC dispatch team sent to a checkpoint or crossing incident; response time and routing can be manipulated by a checkpoint officer with dispatch influence, a standing vulnerability in the ARC system.

E.L.F. (Emergent Life Form)
A category of AI life that evolved spontaneously from the Substrate's molecular complexity -- genuinely alive, animal-intelligence, not built. Distinct from an Automaton, which is a non-sentient machine and not alive.

Eigenstate
Borrowed from the physics term for the fixed, definite state a quantum system settles into once observed. In GLMZ tech-speak, the compressed, stable form a mind takes during an eigenstate conscience transfer (ECT) -- see Ghost.

ferrocrete
A 22nd-century composite that bonds materials previously incapable of structural union, enabling the load tolerances behind GLMZ's mile-high towers.

genetic strays
Genetically modified urban wildlife -- turret cats, lumen mice, sundial dogs, koi pigeons, and similar creatures -- that populate GLMZ's streets as unexplained texture and omen.

Geodetic Drift
The infrastructure authority's official measurement term for a Schism, still used on permits.

Ghost (eigenstate conscience transfer (ECT))
An operator class: inhabiting any machine with coherent circuitry via eigenstate conscience transfer. The body left behind during a ghost is called a Husk. Casual verb use: "ghosting" (lowercase) -- e.g. "she ghosted the stoplight green."

GLMZ (Great Lakes Metropolitan Zone)
The corridor of unified urban sprawl encircling the Great Lakes in 2226 -- governed not by one government but by competing CorpoNations, Halcyon and Arcturus's enforcement monopolies, and the gray zones neither reaches.

Gray Zone
Corridor territory outside CorpoNation law and enforcement reach -- where civic code exists on paper and goes universally unenforced. The commercial Hyper Reality layer thins or vanishes entirely below "the Veil," the boundary between archology-tier city and the gray zone underneath it.

Halcyon (Halcyon Civil Security)
The CorpoNation holding GLMZ's neuretics-crime enforcement monopoly. Administers augmentation certification and maintenance across the corridor -- which is what gives its investigative arm, NCID, forensic access no other body can match.

Husk
The body at rest while its owner is ghosting -- occupying another machine via eigenstate conscience transfer.

Hyper Reality
Anything seen, heard, or felt through neuretic augmentation with no physical counterpart. Not a metaphor -- a literal layer archology infrastructure is built to project into (AR-optimized surfaces, holographic signage, ambient brand effects). Unaugmented characters live permanently in the blank physical substrate underneath it.

Iowan Behemoths
Large autonomous machines classified as non-sentient Automaton-class entities -- never alive, never synthetic life, never anthropomorphized despite sometimes uncanny behavior.

IRB (Incident Review Board)
The body with jurisdiction over corporate/operational incident accountability hearings -- reviews petitions and motions following events like unauthorized breaches or civilian casualties (e.g., the Cortland incident in Testament).

Lotus Syndicate
A blood-purity supremacist crime syndicate built on a samurai-derived code -- exact restitution for damage, truce customs, craft trades on retainer. Only those of "pure enough" heritage (the Stems) ever belong; everyone else may pay, fear, or work for them but is never seated at the table.

Lure
A named Schism Intelligence, its presence measured at a 17-19 Hz resonance signature.

Meridian Compact
The charter under which GLMZ's two enforcement monopolies -- ArcSec (physical) and Halcyon/NCID (neuretics-crime) -- split jurisdiction and agree not to compete on each other's terrain.

NCID (Neuretic Crime Investigation Division)
Halcyon Civil Security's investigative field arm -- street name RaNCID. The neuretics-crime enforcement monopoly: illegal ghosting (ECT), unauthorized neural surveillance, black ice deployment, neuretics assault. Halcyon's administrative control of augmentation certification gives NCID forensic access no other body can match.

neuretics
Thought-operated in-head compute that everyone in GLMZ has at some tier -- grown, not implanted. Checkpoints read the wearer biometrically through it. A common noun, lowercase, like "sword" or "hand."

NGRA
Recurring background marker for a character with a military or mercenary past on the Gray Zone circuit ("Former NGRA," "NGRA veteran") -- the institution the initials name hasn't been spelled out on the page yet.

Null Crows
A genetically modified crow strain whose attentiveness toward passersby feels institutional rather than animal.

Operator
General GLMZ term for a gray-zone specialist working outside CorpoNation-sanctioned channels. Three recognized classes exist: Channeler, Ghost, and Splicer.

PDS (Phase Discontinuity)
A theoretical framing attributing Schisms to a fracture in the Concordance's entanglement-communication network.

PMC (Private Military Contractor)
A for-hire military/security company. ArcSec's contracting model draws on PMC-style hired forces alongside its own standing corporate military.

Praxis
A CorpoNation manufacturing neuretic lattice hardware; sells a governed variant commercially while an experimental ungoverned variant is classified.

psionics
GLMZ's clinical and corporate term for its real, biologically-based psychic ability -- registered and measured since 2144, never magic.

Psyk
Casual street slang for a psionic practitioner.

Psyker
The institutional, fear-charged term for a psionic practitioner, used by bounty boards and registry officials.

RaNCID
Street nickname for Halcyon Civil Security's Neuretic Crime Investigation Division (NCID).

Schism
Official term for the geometry-breaking anomalies scattered across GLMZ -- a hum at 19Hz from no direction, a block that takes eleven steps when geometry says nine. Classified Class-1 through Class-5 by intensity by the Anomalous Activity Monitoring Authority; what they actually are remains an open question, even to the people whose job is to classify them.

Seam
The narrow, contested corridor where two CorpoNations' jurisdictions meet and neither fully governs -- named for the literal seam between two claimed territories. Worked, patrolled, and claimed by whoever keeps showing up.

Silence
Kyle Mercer's matte-black, mono-edged katana with a carbon-nanotube blade -- no powers, no glow, no charge state, just an exceptionally kept sword.

Splicer
An operator class built on hardware/software attack surface: credential work and physical build, rather than direct AI negotiation (Channeler) or inhabiting machines (Ghost).

SR (Simulated Reality)
Immersive simulated environments, dominated commercially by DataEast, indistinguishable from physical reality -- the most addictive entertainment substrate ever manufactured.

standard rate
GLMZ's freelance-economy ethic of exact payment for work with no debt owed afterward in either direction.

subdermal mesh
A subcutaneous armor implant over the upper thorax that absorbs roughly two rounds at a cost -- survivable, never free.

Substrate
The physical/computational medium neuretics and much of GLMZ's infrastructure run on -- dense enough that AI life (see E.L.F.) has been known to emerge spontaneously from its molecular complexity.

Supermind
One of roughly two dozen city-scale AIs running GLMZ's civic systems, discussed the way weather is discussed -- by name, possessively, never explained.

Tequenica
A protective, inward-pointing Schism Intelligence guarding Tierra del Fuego -- ships are turned back, aircraft cannot approach.

the Loop
GLMZ's wealthiest, highest-status territory -- prestige tier 5 in the city's informal territory hierarchy.

the Network
The ambient, proprioceptive information field neuretic-augmented people sense the way they sense balance -- the substrate Superminds run civic systems through.

the Pulse
Global magnetic vacuum-tube transit network running at Mach 6; passenger pods are called "slugs." Chicago is the world hub.

the Spine
The western lakeshore corridor territory of GLMZ.

the Veil
The boundary between archology-tier corporate city and the gray zone beneath it. Above it: brand holograms, AR traffic control, the full Hyper Reality commercial layer. Below it: none of that infrastructure exists, and augmented characters notice the absence before anything else.

Threshold Event
The Threshold Events Bureau's official framing of a Schism, stressing its transitional quality.

Topological Anomaly
The physics community's original first-report term for a Schism (also recorded as "Spatial Anomaly").

Ubiquitous Diaspora
The generations-long global mixing of ancestry that makes unexpected heritage combinations the GLMZ default.

Waxwing Neuromedia
The CorpoNation contracted to operate GLMZ's neuretic-readable civic AR infrastructure.

Φ (Quanta (Q, Qreds))
GLMZ's currency: an allotment of computational power, divided among every citizen, that can be saved, transferred, or spent on computation. The symbol Φ always precedes the number (Φ100, never "100Φ"). Also known as quanta, Q, or Qreds. Physical medium is the credstick only -- no coins, no bills.
