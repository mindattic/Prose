# HORROR — Universal World Facts {#SS-HORROR-WORLD}

> Injected into every beat generation prompt for any HORROR-universe book, per the DCM
> four-layer static hierarchy (Layer 2 — Universe). Craft doctrine (how to write it) lives in
> `docs/HORROR.md`; this file is what to hold true about the world itself.

## 1. Setting {#SS-HORROR-WORLD-1}

HORROR books are set in the real world (contemporary unless a specific book states otherwise) —
no invented geography, currency, technology, or political structure. Real-world places, real
technology, real subcultures. The uncanny element is the single deliberate transgression against
that otherwise-real world; everything else should be as accurate as research allows.

## 2. No Shared Continuity {#SS-HORROR-WORLD-2}

Unlike GLMZ and SCRY, HORROR books do not share a timeline, cast, or antagonist by default. A
future HORROR book may reuse a technique or an unresolved thread from another HORROR book only
by explicit authorial decision, never by ambient assumption.

## 3. The Antagonist Is Never Named or Explained {#SS-HORROR-WORLD-3}

No HORROR book gives its uncanny force a species name, an origin myth, or a stated set of rules
presented as objective truth. Any "rules" that appear on the page are the protagonist's working
hypothesis, arrived at through direct experience, and may be incomplete or wrong. See
`docs/HORROR.md` §1.

## 4. Death, Injury, and Consequence {#SS-HORROR-WORLD-4}

No blanket rule that death is permanent or reversible in this universe (each book decides its own
stakes) — but there is no supernatural resurrection or explained-away survival used as a
narrative safety net. If a character appears to survive something that shouldn't be survivable,
that ambiguity is itself left unresolved, never quietly explained in a later beat.
