Read it clean before they moved her. Not Celeste. Same cold as Celeste's room - it's one thing, many faces.

Outside, the buzzer panel was quiet. The city ran its evening load through the field as background weather, the way it always did. Analog could. Analog was already on the street, generating no signal, which tonight was the only qualification that counted. The room would be found.
