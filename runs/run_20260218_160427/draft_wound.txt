**Scene: The Clinic**

The clinic buzzes with fluorescent light, sharp against the concrete walls. The hum clings, a static cling of desperation. The sterile scent of antiseptic mixes with sweat. A ragged youth darts down the hall, a stolen augment core pulsing in his hand. You can see desperation in the way his fingers tremble.

You hear the soft click of life-support monitors echoing through the waiting room, rhythmic and mocking in their reliability. How many lives depend on this place? You grip the hilt of your katana, its cool metal grounding you. The weight of the blade is familiar, both a comfort and a reminder of what you've become.

“Stop!” Your voice cuts through the sterile air, raw and cracked like the walls. The kid glances back, eyes wide and frantic. They dart over your shoulder as he speeds up. The thrum of the augment core feeds his fear, a siren's call in a sea of silence.

He skids around the corner, feet pounding against linoleum. You chase him, the katana a whisper against your hip, bloodied tales of past decisions echoing in your mind. The room feels too small, the air thick with shattered hope. 

Wound-Line 1: *You think you’re the hero, but heroes don’t chase down children.*

The boy is barely fifteen, maybe younger. His eyes flicker with madness; he doesn’t know what he’s doing. None of them do. The old worries of right and wrong swirl, a toxic mix in your gut. You push the thoughts down, focus on the kill. 

“Please, I just need it, man,” he whines, the tremor in his voice breaking like glass. You can smell his fear, metallic and sharp. He knows he’s cornered, knows he’s about to fight or fold.

He presses a button on his wrist, and your blood runs cold. A dead-man-switch. You can see the panic seep into his skin. “You think I won’t do it?” he hisses, a challenged promise hiding beneath the surface. 

Wound-Line 2: *You’re alive because you’ve learned to survive. Nobody cares how you feel.*

You tighten your grip on the katana, your pulse steadying against the chaos. “You have to give it back.” Words slide out, but they feel hollow in your throat. You can feel the knife-edge of choice waiting.

“Don’t come any closer!” His voice cracks, a boy caught in the throes of survival. “I’ll shut them off, I swear!” He gestures wildly toward the treatment rooms, the steady beep of life-support machines humming like a ghost in the background. The scent of antiseptic threatens to choke you.

Wound-Line 3: *You act like you care, but you’d trade a hundred lives for your pride.*

He’s young, just a kid. What's his life worth against the shadows you harbor? You’ve killed before, but they all had it coming. This is different. Your heart goes cold. A flash of vulnerability grips you, and you push it away. 

“Just let them go!” You take a step forward, the sound echoing louder than his cries. The steel of the katana feels alive, hungry. 

His eyes glisten with desperation. “You think you can save them? I have the power!” 

The machines behind him whir, their beeping a pulse of life, a reminder of the fragility around you. You glance at the door, the rows of patients relying on the very technology he threatens to disable. 

Wound-Line 4: *You believe in honor, but honor is just another way to avoid the truth.*

The weight of the blade seems to shift in your hands. You could slice through him in a heartbeat, end this. Blood would spill, but the patients— They’d be fine. Or would they? Each choice drags your intention into the murky depths. Your katana is cold, but the choice is worse.

Tension thickens around you, and the world narrows. “You won’t do it!” he screams, a challenge drenched in fear. “You’re a coward!” 

Coward. The word strikes deep. You’ve been called worse, faced worse. You’ve felt the pain of others in your choices. 

Wound-Line 5: *You can’t drown out the guilt; it’s already part of you.*

“I don’t want to hurt you.” Your words drop like stones, heavy with truth. The scent of antiseptic stings your nose, a reminder of tender bodies tethered to machines. You realize the bitter irony; you’re the real thief here. You came to take back what’s yours. 

“I’m not trying to be a hero!” His face crumples, revealing a layer of innocence beneath the bravado. The boy who stole from you is just as broken. “You don’t understand!” 

Wound-Line 6: *You’d take a child's life to save your own. That makes you less than human.*

Without thinking, you cut the signal line with a precise swipe of the katana, the edge biting through the metal with a clean hum. The boy slumps to the ground, convulsing in pain as he screams. 

Lights flicker. Beeping monitors begin to wail, their life signals shattering the silence. Through the door, you catch glimpses of the patients, their faces pale, terrified. You didn’t save. You damned them instead.

Wound-Line 7: *You think you’ve won, but you’ve just traded one horror for another.*

In the silence that follows, the boy’s cries fill the room, a cruel reminder that innocence is a currency spent too easily. You’ve severed wires that bind him to life, but you know the cost. You feel it knotting around your lungs. 

His face twists in agony, a reflection of your own. You’ve made a choice, irreversible and irrevocable. 

Standing among the chaos, you feel the weight of your blade sinking heavy into your soul. 

The air buzzes with futility.
