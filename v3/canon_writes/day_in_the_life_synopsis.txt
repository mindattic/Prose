Continuous from Bearing Teeth. Kyle walks back to his motorcycle through GLMZ rain — the tower silhouettes leaning over the Gray Zone like spectators that lost interest forty minutes ago, alleys deeper than they look from the street, the occasional VTOL crossing the canyon overhead carrying someone who has never had to walk. The motorcycle is where he left it. He takes the back roads up to West Town. Pixel hears the bike before he's parked it; her door is already open by the time he reaches the landing across from 2F. She sits him at her kitchen table with his coat off and his shirt up; she pulls three slugs out of his back with the long forceps she has gotten very good at using, talks the whole time about absolutely nothing in particular — the upstairs neighbor's bird, the new noodle stand on Damen that she thinks Mrs. Chen should be worried about, a song she likes — and hands him a cup of something hot when she's done. He retires to 2F, falls asleep in his clothes clutching Silence. Wakes the next morning to the rain still falling. The world has not paused for him to recover. Today is going to be its own day.
