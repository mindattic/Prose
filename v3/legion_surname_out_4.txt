Strip multi-barreled hyphenated last names down to a single, punchy barrel. The best approach is to scan all barrels and select the one that is shortest, has the strongest consonant cluster, or is most culturally evocative — rather than defaulting mechanically to first or last. For cyberpunk aesthetics, favor names that are 1-2 syllables with hard consonants (e.g., Kato, Ryck, Voss). This is a reversible creative choice until production materials are locked.
