# SPRW Logic & Continuity Audit — "The Number That Works" (`the-number-that-works-019ed367`)

**Audit date:** 2026-07-03 | **Scope:** 55 enabled beats (Numbers 4089–4232), post 3-merge/1-seam-fix state.
**Method:** Full untruncated beat text pulled via sqlcmd per-beat SELECT (verified no truncation up to 5,558-char beat); bible read from `docs/nodes/SPRW.md`; disabled beats (4909, 4206, 4908) also read in full to verify what was actually cut, since several findings below hinge on exactly what those beats contained.

## Verdict

The three merges did real, correct continuity repair — the registry-wall arc, the 4969 front-door lead-in, the 4207 morning re-anchor, and the satellite-withholding structure are all clean, and disabling 4909 specifically fixed a premature "satellite" leak that would have broken the mystery. But the node is **not clean end to end**: there is one clear CRITICAL defect (the AI is called "Sparrow" by name twice in Movement I, before the beat where Elias canonically coins that name), a genuine tense-frame gap where the first third of Movement III is written in present tense against the bible's "Movement III = past tense" rule, a merge artifact left over from folding 4206 into 4205, a bible-documented beat (the failed-door/second-pill GAD moment) that doesn't exist anywhere in the realized text, and one stray line of what reads like an editorial note that slipped into the prose. None of these are large rewrites — all have narrow, targeted fixes — but they should be addressed before this is called finished.

## 2. Knowledge states

**CRITICAL — the AI is named "Sparrow" twice before the beat where Elias names her.**
Beat **4117 (SortKey 1000, Number 4117)** ends Movement II with the explicit naming event: *"He keeps the number in his neuretics in the partition he set up seven years ago for personal use... He names it after a bird."* This is the bible's own designated culmination of Movement II (§6: "...the first contact attempt — an open line, then nothing; **he names her after a bird**.").

But the name "Sparrow" is already in active use, spoken aloud and written down, in two earlier beats:
- **Beat 4099 (SortKey 550)**, Movement I, day two: *"'You've worked with Sparrow before,' Elias says."* — Elias uses the name in dialogue with Druh Prykhodko, nine positions before he supposedly invents it.
- **Beat 4111 (SortKey 700)**, Movement II, en route to Mombasa: *"On the fourth page, in the margin, a question he wrote at three in the morning two weeks ago: Who routes the maintenance payments? Not Sparrow. Something routes them to Sparrow."* — the name appears in his own private notepad, again before the naming beat.

This undercuts the emotional payoff of 4117 (it now reads as redundant — he's already been calling it that for days) and directly conflicts with SS-SPRW-1's mandate that Movement I sustain the person/crew/machine ambiguity without a specific identity being available yet. **Minimal fix:** replace "Sparrow" with a placeholder ("it," "the number," "whatever's on the other end") in 4099 and 4111, or move the naming beat earlier and reframe 4117 as personalizing an already-circulating operator nickname (a bigger change, not recommended).

No other knowledge-state violations found — Sparrow's blind spots, the Attendance incident exchange (4207→4208), and Elias's self-disclosures are all introduced at the point the character would plausibly have/share them, not before.

## 4. Plant/payoff

**MAJOR — merge artifact: beat 4205 references a "confirmation" that no longer exists in the enabled text.**
See §5/point (b) below for the full numbers trace. In short: beat 4205 (SortKey 1400, "She Asks") says *"Her earlier confirmation is still open in the other window — temperature delta 2.31K, friction coefficient 0.547, oscillation period 3.97 seconds mean, amplitude 0.003mm, all within modeled range, no anomaly detected. He has already underlined what's absent from it."* This is a specific, checkable claim that Elias already received and marked up this data before this scene. Pulling the disabled beat confirms why: **4206 ("The Vocabulary Problem," disabled, SortKey 1450)** is the source of this exact confirmation, and is in fact almost a duplicate draft of the same "furniture moved in the night" exchange that 4205 developed independently — right down to the identical numbers and the identical AI reply ("Received. Parsing: 'quality' — no parameter mapping available..."). It appears 4205 absorbed and expanded 4206's material, but the vestigial callback phrase "her earlier confirmation is still open" was never cleaned up to match. **Minimal fix:** in 4205, replace "Her earlier confirmation is still open in the other window" with a self-contained framing (e.g., "A confirmation sits open in the other window") that doesn't assert a prior beat the reader never saw.
- Of the four numbers, the temperature figure has partial cover: beat 4203 (SortKey 1300) already gives "ambient temperature flagged at 2.3K above baseline" (a reasonable rounding of 2.31K), so a reader isn't blindsided by that one. The other three (0.547, 3.97s, 0.003mm) have zero antecedent anywhere in the enabled beats before 4205 states them as already-known. **Judgment: this is a real callback-integrity hole, not an acceptable off-page implication** — it isn't vague foreshadowing the reader can infer, it's an explicit assertion of a specific prior event that doesn't exist in the current text. The practical reader impact is mild (it's flavor/texture, not plot-critical — the numbers are delivered in full regardless, just with an unearned "earlier" tag), which is why this is rated MAJOR rather than CRITICAL.
- The recurring "3.97 seconds" oscillation figure is used consistently and without issue in **4207 (SortKey 1500, twice)** after this point — no problem there.

**MAJOR — a bible-locked GAD-arc beat does not exist in the realized text (pre-existing, not caused by the 3 recent merges).**

This predates the current merge campaign (none of the three disabled beats match it), so it isn't something the recent merges broke — but it is a genuine bible/text mismatch worth flagging. **Minimal fix:** either update the bible to note this beat was never realized/was cut earlier, or add a short beat between 4100 and 4110 dramatizing it.
