# Rook Trilogy Logic & Continuity Audit
**MxG (Magenta & Gunmetal) -> NxR (Neon & Rust) -> CxC (Crimson & Chrome)**
Read in publication order as one continuous work. Report-only; no prose, beat, or node data was modified.

Beats audited: MxG 16 (slug `magenta-gunmetal-019f00a6`), NxR 26 (slug `neon-rust-019f06da`), CxC 14 (slug `marrow-chrome-019f0968`) — all 56 enabled beats read in full via direct SQL export, cross-checked against `docs/nodes/MxG.md`, `docs/nodes/NxR.md`, `docs/nodes/CxC.md`.

## Verdict

The trilogy holds together remarkably well for a three-book, ~56-beat continuity chain: the Tidewell casualty math, the PEREGRINE four-person arithmetic, the Adalemo/Sefi Okonkwo arc, the palm-cut injury chain, and the guard-pivot timing in CxC's finale all check out cleanly across books. But there are four concrete, fixable errors — one arithmetic error in the trilogy's own closing line, one anthropomorphizing lapse on Gerald that the bible explicitly forbids and that the team already fixed elsewhere but missed here, one invented/contradicted plot event (a shooting that didn't happen), and one same-book sequencing bug where an injury is referenced before it occurs. None of these break the trilogy, but the arithmetic error sits in the single most load-bearing sentence of the whole series (CxC's final line), so it's worth prioritizing.

---

## 1. Causality

**No issues found:** MxG's Class V/Class II setup properly gates NxR's premise (Ohara's public formula triggers PEREGRINE's reconstitution under a Triumvirate competitor); NxR's manifest discovery properly gates CxC's premise (the twenty-one, the harvest, Vox's sold face). The "seam" retrofit the CxC bible describes (§9) — MxG's "felt slightly too available" line, NxR's registered-Reads/harvest clause — is present and reads as planted, not asserted from nowhere.

- **[MAJOR] Adalemo's shoulder wound is causally invented.** NxR (SortKey 225, Beat #4983) states as established fact: *"She had put a round through the meat of his shoulder that night, close enough to matter and not close enough to finish it, and heard him go into the lake instead of finishing the conversation... the repair job on that shoulder was the kind you got when your employer prioritized new funding over old maintenance."* This is restated at NxR's climax (SortKey 700, Beat #4843): *"a man she'd shot once, in a storm, on a different lake."* But MxG's actual Lake Platform confrontation (SortKey 650, Beat #4757) never depicts a shooting — Adalemo goes into the lake because a damaged safety railing gives way during a physical/verbal negotiation with Rook ("the railing gave all the way and Seun Adalemo went off the observation deck in a controlled fall"). No gun is drawn or fired in that exchange. NxR asserts an event (Rook shooting Adalemo) that its own predecessor book does not contain.
  - *Minimal fix:* Either add a line to MxG's Beat #4757 railing struggle where Rook actually fires on Adalemo before/as he goes over, or revise NxR's #4983/#4843 lines to describe the railing fall instead of a gunshot.

- **[MAJOR] Wennick's death is retroactively reassigned from a PD surveillance op to the Tidewell extraction contract.** MxG establishes (bible §3, echoed in beats #4749/#4752 SortKey 250/400) that Wennick died on "a surveillance op" — a small, PD-flavored incident: "the window she'd called clean wasn't, and he went through a door she'd said was safe." CxC (SortKey 50, Beat #4844) ties Wennick's voice directly to "Tidewell" — a *freelance extraction contract*, "paid by the head," 21 in/14 out/7 lost, with Lace also on the crew (Beat #4845). A one-door surveillance mishap and a 21-person paid harvest-adjacent contract are different-scaled, differently-sourced jobs; CxC's text explicitly fuses them as the same wound ("the shape of a voice she'd been carrying at the bottom of the count since Tidewell") without reconciling the job descriptions.
  - *Minimal fix:* Add one clause in CxC clarifying Wennick died specifically during the Tidewell extraction itself (not a separate PD op), or one line in MxG's bible/beats acknowledging Wennick's death happened on a paid extraction contract, not strictly "PD surveillance."

## 2. Knowledge States

**No issues found on the primary checks (c, d):** Adalemo's private search for Sefi Okonkwo (NxR), Sefi's own testimony (CxC), and Adalemo's mercy/credential arc are internally coherent — see Plant/Payoff section below for the full trace. PEREGRINE is consistently depicted as human contractors throughout (breathing, bleeding, floating, hand-to-hand) in all three books; no synthetic/AI framing appears.

- **[MINOR] NxR's closing tally over-attributes knowledge to Lace.** NxR's final beat (SortKey 700, Beat #4843) counts "six people" who "carried a piece of what had been behind that wall — herself, Vox, Lace, Stave, Ohara, Adalemo," implying Lace knows the manifest's content (61 names/40 delivered/21 bare). But during the procedure itself (SortKey 550, Beat #4840), the text is explicit that Lace does *not* know the content: *"Lace didn't know the tone was gone. Lace didn't know PEREGRINE had the meters. Lace knew only what she'd agreed to know."* No on-page beat shows Vox or Rook briefing Lace afterward.
  - *Minimal fix:* One line, off-page-acceptable, showing Vox or Rook telling Lace the shape of what was found before the final diner scene — or drop Lace from the "six" and explain the count as five plus the stranger (Adalemo).

## 3. Timeline

**No issues found on gross chronology:** MxG -> NxR is consistently "two years" (NxR Beat #4830: "reaching into the one channel that should have been cold for two years"). NxR -> CxC is consistently "four years" (repeated across nearly every CxC beat: Stave, Vox, Soraya, Adalemo all cite "four years"). Tidewell's "seven years before Soraya" placement doesn't conflict with any other stated date.

- **[MAJOR] CxC's closing diner reuses NxR's diner name "The Sojourn" for a different, unacknowledged location.** NxR's finale (SortKey 700, Beat #4843) names its Z1-lakeshore diner: *"The diner on the Z1 lakeshore didn't have a name on the sign anymore... The regulars called it the Sojourn anyway."* CxC's finale (SortKey 700, Beat #4857) opens: *"The Sojourn sat where Cinderblock's tier met the Gray Zone seam"* — a different location description entirely, with no acknowledgment it's the same place (moved? renamed? coincidentally identical name?) or a deliberate callback. The CxC bible itself frames the series' diner motif as three *distinct* stops ("MxG's Spine diner, NxR's Sojourn" implying CxC's should be its own new beat), which cuts against reusing the exact proper noun.
  - *Minimal fix:* Rename CxC's diner (e.g., "the Cinderblock counter") or add one clause noting Rook deliberately returns to/renames a diner as "the Sojourn" again, making the reuse a character choice rather than an apparent slip.

- **[NOTE] Tidewell's era (~2206) and location (Meridian) are never stated verbatim in beat prose** — only "seven years before Soraya" appears. Not contradicted, just unconfirmed against the specific figures in the audit brief; no fix needed unless those figures live in DB fields not surfaced here.

## 4. Plant / Payoff

**Adalemo / Sefi Okonkwo arc (check c) — coherent, no issues found.** Traced end to end:
- NxR (SortKey 225, Beat #4983): Adalemo privately queries "Sefi Okonkwo" against the Old Harbor rolls and manually deletes the search rather than trust the automated purge — the plant.
- NxR (SortKey 650, Beat #4842): after PEREGRINE's raid costs the team 3 of 4 operators (Beat #4984/#4972, "Three down... The fourth was already past them"), the surviving fourth — Adalemo himself — reaches Vox at the wall, independently confirms "Sefi Okonkwo is one of the twenty-one," and chooses mercy: *"I confirm a data-cache and I never read a forty-first name."*
- CxC (SortKey 400, Beat #4851): Adalemo produces the still-valid Helix credential ("They never revoked me... You don't crack that vault, Rook. You knock with my hand inside it") that gets the crew into the Marrow — the completion via a rerouted/still-live credential.
This is a clean three-beat arc: same name, same mercy logic, same method, no contradiction.

**Tidewell numbers (check a) — internally consistent, no conflation with Axiom found.** CxC repeatedly and consistently states 21 in / 14 out / 7 lost for Tidewell (Beats #4844, #4846, #4847), and the text explicitly guards against conflating it with the Axiom/MxG job: *"Not Axiom. Tidewell."* (#4847). The Axiom (MxG) job's own facts — five-person crew, no crew deaths, everyone paid Φ8,000 — are never blurred into Tidewell's figures anywhere in the trilogy. (See Causality section above, however, for the separate issue of Wennick's *origin story* being reassigned to Tidewell.)

- **[MAJOR] The "processed" manifest in CxC inverts the "marked" logic established in NxR.** NxR's manifest reveal (SortKey 600, Beat #4841) establishes: of 61 names on the barge, **40 carried a "delivered" mark** (accounted-for/reconciled) and **21 carried no mark at all** (the missing, to-be-rescued group) — the "bare" column is the *absence* of a stamp. CxC's version of the manifest, shown to Rook directly (SortKey 150, Beat #4846), instead shows **21 names, all of them stamped with a repeated word — "processed"**: *"They ran down the page into one column, under one repeated word, the word stamped beside every name like a checkmark — processed."* This makes the 21 the *marked* group, not the *unmarked* one — the opposite convention from NxR. It's plausible "processed" is bureaucratic-neutral phrasing rather than a fate marker, but as written it risks readers concluding the 21 were already administratively closed out/killed, contradicting the premise that they're alive and rescuable.
  - *Minimal fix:* Either drop the "processed" stamp language from CxC's manifest scene, or add a clarifying clause that "processed" here means "flagged/queried," distinct from NxR's "delivered" reconciliation mark.

- **[CRITICAL] The trilogy's closing arithmetic does not add up.** CxC's final beat (SortKey 700, Beat #4857 — "The Count, With Names," the single most load-bearing line of the entire series) has Rook write 21 names from the Bay C rescue, then: *"She left a gap after the last name, enough space for the seven who had come out of the secondary block... and below the gap she wrote *31 confirmed*."* 21 + 7 = **28, not 31.** The number is then repeated three more times verbatim ("Thirty-one," "31 confirmed," "Thirty-one... More than it came in with") with no additional group introduced anywhere in the beat to account for the missing 3. This is the trilogy's climactic "the count comes out whole" payoff (paying off the "count never says who didn't" motif planted in both prior books per CxC bible §9), and its own stated math doesn't reconcile.
  - *Minimal fix:* Change "31" to "28" throughout the beat, or change "the seven" to "the ten" (or introduce/name 3 more confirmed survivors) so the components sum to the stated total.

## 5. Orphan References

**No significant orphans found.** Every major device introduced (Gerald, Stave's transformer-room debt, Boiler's condemned building, the substation, the Lake Platform, the Marrow/Helix Reclamation) is set up before it's used.

- **[MINOR] Duplicate surname "Okonkwo" across two unrelated named characters.** MxG's Axiom facility manager is **Petra Okonkwo** (SortKey 150, Beat #4747: "the facility manager was named Okonkwo. Petra Okonkwo"). NxR/CxC's harvested survivor is **Sefi Okonkwo** (NxR #4983; CxC #4846). Different first names, but the project's own naming-diversity rule (no duplicate surnames) is on record; two unrelated, plot-significant "Okonkwo"s in one trilogy is likely to read as a mix-up rather than intentional family/coincidence (nothing in the text suggests they're related).
  - *Minimal fix:* Rename one of the two (Petra Okonkwo, the smaller/one-scene role, is the lower-cost change).

## 6. Bible Agreement

**Locks generally honored.** MxG's twelve Locks (§7) are all satisfied in the beats as written: Ohara publishes herself, Gault is burned not killed, Boiler breaks the bridge and the Lake Platform, Rook jumps the VTOL, QCE's optic-severing mechanic is followed for Scout, the color language appears on schedule. NxR's Locks (Ohara locked the partition herself, Lace's secret surfaces, Vox stays at four channels, Boiler's building is load-bearing) are all honored. CxC's Locks (the win is not clean — Boiler is hurt; Adalemo finishes crossing on-page; Vox's sold face pays off) are all honored.

- **[MINOR] MxG's own bible is internally inconsistent on the payment split** — not a beat error. Bible §0b: "Five freelancers accept a corporate extraction job... split five ways = Φ 8,000 per person." Bible §2: "Payment: Φ 40,000 split **four** ways." The beats consistently and correctly show the 5-way/Φ8,000 split (Beat #4745: "Forty thousand QUANTA. Five-way. Eight each."), matching §0b, not §2. §2 appears to be an uncorrected typo in the bible document itself.
  - *Minimal fix:* Correct "split four ways" to "split five ways" in `docs/nodes/MxG.md` §2.

- **[MAJOR] Bible's own account of where Vox was grazed doesn't match the beats.** The NxR bible (§4) states: *"The graze wound from the Lake Platform healed."* But MxG's actual prose places the graze during the PEREGRINE breach of the safehouse (SortKey 475, Beat #4966: "He clipped her. A graze, high on the left arm"), which happens *before* the crew ever leaves for the Lake Platform (the platform assault is SortKey 650, Beat #4757, and contains no re-injury to Vox). The bible's summary attributes the wound to the wrong location/beat.
  - *Minimal fix:* Change the NxR bible's "from the Lake Platform" to "from the safehouse breach" (or wherever the canonical intent is), and/or verify against the MAJOR finding below before choosing which text to correct.

- **[NOTE] No conflict found for escrow/replacement-payment (check f).** MxG's Lock #3 rule is explicitly stated at SortKey 250, Beat #4749: *"'The Axiom-routed escrow is compromised... So it isn't a balance. It's a replacement.' ... 'Forty thousand, structured identically to the original, from an account they can't touch.'"* Neither NxR nor CxC references escrow or replacement-payment logic at all (NxR's job explicitly has "no payment structure" per its own bible; CxC's job is unpaid/mercy-driven). There is nothing to contradict — reporting per the ground rules as a clean pass.

- **[NOTE] No conflict found for NxR's "18 months" partition dating (check g, third bullet).** NxR establishes the partition Ohara sealed 18 months before NxR's events, "Ohara's own work" (Beat #4832: "When I sealed it eighteen months ago..."). CxC does reference "my partition" (SortKey 400, Beat #4851), but that is explicitly a *different* object — one Soraya built "four years ago... while I was still inside Axiom, before the job you ran [MxG]," i.e., pre-dating even MxG, not the 18-months-before-NxR partition. Since CxC never actually references the NxR partition's dating, there's no figure to contradict. Worth a light editorial flag only: two similarly-described "partitions" belonging to the same character across two books could read as the same object to a fast reader; a one-clause disambiguation (e.g., Soraya explicitly noting "this is a different lock than the one Stave opened") would help but isn't a factual error.

## 7. Per-Book Internal Seams (check g)

**MxG beats 9-12 (dread/chaos sequence):**
- **[CRITICAL] Vox's graze is referenced as already-healed/already-happened *before* the beat in which it occurs.** In SortKey order, the safehouse scene (Beat #4752, SortKey 400) — chronologically the *night before* the PEREGRINE breach — contains: *"Her right arm was elevated slightly, braced off the floor. She hadn't mentioned the graze since it happened. She had mentioned, once, that she was running four channels instead of six..."* This is unambiguous past tense for an injury that has *already* occurred. But the graze itself doesn't happen until the door-breach fight at SortKey 475 (Beat #4966): *"He clipped her. A graze, high on the left arm..."* — which is chronologically *after* Beat #4752 in the SortKey sequence. The wound is described before its cause. This also creates a channel-count discontinuity: Beat #4752 (pre-injury per the actual sequence, but post-injury per its own text) says "four channels instead of six," while the true post-injury coda (Beat #4758, SortKey 700) says "Three channels instead of six" — two different reduced counts for what should be one graze event.
  - *Minimal fix:* Either move the "four channels instead of six" / graze-reference lines out of Beat #4752 into a beat after #4966, or re-sequence #4752 to a later SortKey consistent with its content.
- Spatial/exit logic of the chaos sequence itself (Beat #4966) is otherwise sound: Rook has Ohara's collar, a hand grabs her wrist, Ohara is knocked clear toward the wrong side of the room, Boiler blocks the first attacker, the second attacker fires past Boiler and clips Vox — geography and blocking are legible and consistent with the safehouse's stated two-exit layout (Beat #4752: "Three ways in, two ways out").

**CxC beats 11-14 (guard pivot / palm-cut chain):**
- **No issues found — timing is causally sound.** Vox's "loud channel" broadcast (Beat #4854, SortKey 550, Beat 11) triggers the guards' redirection *before* Rook's confrontation with Anneke Oyelowo (Beat #4855, SortKey 600, Beat 12) references it after the fact: *"You pivoted your guard off Sefi's column to chase one loud voice up the west core."* The pivot demonstrably follows its trigger; no reversed causality.
- **No issues found — the palm-cut chain is clean.** Rook's palm is cut on shattered tablet glass during the Anneka confrontation (Beat #4855, SortKey 600: "the edge of it opened Rook's palm... a clean line from the heel of her hand toward the wrist. Blood welled dark"). The final beat (#4857, SortKey 700) confirms the same hand, consistent healing state, and a deliberate choice to leave it unbandaged: *"the heel of it still faintly pink where the tablet glass had opened it going down into the Marrow, left to close on its own instead of under a synth-patch, because some lines she'd decided were worth keeping."* Same hand, same wound, plausible overnight healing progression — no contradiction.

**NxR's partition dating / Boiler's building:** already covered under Bible Agreement (NOTE) and confirmed as a clean payoff — Boiler's condemned building (introduced as color in NxR's Act One) becomes the literal procedure site and later the site of the collapsing-stairwell defense (Beats #4901, #4984), fulfilling NxR Lock #8 with no contradiction.

**Gerald (check b) — otherwise consistent, one clear violation:**
- Six-legged crawler, consistently described as such in every appearance (MxG #4752: "a scuffed six-legged unit"; CxC #4845: "a salvaged six-legged crawler").
- "Checks it last" habit is established cleanly in MxG (#4752: "she checked it last, every night, before she went in") and not contradicted elsewhere.
- Scout never using the name aloud is honored in every scene where she speaks to/about Gerald (she calls him "Gerald" only in direct address during the CxC rescue, #4849, which is consistent with using the name operationally rather than affectionately — not a violation on its own).
- **[CRITICAL] CxC Beat #4845 (SortKey 100, "The Trusted List") anthropomorphizes Gerald with exactly the child-like/pet-like framing the bible locks out, and misattributes the naming.** *"She's got the dog."* ... *"The dog was Gerald, a salvaged six-legged crawler Scout had named something stupid on purpose, because a thing called Gerald doesn't read as a weapon on a checkpoint scan — it reads as a girl's project, a junk pet, a reason to wave you through."* This directly contradicts the established canon (MxG bible §3 and beats, NxR beats) that **the crew** named Gerald over Scout's objection and that **Scout has never used or endorsed the name** — here she is credited with choosing it deliberately for tactical reasons. It also calls Gerald "the dog" and "a junk pet," precisely the cutesy/pet framing the bible prohibits. Notably, the CxC bible's own workflow log (§10) records a *targeted fix* to this exact problem — *"reconciled Gerald continuity across B9–B13 (crawler/rig, NOT a child — removed 'boy/son/children' framing)"* — but that fix's beat range (B9-B13) does not include Beat 2 (SortKey 100), where this violation lives. It appears the anthropomorphizing pass caught the rescue sequence but missed this earlier beat.
  - *Minimal fix:* Rewrite the "She's got the dog" / "junk pet" lines to remove the pet framing and restore "the crew named him, Scout never uses it" as the attribution, consistent with the rest of the trilogy.
- **[MINOR] Adjacent ambiguity.** Beat #4850 (SortKey 400): *"Gerald — her oldest Shell, the deal that held even here — ticking out the only thing he had, which was the count, twenty-one, steady, so the floor that was about to remember Vox would have to hear the children first."* "The children" most likely refers to the 21 rescued (several described as young — "a kid turned the wrong way," "Bram, early twenties") rather than to Gerald, but its placement immediately after Gerald's name risks being misread as continuing the child-framing the team already flagged and tried to remove. Worth a second look, not urgent.
