# MNEMO Logic & Continuity Audit — 2026-07-03

## Verdict

The 28-beat enabled reading order holds together well at the macro level: the numeric spine (847
recipients, 112 gaps, 27 citations, the 8-second broadcast) is consistent from its first statement
in "What She Knows" through "10:47," the read-only-bleed plant/payoff chain is planted and paid off
cleanly, and character-level locks (Seto he/him, Amara she/her, the razor/machete dichotomy, class
registers, Ciro's grooming tell) never slip across any of the 28 beats. The problems are
concentrated and specific rather than diffuse: recent surgery has left two real orphaned threads
(a Guzman/"room inside the room" plant whose payoff beats were disabled, and Ekow's seven-day
ultimatum, which is never paid off on-page anywhere in the enabled set), a multi-beat factual
contradiction over which character owns "Row 19" on the Batch 44-C manifest and what zone Nuru is
in (this also breaks a bible-LOCKED fact), one clearly misplaced/untitled beat (SortKey 3350) whose
method contradicts the plan the immediately preceding beat just committed to, and one finale
knowledge-state violation where Amara's own POV narration names "Nuru" — a name she is never shown
learning — which also breaks the bible's LOCKED bleed-intrusion attribution rule. None of these are
cosmetic; all five are fixable with beat-level edits (rewrite, re-enable, retitle, or reposition)
rather than structural rewrites.

## 1. Causality

- [SEVERITY: high] SortKey 2800 (Beat Id `019EE123-86B7-73AE-B80F-E91974DBC590`, "Sealed System") —
  internal contradiction within a single beat. While reading the Zone 5 section of the manifest,
  the text says: *"Zone 5 was long, and somewhere in its middle was a name he did not let his eye
  fix on ... Facility C-9. Row 19."* Facility C-9 is Nuru's clinic (established two beats earlier
  in Cleanup List as "Zone 6, Facility C-9"), so this line silently relocates her to Zone 5 and
  assigns her Row 19. Two paragraphs later, in the Zone 6 section of the *same beat*, the text
  says: *"Zone 6, twenty-two entries, and her name in the nineteenth row"* — referring to Amara
  (continuing the "eight days of carrying the match" thread from Cleanup List). Row 19 is thus
  claimed for two different characters inside one beat. Fix: give Nuru's manifest entry its own
  row number under the Zone 6 heading (where Facility C-9 belongs) and remove the Zone 5/Row 19
  language.
- [SEVERITY: high] SortKey 3350 (Beat Id `019F21AD-E6DF-79E0-B68F-C46204F676CF`, untitled) —
  contradicts the plan just established one beat earlier. "No Clean Line" (SortKey 3300) has Seto
  reason explicitly that couriers "optimized for speed and frequency and low signature" are exactly
  what the monitoring node can track, and settle on an *analog, unregistered* solution: a physical
  lockbox at a noodle stall run by a woman with "no registered channel." The very next beat
  (3350) instead shows him approaching a *credentialed, networked* drop panel ("He reached the
  panel and put his hand flat on the reader") — precisely the kind of chokepoint 3300 just reasoned
  him away from — and it gets remotely killed mid-access. The two beats describe two different,
  unreconciled extraction methods back to back. Fix: see §3/§6 for the full recommendation on this
  beat (reposition or rewrite to use the lockbox method).
- [SEVERITY: none] The read-only-bleed rule-break in "The Procedure" (SortKey 3800) is properly
  set up by "Sealed System" (SortKey 2800: *"He could not send the manifest through it. He could
  read the manifest through it."*) before Seto violates it. No causality issue here — flagged as a
  positive in §4.

## 2. Knowledge States

- [SEVERITY: high] SortKey 4200 (Beat Id `019F2A3E-D910-706C-9242-36DCEB763CCC`, "10:47") — Amara's
  own POV narration names the bleed-intrusion source: *"The sentence arrived and passed through her
  without breaking her attention, the way they did now, and she let Nuru's stillness sit in her for
  a moment before she let it go."* Amara is never shown, in any of her chapters, learning that
  Seto's sister's name is Nuru. Every appearance of the name "Nuru" in the enabled text occurs
  inside Seto's or Nuru's own POV beats (Minimum Footprint, Cleanup List, Sealed System, Story
  Runs, The Last Route, The Procedure, The Weight Returns) — Seto's messages to Amara in "Different
  Frequency" and "Zone 7, Tuesday, 9AM" refer to her only as "she." This is both a knowledge-state
  violation (Amara acting on a name she was never shown acquiring) and a violation of the bible's
  LOCKED bleed-intrusion rule (§6: intrusions arrive "without attribution... The POV character does
  not acknowledge them"). Fix: remove the name from Amara's narration — "she let the stillness sit
  in her" — and let the reader supply the identification via dramatic irony, per the rule as
  written.
- [SEVERITY: medium] SortKey 4200 ("10:47") and SortKey 4000 (Beat Id
  `019EE125-4FB9-7A76-A817-CBD3F29B277B`, "Different Frequency") — Amara treats "eight seconds" as
  a known, precise figure (*"112 gaps, eight seconds, the four minutes a query had stayed open
  before someone closed it"*), but no on-page beat shows Seto reporting that duration to her. Her
  only shown exposure to the reverse-transfer event is a single unattributed bleed sensation during
  "The Procedure" (SortKey 3800: *"Amara doubled forward in her seat as a memory she had never
  owned arrived..."*), which would not by itself convey a precise duration. This is plausible as
  off-page communication (the two are in constant contact by this point in the book) but is not
  shown. Fix: add one line where Seto states the figure to her, or soften "eight seconds" to
  something she could plausibly infer without being told.
- [SEVERITY: none] Phase II — both Amara and Seto are consistently and verifiably informed on-page
  from "What She Knows" (SortKey 2400) onward; no gaps found through the finale.
- [SEVERITY: none] Each other's Act 2/3 situation (Amara's forced leave and credibility campaign;
  Seto's route closures and certification suspension) — tracked consistently through the
  bleed-and-text channel across "The Turn," "Story Runs," and "Different Frequency"; no beat has
  either character acting on information not shown being exchanged.

## 3. Timeline

- [SEVERITY: medium] SortKey 3350 sits in a sequence that reads as three unreconciled extraction
  attempts in a row: the analog lockbox plan (3300, never shown executing), the credentialed-reader
  drop that fails (3350), and then a same-day pivot to an in-person meet-and-carry to Doru's relay
  shop (3400, "Story Runs") with no acknowledgment of either earlier plan's fate. As currently
  ordered, 3350 reads as happening *after* Seto has already reasoned his way to avoiding exactly
  its method, not before. See §6 for the placement recommendation.
- [SEVERITY: low] Minimum Footprint (SortKey 2450) sets Ekow's ultimatum at a rooftop encounter;
  Cleanup List's callback (SortKey 2500) labels the same encounter "the Seam crossing" ("Ekow Ato
  had given him seven at the Seam crossing"). This is likely just shorthand for "a crossing point
  within Seam territory" rather than a distinct location, but it sits close in the text to the
  separately-named "Shen crossing" (SortKey 3400, the Amara/Seto meeting point) and risks reading
  as the same place on a fast read. Fix: rename one of the two ("the Seam crossing" vs. "Shen
  crossing") for clarity, or make explicit that the rooftop sits inside Seam territory the first
  time it's called that.
- [SEVERITY: none] Ekow's countdown arithmetic itself is internally consistent: "seven days left"
  (Minimum Footprint) → "three [days] already spent... Four days [remaining]" (Cleanup List) is
  correct subtraction and lands on the right beat in sequence.

## 6. Bible Agreement

- [SEVERITY: high] The bible LOCKS Nuru's manifest position as "Row 19 on the Batch 44-C manifest"
  and "Zone 6, Facility C-9" (`docs/nodes/MNEMO.md` §4). Of the four enabled beats that state a
  row/zone for her, only one agrees with the bible: Cleanup List (SortKey 2500) correctly has
  *"Banda, Nuru. Batch 44-C. Zone 6, Facility C-9"* but assigns Row 19 to Amara instead of Nuru.
  Sealed System (SortKey 2800) places Nuru's Facility-C-9 entry under a Zone 5 heading and re-uses
  Row 19 (already claimed for Amara two paragraphs later in the same beat — see §1). Story Runs
  (SortKey 3400) states *"Nuru's name was the fourth in Zone 5"* and The Last Route (SortKey 3700)
  states *"Nuru's row was Zone 5."* Net effect: the bible's one locked fact about Nuru's manifest
  position is contradicted by three of the four beats that cite it. Fix: canonicalize on "Nuru =
  Zone 6, Facility C-9" (bible + Cleanup List) and remove the Zone 5 language from Sealed System,
  Story Runs, and The Last Route; give Nuru her own row number distinct from Amara's Row 19.
- [SEVERITY: high] The bible LOCKS Ekow's withdrawal as load-bearing (§4, quoted in §4 above) and
  his chapter map assigns him POV chapters at "Thursday Morning" (Act 2) and "Contract Delayed"
  (Act 3). Neither beat currently occupying those titles contains Ekow; his only on-page appearance
  in the whole enabled book is the single Minimum Footprint scene. This is a bible-vs-beat mismatch
  significant enough to affect how the book's Act 2/3 antagonist pressure reads — see §4 for the
