## Overall verdict

The core machine of VATD holds together and lands hard: the accidental shot (250), the too-late IdentiScan (250), the loaner clock, the Maisy match with the "Providence" blow-up (450), the Casimir turn (675–715), the no-scar dawn discovery (715), and the "Change of heart" reinstall (875) are all present, causally earned, and faithful to the bible's locks. ## 1. Causality chain

Mostly sound. Each Act I–IV beat follows from the last: recall drive → den → snag → shot → loaner → hunt for match → Maisy → clinic → staging → Casimir turn → dawn collapse. The Casimir turn (675) is properly set up (his outsider-dog status planted in 75/275/525, the survival-by-redirection logic built in 350/500) and pays off as a genuine flip that HELPS (700), matching the §5c retcon.

**Two breaks in the reinstall sequence (Act V):**

- **BLOCKER — SortKey 850 (with 825, 875).** Beat 850 is a stale/misplaced beat that breaks the causal chain and reintroduces a cut plot direction. In 825, Tomas goes to Levin, says the old man's kidney is "still viable… If we move now, we have tonight," and the two of them get in the Wagon together and Tomas drives off to do the reinstall. In 875, they drive to the depot together and reinstall the kidney into the original old man. But **850 sits between them** and depicts Tomas **alone on a Pulse car**, "the hands of a man who had locked the Wagon and walked away," riding home, with the kidney still "in the rack" and "the matching algorithm will run" — i.e., the system reallocating it to a *new* recipient (the exact arc the fold cut; cf. disabled beats 1000 "North Tier clinic… kidney delivery" and the greyed-out `REALLOCATE TO NEW RECIPIENT` in 875). Tomas is "not going to… revise his position," undecided, going home solo — which cannot follow 825 (already decided, in the Wagon with Levin) nor precede 875 (executing the reinstall together). It is an orphaned remnant of the pre-fold kidney-reallocation storyline.
  *Fix:* Disable beat 850 (it belongs to the cut reallocation arc). If any of its imagery is wanted, relocate it to **before** 825 and reframe it as Tomas's daytime deliberation over the unsent form — but as currently placed it must come out.

- **MODERATE — SortKey 500 → 525 (Osei's death vs interrogation).** See §5/§(e): the Full Repossession order at the end of 500 establishes Osei is dead; 525 then shows Osei alive being interrogated. Causally the interrogation causes the death causes the strip order, but they are read in reverse.

No effects without causes elsewhere; no causes that go nowhere (the machete, handkerchief, no-scar weld, blood-drive flashback all discharge).

All four pre-fold waypoints (**72 → 68 → 58 → 48**) are present, in place, and the countdown is monotonic. **No waypoint was orphaned or dropped by the fold.** The heart is installed at the 48-hour mark, well inside the window; the tighter real deadline is the dawn wedding (~24 h), which is intentional per bible §1.

### Wall-clock contradictions (the timeline's weak spot):

- **BLOCKER — SortKey 300.** "The Wagon pulled out of the block at **a quarter to three in the morning**… clock reading 71:48:09." 2:45 a.m. is **before the shift's own start**: beat 50 puts the manifest at **4:12 a.m.** and the first (old-man) harvest at **four-thirty**, and beat 100 says "forty minutes to the next one," placing the den at ~5:10 a.m. and the loaner-seating at ~5:30 a.m. The loaner cannot be seated ~2.75 hours before the workday began.
  *Fix:* Change "a quarter to three in the morning" to a time consistent with the shift (≈ "a quarter to six in the morning"), or drop the absolute stamp and keep only the 71:48:09 clock.

- **MODERATE — SortKey 600.** "They had been gone from Osei's apartment for eighteen hours — **4:12 in the morning** to ten past ten at night." This anchors the *den departure* to 4:12 a.m., but 4:12 was the manifest time for the *first, different* job (the old man's kidney; beat 50), and it also conflicts with beat 300's 2:45 a.m. Three mutually inconsistent times exist for leaving the den (2:45, 4:12, ~5:15). The 18-hours math (4:12 → 22:10) is internally fine; the *anchor* is wrong.
  *Fix:* Re-anchor to the true den departure time and keep the 18-hour span (or restate the span from the corrected den time).

- **MODERATE — SortKey 825.** "The old man's kidney. I pulled it **this morning** before we went to the Ward and never moved it." The kidney was harvested at the shift's very first job (beat 100, Day 1 dawn); the reinstall happens after the Day 2 dawn wedding-collapse (825 = "we have tonight," i.e., Day 2 night). "This morning" mis-dates a Day-1 harvest as same-day.
  *Fix:* "I pulled it yesterday morning" (or otherwise reconcile to the multi-day span).

- **MODERATE — SortKey 900.** "the first thing close to a smile Tomas had seen on him in **four days, since the storm grate**." The storm grate is beat 600 (Day 1 night); the coda is ~Day 2 night / Day 3 — roughly one to one-and-a-half days later, not four. "Four days" also strains the kidney's "still viable / we have tonight" window (825) and the "twelve more hours" viability (850).
  *Fix:* Change "four days" to match the compressed span (e.g., "in a day," "since the storm grate").

- **MINOR — SortKey 400.** "The Wagon's ward had been holding Do-yun for **two hours**, the loaner had sixty-eight to go." 72 − 68 = ~4 hours elapsed, not two. The "sixty-eight" is consistent with the descending clock (70:51 → 68 → 63); the "two hours" is the loose figure.
