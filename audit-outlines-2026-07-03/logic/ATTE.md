# ATTE ("Attendance") — Logic & Continuity Audit

**Scope:** Report-only. 31 enabled beats read cover-to-cover in ascending SortKey order; disabled beats sampled for orphan-reference hunting. Bible: `docs/nodes/ATTE.md`. No DB or code changes made.

---

## Overall verdict

The story holds together at the level that matters most — the central logic (the architecture-as-antagonist, the cross-corp clearance being the only node from which 47 becomes visible, the deliberate calendar impossibilities, the no-rescue institutional-ambiguity ending) is sound, and the bible's locked beats (Bear's four-minute wait as obligation-not-guilt, Nadia's wind, the 4–11 minute gap, the echo/heading mechanism, 47 children / 22 sites / 22 months) all land correctly. The rescue-act excision was **clean** at the level the extra-item hunt was worried about: no enabled beat references "Selvamani's modulator," and the "threshold" material that survives reads as Selvamani's *explanation of why following is impossible* — which now actively justifies the no-rescue ending rather than dangling. **However**, the editorial pass left a cluster of real continuity seams concentrated in the mid-story evidence-building sequence (beats 500 / 525 / 550 vs 600) and two structural artifacts of disabling beats (a vanished "Chapter 3" heading and a Selvamani scene that now opens with no arrival). None are individually story-breaking, but the mid-story cluster is the kind of thing a careful reader will trip over, and one genuine orphan reference survives (a "2 a.m. phone call" that only ever happened in a disabled beat). These are worth fixing before this is held up as the gateway story.

## 1. Causality chain

Causality is largely intact. Each discovery is motivated: Ren's phrasing triggers the corridor epiphany (500); the epiphany drives the scan-log pull (525); the burnout-flag metadata drives the blank-sender discovery (600); the intruder's pre-marked records drive her to Selvamani (900). The intruder-fed information is always delivered on-page before she acts on it, so nothing is an effect without a cause.

**FINDING 1.1 — MODERATE — SortKey 900 → 1100.** The Selvamani scene has no establishing action. Beat 900 ends "Yemina put the gray car in gear"; beat 1100 opens cold on mid-dialogue ("'Where are they,' Yemina said. 'I don't know.'") with a brand-new named character. The reader is never shown Yemina arriving at the Back-of-the-Yards apartment, being admitted, or seeing Selvamani. The establishing beats that did this work (disabled SortKeys 950 and 1000: "Sit somewhere, Dr. Selvamani said… The office had been a faculty workroom once…") were disabled and not replaced. *Fix:* re-enable/adapt a short establishing beat, or prepend two–three sentences to 1100 covering arrival, the door, and first sight of Selvamani and her printout-buried office.

Otherwise no effect-without-cause or cause-going-nowhere was found.

## 2. Knowledge states

No character acts on information they could not plausibly have. Yemina's leaps are each preceded by on-page input (Ren's testimony, the scanner logs, the autocomplete, Selvamani's map). The intruder supplies the 47-count and Selvamani's address, and both are rendered on-page before she uses them, so this is not a knowledge violation — it is the story's engine.

**FINDING 2.1 — MINOR — SortKey 700 vs 750.** In beat 700 Yemina reads the full 47-name list and reacts only to Kito; her own name is not mentioned. In beat 750 she refers to having sat "with forty-seven entries in front of her and her own name at the bottom of the list." The chilling implication (she is being tuned too — supported by the six-week frequency plant) is intended, but it is *asserted* retroactively in 750 rather than *shown* in 700 where she actually read the list. *Fix:* plant the self-recognition beat in 700 (a half-line where the scroll reaches the bottom and stops) so 750's callback is earned.

(The 2-vs-3 data-point contradiction that also touches "what Yemina knows at this point" is filed under Timeline §3.2, since its root is the temporal/geography seam.)

## 3. Timeline

The macro-clock is deliberately loose ("three weeks that had not felt like three weeks") and the final-morning impossibilities (6:02 notice naming a 0540 appointment; 0.4-sec and 0.3-sec responses) are canon texture — **not flagged**, and correctly self-acknowledged in beat 1249.61. The final-morning clock chain is internally sound: Chinwe visit 5:11 → "twenty-nine minutes" → dock transfer 5:41 (beat 1243.75) → drive-back dashboard 6:52 (beat 1249.61). 6:52 sits correctly after the 6:02 notice and the 5:40/5:41 dock events. Case-number and headcount consistency (extra item b) checks out: 3-child caseload throughout; Tomas=45 / Daria=46 / Kito=47 (beats 50, 600) with the elegant intended payoff that Kito, the newest, is case #47 and there are exactly 47 children citywide; 22 sites / 22 months holds (beats 1100, 1225). The three separate "22"s (22 days for Tomas, 22 months for the pattern, 22 sites) are echoes, not contradictions.

**The mid-story cluster (beats 500 / 525 / 550 vs 600) is the story's biggest logic problem.** It reads like beats 500/525 were written under an earlier case model (three *prior* cases spread over six months, one already scrubbed) that was never reconciled with the current model (three *current* cases all inside 22 days, all data fresh). Broken out:

**FINDING 3.1 — MODERATE — SortKey 525 vs 600.** Beat 525 states "Back of the Yards was seven months ago and the scan retention window for that district was ninety days — that one was gone, scrubbed by schedule." But beat 600 fixes Tomas Reyes's school as "a school in Back of the Yards," disappeared "twenty-two days ago." A 22-day-old case is inside any 90-day window and would not be scrubbed. If the beat-525 "Back of the Yards" and Tomas's Back of the Yards are the same site, the timing is contradictory; if they are two different Back-of-the-Yards child cases, the story never distinguishes them and the reader will conflate them. Relatedly, beat 500's "For six months she had found the same mark… three times" clashes with beat 600's caseload being three children all within the last 22 days. *Fix:* decide whether 500/525 describe prior closed cases (and label them as such, distinct from the current three) or the current three (and then delete "seven months ago"/"six months" and the scrubbed-retention line).

**FINDING 3.2 — MODERATE — SortKey 525 vs 550.** Point-count contradiction. Beat 525 ends with "a map… that now had two points instead of three, and two points was a line" (Back of the Yards scrubbed, so she has only Kito + Rogers Park). Beat 550 immediately asserts the opposite: "She had just pulled three children's last walk out of three buildings" and "the bearing she had just drawn three times on a map." She cannot have both 2 and 3 points across adjacent beats. *Fix:* pick one. If Back of the Yards is genuinely scrubbed, 550 must say two points/twice; if she recovered all three, remove the scrubbed line in 525.

**FINDING 3.3 — MODERATE — SortKey 525.** Kito's school is named "Northside Elementary" here ("Northside Elementary logged by zone… Kito's scan cleared the east wing at 10:51"), but Kito's school is the **Aldiss Mwangi Community Learning Center** everywhere else (beats 100, 150, 550) and Yemina is physically inside it (on a bench outside its main office) when she pulls these logs. "Northside Elementary" is a leftover name. *Fix:* change "Northside Elementary" to "the Aldiss Mwangi center" (or "the building"). (Also filed under Orphan references §5.)

**FINDING 3.4 — MODERATE — SortKey 250.** Yemina's career length is given as "eleven years" ("She had spent eleven years learning that the doors which hurt you most…"), but every other reference makes it fifteen years (beats 400, 450 ×2, 1150, 1249.61, 1249.8) — with her tenure on *this contract* separately and consistently six years (beats 50, 1225, 1249.8). "Eleven" fits neither. *Fix:* change "eleven years" → "fifteen years."

**FINDING 3.5 — MINOR — SortKey 825.** The counseling record is sealed at 11:52, "nine minutes after she'd walked out of an office" — i.e., Achebe's, which the sentence unmistakably describes ("a man who had ironed his collar… folded his hand back from his lanyard"). But the visit chronology has her leave Achebe's office early (he yields "Room 214," she then does the whole Ren interview, the corridor realization, and the bench scan-pull, exiting the building only around noon). Eleven-fifty-two cannot be nine minutes after she left Achebe. *Fix:* either move the seal timestamp, or reword to "nine minutes after she left the building."

**FINDING 3.6 — MINOR — SortKey 1225 vs 1150.** The 72-hour window "ran out on a Tuesday," but the AAMA voicemail that starts the window (beat 1150) reads as occurring the same Thursday as the school visit; Thursday + 72h = Sunday, not Tuesday. The intervening days are unshown. Given the deliberately soft calendar and the "nine days before" reference in 1249.8, some time clearly passes — but the 72h/Tuesday arithmetic doesn't visibly close. *Fix:* one clause acknowledging the days between the call and the deadline, or drop the specific "Tuesday."

**FINDING 3.7 — MINOR — SortKey 750.** Chinwe's grandson is "gone three days" / "three days ago… would have been a key in a lock," but Kito "Disappeared Tuesday" (600) and this visit is the Thursday of the school visit (~2 days). Off by one under a strict count. *Fix:* "two days," or leave as colloquial rounding.

**FINDING 3.8 — MINOR — SortKey 100.** Aldiss-Mwangi Capital Partners "was absorbed into Arcturus Holdings in 2207 and hadn't existed for eighteen years." Present is explicitly 2226 (beat 550), so it's been nineteen years. *Fix:* "nineteen years."

**FINDING 3.9 — MINOR — SortKey 250.** Kito's room (Room 214, fourth grade; Kito is nine) is described as "a room full of eleven-year-olds." *Fix:* "nine-year-olds" (or "ten-year-olds").

## 5. Orphan references

**The rescue-act excision is clean on the three specific worries (extra item a):**
- **"Selvamani's modulator":** the word *modulator* appears in **no** enabled beat (it lives only in disabled SortKey 1350, "I'm already packing the modulator"). No orphan.
- **Threshold-crossing:** "threshold" survives only as Selvamani's *explanation* of why the children can't be followed ("threshold instability… the door isn't a door… doesn't keep its address," 1100) and as a metaphor ("handed it across the threshold," 763). The literal rescue crossing (disabled 1450–1900, "Ren walked through the threshold at 6:14 AM") is fully excised with no dangling callbacks. No orphan.
- **"Ren":** *not* an orphan — Ren Vasquez is a central enabled witness (300–450) and is correctly retired as a closed line ("no further contact required," 827). His disabled rescue role leaves no dangling reference.

**FINDING 5.1 — MODERATE — SortKey 1249.8 (also 827 region).** Genuine orphan reference. The final disposition prose notes the form "said nothing about a phone call at two in the morning." That 2 a.m. call with Ren is depicted **only in a disabled beat** (SortKey 1300, "He picked up on the second ring…", part of the disabled rescue-planning run). In the enabled cut Yemina interviews Ren exactly once (300–450) with no phone exchange, so the reader hits an event that never happened on-page. *Fix:* cut "a phone call at two in the morning" from the sentence (the companion details — the three-week tremor, the never-crossed-out word — are both established and can carry the line alone).

**FINDING 5.2 — MODERATE — structural, SortKey ~1100.** The "Chapter 3" heading is orphaned onto a disabled beat. Enabled chapter titles are "Chapter 1" (50), "Chapter 2" (550), "Chapter 4" (1225) — there is **no Chapter 3** in the enabled reading order. The title lived on disabled SortKey 1050 (the superseded duplicate of 1100). Readers go Ch1 → Ch2 → Ch4. *Fix:* set BeatTitle "Chapter 3" (and IsChapterStart=1) on beat 1100. (Note also that beat 1225 carries the "Chapter 4" title but has IsChapterStart=0 — minor metadata cleanup.)

(FINDING 3.3, "Northside Elementary," is also an orphan/leftover name; cross-referenced here.)

## 6. Bible agreement

Strong agreement overall. Verified matches: Bear = the four-minute wait as **obligation, not guilt** (1248.44 explicitly rejects "guilt" for "duty discharged without complaint" — matches §3 exactly); Nadia holding the bag against a wind that isn't there (1243.75 ↔ §3); the folder to Bear at the dock, held four minutes, then the locker (§1); the 4–11 minute gap (1225 ↔ §1/§4.1); 47 children / 22 sites / 22 months and the one-short-of-threshold logic (700, 1100 ↔ §4b); echo/heading/no-teleport mechanism (400, 1100 ↔ §4b); children not recovered, ends on institutional ambiguity (§0/§4.3); the gray car never redeemed (§4.5); school at 20th & Halsted with the schism south at 35th & Halsted, children leaning southwest (§4b).

**FINDING 6.1 — MODERATE — SortKey 150 vs 550.** Reception is a human on arrival and an android on departure, with an internal seam. Bible §3 designates the reception interface as an android. Enabled beat 150 introduces a **human** — "SUMI… FAMILY LIAISON" — at "the reception desk," who greets Yemina and pages Dr. Achebe. Beat 550 then has "The reception android… waiting at the desk where she had left it… it had no reason to be anywhere else," implying the android was there the whole time — but she left Sumi there, not an android. Either two desks/entities are being conflated, or a shift change happened that the "where she had left it / no reason to be anywhere else" phrasing contradicts. Sumi does not appear in the bible at all. *Fix:* reconcile — either make arrival the android too (align to bible), or make 550 acknowledge the human→android handoff rather than asserting continuity.

**FINDING 6.2 — MINOR — SortKey 350 vs 900.** A missing child is named "Priya" (350, one of Ren's kept name-strips) while the researcher is "Dr. Priya Selvamani" (900). Same first name for two characters; low collision risk since one is first-name-only, but it brushes the project's name-diversity rule. *Fix (optional):* rename the child.

**FINDING 6.3 — MINOR — SortKey 800.** Daria Drew's household is called "the Osei family" with mother "Abena Osei" and father "Lech Szymborski" — the child's surname (Drew) matches neither parent nor the family label. This is a consequence of the bible-documented rename (§4b: "Daria Drew, formerly Amara Osei," to avoid collision with Mnemosync's Amara Osei), so it is canon, not an error — flagged only because "the Osei family" containing a child surnamed "Drew" may read as a slip. *Fix (optional):* a one-line in-prose signal that Daria took a different surname, or accept as diaspora texture.
