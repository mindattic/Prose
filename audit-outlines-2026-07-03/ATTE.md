## Structural Findings

1. **The bible's entire central object and ending are absent from the beats — not weakened, not altered, simply not present.** `docs/nodes/ATTE.md` §1 calls "the folder" handed by Nadia to Bear at a dock gate the story's central object, and §5 states Chapter 4 *is* "Nadia at the dock gate. Bear with the folder. Four minutes. The locker. Yemina's report goes in." A full-text search across every beat (enabled and disabled) for "Nadia," "dock gate," "locker," and "Bear" (the character) returns zero hits. Chapter 4 as actually written (Beats 32–41) is a completely different scene: a physical extraction through the schism. This is not a case of the bible needing a line updated — it means the story's documented spine and its actual content have no beat in common for the entire final act. Whatever produced the 92.7 score, it was not scoring the story the bible describes.

2. **The climax violates the bible's own genre lock and is not a story yet — it's a fragment.** §0 states explicitly: "This is NOT: A rescue story... whether the children are recoverable... is a separate question from solving the investigation," and Lock #3: "The children are not recovered on the page." Beats 32–41 are a page-by-page physical rescue: modulator, countdown, wrist-hauling, a threshold that "fails," survivors on the far side. That alone is a locked-rule violation. Worse, the story doesn't even finish its own rescue plot — Beat 41 ends on an open decision to breach a "sealed section" for the one lost child, with no filed report, no institutional handoff, no ambiguity — the ending the bible promises literally never occurs in the text. This is not "structurally sound at 90+"; it's an unresolved cliffhanger being scored as a finished 40-beat story.

3. **The extraction math does not add up, and the discrepancy is never acknowledged on the page.** 47 children need to cross (Beat 36 explicitly does this math: "forty-seven at one per second is forty-seven seconds"). At the "twenty seconds remaining" mark (Beat 38) the text states "twenty-three children are still inside" — meaning only 24 have crossed with 20 seconds left. The extraction continues for the remaining time, yet the final tally (Beat 39) is **twenty-two children who made it through** — fewer than had already crossed at the earlier checkpoint. The story then narrates this as "one child unaccounted for" (Kito) as if 46 of 47 were saved. The actual numbers on the page describe roughly half the children (≈24–25, not 1) unaccounted for. This is a hard continuity/arithmetic error, not a style note, and it guts the intended gut-punch (losing exactly one named child) — the text's own numbers say the rescue was a near-failure, not a near-success, and nothing in the prose reconciles this.

4. **The climax silently swaps in a physics/mechanism that contradicts the bible's LOCKED §4b in the same story.** §4b is explicit and locked: the schism is a 5D cross-section with "no inside accessible to us" — it reaches into sealed rooms, drawers, and skulls without an interior anyone can occupy; "children are not moved, they are reached, and then they walk." The entire forensic apparatus (echo + heading) exists specifically because direct entry/observation of an "inside" is impossible. Beats 31–41 discard this wholesale: characters physically walk through a stabilized threshold into a walkable, countable, room-like interior with light, temperature, and a rescue party that hauls bodies back by the wrist — and then Beat 40 introduces literal underground architecture ("the Dead Realm," a "third sublevel," a "main access shaft," "bearing 14-north by 7-east," "sealed section," a faction/entity named **"Axiom"** who "never bothered to demo" corridors). None of this — Dead Realm, sublevels, shafts, Axiom — appears in the ATTE bible at all. It reads like the climax was drafted against a different (portal/dungeon) mechanism than the one the bible locks, and nobody reconciled the two. For a story explicitly designated the universe's zero-prior-knowledge gateway (§0), dropping an unexplained faction name from a different arc (per project memory, "Axiom" belongs to the separate Sable/Axiom arc) is a direct violation of the gateway design.

5. **The antagonist is not "the system's architecture" — it's a small ensemble of unresolved supernatural agents, contradicting §0's stated design.** §0: "This is NOT... a CorpoNation villain story. The antagonist is the system's architecture — not a person." By the climax there are at minimum three distinct agentic entities: (a) "the intruder," an unidentified Channeler that pre-solves large parts of Yemina's investigation for her (marks pages, autocompletes queries, pre-fetches Selvamani's address, physically shields Yemina from) (b) "the deeper thing," an ancient, godlike presence that examines Yemina personally, and (c) the unnamed source of the 72–74 Hz "second signal" that Selvamani frames as being in a "contest" or "conversation" with the first. None of these are named, motivated, or resolved by the end. A story billed as deliberately small-scale procedural horror ("three children, three flags, one investigator") has, by its climax, accumulated a three-way supernatural power struggle with genuine stakes and intent — the opposite of "the antagonist is architecture, not a person."

6. **A significant share of Yemina's "detective work" is not hers — it is handed to her by the intruder, undercutting the bible's central character claim.** §3 states her competence is archival, that she "reads bureaucracy as a weapon," and §6 that the register exists to make *her* competence visible. In practice, the intruder supplies the 17–19 Hz page highlights (Beat 20), Selvamani's address (Beat 21), and the "72" clue (Beat 26) — the load-bearing breakthroughs of the middle act are being fed to the protagonist by an unexplained third party, not discovered by her method. This is fine as a mystery hook, but it is in tension with the bible's insistence that her filing-and-cross-referencing method *is* the story's engine.

7. **Tonal cliché in the extraction sequence breaks the register the rest of the story establishes.** §6 is explicit about what Yemina's voice does *not* do (marvel, dramatize, editorialize) and the register is "administrative horror," quiet and procedural. Beats 36–39 shift without transition into stock action-rescue dialogue and structure — a shouted numeric countdown ("Sixty." "Fifty-eight." "Forty seconds."), "single file, you don't stop for anything, if you drop something it stays dropped," a wrist-drag extraction, a dying-light ticking clock. This is a standard genre-thriller extraction beat, not GREY-register administrative horror, and it arrives with no register bridge from the preceding 30 beats of paperwork, kitchens, and hold music.

8. **Missing consequence: the sealed counseling record (Beat 19) is a loaded gun that's never fired.** A record gets sealed nine minutes after Yemina's request — clearly implying someone at the school (or above it) is actively obstructing her, in real time, in response to her specific movements. This is never followed up, explained, or connected to anything else in the case (not the intruder, not the AAMA, not Achebe). As written it's a dangling implication of an antagonistic human actor, which itself sits oddly against §0's claim that there is no CorpoNation-villain / personal-antagonist story here.

## Verdict

**Not structurally sound at 90+ as currently written, and the 92.7 score cannot be trusted to describe this text.** The story's documented ending (Findings 1–2) does not exist in the beats that were scored; what exists instead is an unresolved rescue-mission fragment that (a) violates two of the bible's explicit narrative locks, (b) contains a hard, uncorrected arithmetic error in its own climax (Finding 3), and (c) contradicts the story's own LOCKED schism physics in the same act it's set in (Finding 4). These are not polish issues — they are load-bearing contradictions between what the story claims to be, what its own locked worldbuilding says is possible, and what is actually on the page.

Structural changes needed (not prose-level):
1. **Reconcile bible and beats on the ending.** Either restore/write the Nadia–Bear–folder Chapter 4 the bible describes and cut the schism-rescue climax entirely, or rewrite §0/§1/§5/Lock #3/Lock #4 of the bible to match the rescue story that was actually written. The current state — bible and text describing two unrelated stories — has to resolve one direction before this can be scored at all.
2. **Fix or remove the extraction headcount.** The 47/24/23/22 numbers must reconcile to a single, checkable total, and the gap between "one child lost" (the story's stated stakes) and "twenty-two survivors" (the story's actual arithmetic) needs to be either corrected to match intent or the intent changed to match the numbers.
3. **Resolve the physics conflict between §4b (no accessible inside) and the walkable-interior/Dead Realm climax**, and either cut "Axiom" and "Dead Realm" (undefined, cross-arc, gateway-breaking) or formally introduce and gloss them as new locked canon.
4. **Give the story an actual ending on the page.** A 41-beat story that stops on an active decision to attempt a second, unresolved rescue is not a complete structure — it needs either a closing beat (report filed, institutional handoff, ambiguity restored) or an explicit acknowledgment that ATTE is now serialized/open-ended, which is itself a call that needs to go through the bible, not be discovered by a reader mid-climax.
