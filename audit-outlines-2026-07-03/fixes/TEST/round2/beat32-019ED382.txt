The main door came off its hinges at 1312.

Not a shaped charge - a crowbar, which told him most of what he needed. Six of them through the main entrance, black streetwear over spray-painted tactical surplus, CITIZENS FOR COMPACT ACCOUNTABILITY stenciled across the backs in block letters. The footwork was wrong. The spacing was wrong. Two went straight to phone-filming position, which was exactly right for a news story and exactly wrong for anyone who meant to take a room. Bear read them in about a second: hired street crew, told to make noise, handed a political cover story, no idea they were running interference for a retired colonel collecting on a debt. He'd been wondering what Brandt's third call looked like. This was it - a different kind of favor from a different kind of account.

He didn't stop his testimony.

He told the panel about the morning of the Cortland Engagement and the orders that came down at 0300. The crew got as far as his reach and no further. A man his size doesn't have to do much; he did exactly that much and no more, the way you move furniture out of a doorway, without looking at it and without breaking sentence. The ones with the phones stayed by the door. The panel members were frozen behind their partition glass. The recorder was still running. Brandt had come in during the noise - found the observer's chair behind the glass and sat there watching. Bear could feel him watching, and kept talking.

The remaining Citizens for Compact Accountability left the way they'd come in.

At 1327 a shaped charge went off on the interior service door, and the real team came through. Atlas III chassis, Halcyon load-out. Proper stack, proper spacing, threat assessment inside four seconds - three in the stack, one on the corridor. The ones Brandt had been saving. His first call of the morning, which meant they were the best he had left.

"Terminate the submission," the lead said.

"In a moment," Bear said.

He told the panel why forty-three names appeared nowhere in the official record. He told them about the asset designation that had put those forty-three people in the wrong building at 0400. He was clear and he was complete. The record would carry it or it wouldn't, but it would carry exactly what he had.

What passed between the lead reaching for his arm and the lead sitting back down took less time than a sentence, and cost Bear nothing he had been saving. A trained compliance grip needs the other man to move against it. Bear didn't. He let the wrist come to him and closed a hand around it and held it the way a wall holds - no squeeze, just present - until the man made a professional assessment of his own odds and arrived at the sensible answer. The one who moved to flank arrived at it a moment later. The third never left the door. None of them escalated. Twenty years had taught Bear that the discipline was in the force you chose not to spend, and he spent almost none of it here; he still had the Manowar's charge banked for the two things that would actually ask for it today, and neither of them was this room.

The submission closed at 1344.

The lead sat when Bear let him. The other two read the room.
