Per plan.md, the Casimir/Ekow question is already resolved correctly on the page: the prose
consistently narrates Ekow's own POV beats (3916, 3917, 3918, 3919, 3882, 3883) using his real
name, and narrates him as "Casimir" only inside scenes filtered through Tomas's perception

The only thing genuinely missing is a hard, first-read-friendly link between the closing

now a reader who doesn't recognize Fante day-names has to take it on faith that the two are
connected. This patch is cheap insurance for that reader, nothing more. Skip it if the
day-name image is meant to stay purely atmospheric.
