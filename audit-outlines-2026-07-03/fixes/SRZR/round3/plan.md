# Holdout Fix — Round 3 — Steppin' Razor (SRZR)

Slug `steppin-razor-019ef7be`. Files only — no database writes. Scope: the camel-man / cat /
schism-strangeness register only, per the unanimous 20/20 complaint that it "strains the earned noir
register," split between under- and over-explained. Beat order below is `NodeBeats.SortKey` order
(true reading order); each beat cited by `Beats.Number`.

## The treatment rule

Applied to every beat below: Sasha meets the weird the way she meets everything — signal, read with
instruments she trusts (body first, then pattern). Compliant beats give (1) one concrete sensory
anchor, (2) an instinct-verdict in her own doctrine terms, (3) zero narrator reaching (no
significance-language, no "as if" chains, no questions the narration asks itself). The entity's own
lines stay oblique (locked); the scene *around* him stays procedural. The cat is observed precisely
and never commented on.

## Diagnosis after reading all 31 beats

Round 1 and round 2 already did the heavy lifting: the camel-man's canon-lock violations (Devereux,
Act-1 appearance, the Sigma/Ferreira origin bleed) are gone, and the two round-2 sensory plants
(4863's warm-ground read, 4868's double-called crow) are genuinely compliant with the rule as written
— see verification below. What's left, reading straight through, is **not** an oscillation between
flat and reaching beats — the great majority of the uncanny material already follows the rule. What's
left is a **small, escalating cluster of overworked passages concentrated toward the climax**
(4773, 4775, with smaller instances at 4420, 4422, 4769, 4774, 4876): single sentences or clauses
where the narration stops reporting Sasha's read and starts supplying significance, motive, or a wry
generalization on her behalf. The worst offender (4773, the colonnade) also contains the single clearest
rule violation in the strand: the cat's jaw-touch is directly commented on ("a thing that has something
to say"), which is the exact thing the cat rule bans. If a panel is reading these as "overworked" and
also, elsewhere, calling the entity's own oblique dialogue "underexplained," that's not an
inconsistency to fix by adding more to the flat scenes — the flat scenes (4421, 4423, 4767, 4768,
4873, 4874, the man on the camel's own lines throughout) are doing exactly what the bible's lock
requires (oblique, never mystic fog, instinct not information) and touching them would break canon.
The fix is entirely subtractive: strip the reaching from the handful of beats that have it, so the
register holds flat and procedural end to end, the way it already does for 80%+ of the material.
