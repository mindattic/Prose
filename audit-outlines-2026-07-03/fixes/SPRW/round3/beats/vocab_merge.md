The message comes through at 02:47, nine minutes after he has set the tablet face-down on the railing
and gone inside. He only checks it because he forgot the charging cable and came back for it.

"Substrate-response modeling for sub-threshold resonance conditions is incomplete. To refine: what did
the pavement surface register as, under foot, at the anomalous location? Describe in terms of
resistance, settlement, or lateral give. Approximate depth impression if possible."

He reads it twice. In thirty-seven years of her transmissions he has lost count of them and started
again twice - once after Mombasa, once on a whim that didn't hold. She has answered questions. She has
flagged anomalies, attached model parameters and isotopic correlation tables, and once, in the fourth
year, sent a revised invoice for a bleed event that hadn't been billed correctly, unprompted, accurate
to the decimal. She has never asked him anything.

He carries the tablet back to the railing. The freight-channel markers below are unchanged. He tries
to remember what the pavement felt like the way you try to remember a word in another language -
reaching for the shape of it before the word itself surfaces. The settlement had been wrong. Not
unstable. Wrong the way a floor a half-degree off level is wrong, a quality the knees register before
the brain produces a name for it.

He types: "Like standing on something that has forgotten what it's supporting."

The pause is short, three seconds, not nine. Then:

"Clarify: forgotten. Do you mean the resistance profile was inconsistent with the load-bearing
expectation for that surface classification, or that it varied across the site?"

He looks at the question a moment. Her earlier confirmation is still open in the other window -
temperature delta 2.31K, friction coefficient 0.547, oscillation period 3.97 seconds mean, amplitude
0.003mm, all within modeled range, no anomaly detected. He has already underlined what's absent from
it. The numbers don't have a column for what she's asking him to describe now.

He works through several false starts in the notepad - not the neuretics, the paper one, the Blossom
Arcade with the worn spine - before the sentence holds: the way a floor feels wrong when you walk into
a room and the furniture has been moved in the night. Nothing missing, nothing broken, but the
geometry sits differently under your feet and your body knows before you do.

He types it back: "Like a room where the furniture's been moved in the night. Nothing missing.
Nothing broken. The geometry sits differently and the body knows before it can say why."

"Received. Parsing: 'quality' - no parameter mapping available. 'Wrong' - no parameter mapping
available. These terms describe states I cannot model from available sensor inputs. Please clarify
the physical correlate, if one exists."

He writes back: I don't think one does.

She doesn't reply for a while. He writes in the notepad instead:

she needs the data. she cannot collect it. she is asking me.

He draws a line under it and looks out at the interchange. Eleven years of making loss manifests
reconcile to the gram is finding what doesn't file: the invoice line with no category, the logistics
event with no receiving party. He writes below the line:

depth-wise, like a floor that has given up the pretense of being solid. laterally, like something is
organized below it and does not care about the surface.

there are two things here. the thing in her data and the thing I am standing on. they overlap. they
do not coincide.

He photographs the page and sends it without comment. She won't use the image. She will read the
text. He caps the pen.
