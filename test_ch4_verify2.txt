"The ventilation fan came back on at 0419. I counted it the way I had been counting things all night, without meaning to. One. The second-floor window I had been watching did not come back on. I watched it anyway.

There is a habit your eyes get, of returning to the place where the thing was supposed to be, and they keep returning after the part of you that hopes has gone quiet. Mine returned to that window for the rest of the time we were there. The window stayed dark. I am not going to dress that up for you.
