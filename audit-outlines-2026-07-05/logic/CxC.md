# CxC Logic Sweep — 2026-07-05

## Summary

CxC is largely clean across causality, timeline, and register. One BLOCKER — an east/west core
contradiction introduced within two adjacent beats — requires a single-word fix. Four MODERATE
findings require bible updates or minimal prose patches; none breaks the story's logic. Five MINOR
findings are noted for awareness.

## Findings

### BLOCKER

**[SortKey 550 → 600]** — **East/west core directional contradiction**

Beat 11 (SortKey 550), Vox commits: *"I'm walking up the east fire core and I'm going to keep
talking."* Beat 7 (SortKey 350) earlier planted this: *"You're going to need someone on the east
core."* These are consistent.

Beat 12 (SortKey 600), Rook to Anneke: *"You pivoted your guard off Sefi's column to chase one
loud voice up the **west** core, and twenty-one beds went unwatched long enough for the count to
reach them."*

Vox walked east. Rook tells Anneke west. Same operation, adjacent beats, direct factual
contradiction. A reader tracking the floor plan loses the geography exactly when the spatial logic
of the distraction manoeuvre needs to be legible.

**Minimal fix:** In Beat 12, change "west core" → "east core." One word.

### MODERATE

**[SortKey 500]** — **Scout's physical presence contradicts bible spec (bible needs updating)**

The bible key-facts note reads: *"Scout 'burns a frame' into the Marrow via NSB projection
(Beat 10 is NOT a physical breach)."*

Beat 10 prose is unambiguous: Scout walks the ramp on foot, in her body, in her boots, *"making
weight on the tread surface the way the caster wheels had made weight for years."* Her interiority
is entirely about the "specific wrongness of being **present** like this" — the whole point of her
arc, established in Beat 9 when she argues for riding in rather than holding perimeter. She
physically lifts Sefi: *"she got a forearm under the woman's shoulders."*

The prose is clearly intentional — Scout's character arc is precisely the inversion of her Exo
tradecraft. The bible note is an outdated spec that was overridden during writing. Per project rule
("When bible and prose contradict on a character detail, fix the bible"), the note in the CxC
bible key-facts section must be updated to reflect physical ingress. The NSB-projection framing
and the related Black Ice kill-switch parameters should be removed from the bible note (see also
MINOR-3 below).

**Minimal fix:** Update bible note to: "Scout physically walks the Marrow ramp — in her body, in
her boots — with Gerald beside her. This is deliberate; her arc is the inversion of her Exo
tradecraft. Beat 10 IS a physical breach."

**[SortKey 400]** — **Soraya's partition contents mismatch bible**

Bible: *"What the partition actually contained was Helix's internal biometric access layer —
credentials Soraya seeded before leaving Axiom. This is the door that Beat 8 pays off."*

Beat 8 prose: Soraya opens her partition to reveal *"a research file — dense, cross-referenced ...
the Class V pathway, the tissue requirements, the acquisition logic ... a complete shutdown
procedure."* It is her own documentation, not Helix's credential layer. The credential function
is served entirely by Adalemo's separate slate: *"here's the credential set that walks you into
the only door they kept the lights on for, because I'm still on it."*

The partition's shutdown-procedure contents are load-bearing — Soraya says this information is
what makes the rescue technically possible (*"the only one in this room who knows how to undo it
cleanly"*). The bible description is wrong about what is in the partition, and wrong about who
provides credentials.

**Minimal fix:** Update the bible note to: "Soraya's partition contains her own covert research
file: Class V pathway documentation + a complete shutdown sequence. This is her arc completing —
she stops protecting the data. Adalemo separately provides the access credential (his credential
was never revoked). Both are needed; Beat 8 pays off both."

**[SortKey 100 / 350 / 450 → 550]** — **Stave plant vs. payoff gap**

Beat 2 (SortKey 100): *"Stave's the only one who can get us into the registry vault clean, and he
knows it."* Beat 7 (SortKey 350) maintains this: Stave stands in "the only door you've got."
Beat 9 (SortKey 450) builds the plan around two cracks — the "soft knock" (Stave's vault crack)
and the fallback.

In the actual operation (Beats 10–13), Adalemo's credential is the operative door. The "soft
knock" plan element never appears on-page: we do not see it attempted, detected, or abandoned.
Stave's only on-page contribution to the operation is two seconds of comm static in Beat 11:
*"Stave's line opened last — no words, just the static of a comm left on a table for four years,
live again ... It closed after two seconds."*

The "only one who can get us into the vault clean" promise is never paid back — either because
the soft knock ran offscreen (PEREGRINE's premature reconcile could imply detection) or because
Adalemo's credential made Stave's crack redundant. Neither reading is on-page. A trilogy finale
that extensively plants a reluctant crew-member's irreplaceable function deserves at least an
on-page acknowledgement that the plan changed and why.

**Minimal fix (prose):** In Beat 9 or Beat 10, one sentence from Rook acknowledging that
Adalemo's credential has displaced Stave's vault crack as the primary approach — e.g., a
Rook-interiority note that "the soft knock was already answered" or that Adalemo is "the door
Stave was meant to be." Alternatively, add a beat-level note clarifying the hand-off.

**[SortKey 650 → 700]** — **Secondary-block survivors appear unplanted**

Beat 14 (SortKey 700): *"She left a gap after the last name, enough space for the ten who had
come out of the secondary block."* Soraya explains the secondary block as *"secondary substrate
staging — they moved the lab residuals into that block when the primary facility filled."* Bram
(one of the ten) is named. The count reaches 31.

The rescue of ten people from a "secondary block" is established retroactively. It is never
mentioned during the operation (Beats 9–13). Lace "found" the ten — the only attribution — but
this happens entirely offscreen, with no plant in the planning beats and no on-page moment during
the run. Sefi's Beat 3 line *"I can give you nine"* does not establish a secondary block; it
implies nine of the same twenty-one are reachable.

**Minimal fix:** One line of plant in Beat 9 (planning): Adalemo or Soraya mentions a secondary
substrate staging area adjacent to the primary floor; Rook assigns Lace to sweep it. This gives
the ten survivors a causal anchor before they appear in Beat 14's count.

### MINOR

**MINOR-2 [SortKey 400]** — **Adalemo's chrome seam unexplained**

Beat 8: *"the chrome at his wrist caught the crimson light the same as Sefi's, the same seam, the
same unlogged date."* Sefi's chrome is from Marrow processing. Adalemo's chrome mirrors it
exactly — but his role is that of an administrative clerk who walked out with stolen data, not a
processed Read. The prose does not explain whether Adalemo was himself subjected to Helix
intake or had unsanctioned Splicer work done for another reason. The ambiguity may be intentional
atmosphere; it risks reading as an error if Adalemo was never meant to have been processed.

No fix required if the ambiguity is deliberate. If it is unintentional, one phrase in Beat 5 or 8
clarifying the origin of the seam (Splicer work to pass internal biometric gates, for example)
would close it.

**MINOR-4 [SortKey 550]** — **Gerald called "her oldest Shell"**

Beat 11: *"Gerald — her oldest Shell, the deal that held even here."* The project canon defines
Shell as an inhabited machine (NSB host body). Gerald is a six-legged crawler — hardware, not a
Shell in the canonical sense. The phrasing could cause confusion about Gerald's nature for
readers familiar with the Shell/Husk distinction. Scout inhabits Gerald during NSB runs on other
jobs; in CxC she is physically present and Gerald is walking beside her, so the "Shell" label is
technically inaccurate here.

**Minimal fix:** *"her oldest crawler"* or *"her oldest rig"* to match Beat 2's established
framing (*"a salvaged six-legged crawler"*).

---

**MINOR-5 [SortKey 450]** — **"CREDENTIAL OBSERVED" flag orphaned from bible**

The bible round-2 continuity note reads: *"Helix CREDENTIAL OBSERVED flag forces Rook to abandon
registry probe mid-reach in B9."* Beat 9 prose contains no registry probe by Rook and no
CREDENTIAL OBSERVED event. This note describes an action that is either offscreen or was cut.
Given MODERATE-3 (Stave's vault crack also absent from the operation), this may be part of the
same dropped subplot. The bible note should be cleaned up when the Stave/soft-knock arc is
addressed.
