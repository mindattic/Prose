- Phase 0 data: 200,000 Z6 registered neuretics users — combined with Headcount = complete targeting profile

THREE JOBS → ONE PLAN:
1. Art recovery: Headcount data sculpture (Φ12,000 contract from Ferris)
2. Defector extraction: Lee Nari from Z6 Lotus territory
3. Civic grid exfiltration: Priya + firmware backdoor disabling
