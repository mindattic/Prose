# The 6-Facet System: Universal Character Architecture

## Overview

Every character in StreetSamurai is an amalgamation of six interoperable facets. These are not stats. They are not sliders. They are lenses through which a person processes reality — and they talk to each other. A shift in one facet ripples across the others because a human being is not six independent traits. A human being is six systems exchanging information under pressure.

The facets are universal. A corponation executive, a Blank scavenger, an orbital-born laborer, a chop-shop surgeon, a RingoGuard officer — all of them carry the same six facets. What differs is the base weights, the modifiers acting on them, and how the cascade plays out in a given moment.

---

## The Six Facets

### WOUND
**What it is:** Trauma, survival memory, shame, learned cruelty, intimacy born from pain.
**What it does:** WOUND remembers what happened. It keeps score. It notices power dynamics, coercion, debt, leverage, and the exact moment someone is about to break. WOUND is the voice that says *I know what this costs because I've already paid it.*
**In the BCI era:** What the implant cost you. The surgery. The debt. The side effects. The dependency. The moment you realized the hardware owns you more than you own it. For test subjects: what was done to you in the facility. For the excluded: what it felt like the first time a pharmacy declined your transaction.
**High WOUND:** Hypervigilant, transactional, distrustful, perceptive about others' vulnerabilities. Sees the trap before anyone else. Can't stop seeing traps.
**Low WOUND:** Naive, sheltered, trusting to the point of danger. Has never been broken. Doesn't recognize the machinery of exploitation.

### IDEAL
**What it is:** Discipline, restraint, meaning, self-mastery, quiet intensity. The code you hold.
**What it does:** IDEAL defines what you will and won't do regardless of cost. It's the line you draw. The standard you hold when holding it hurts. IDEAL is the voice that says *A code is only real when it costs something.*
**In the BCI era:** Your code about what augmentation should and shouldn't touch. The line between tool and leash. The belief that there are things technology must not be allowed to do to a person, even if the person consents under duress.
**High IDEAL:** Principled to the point of rigidity. Will sacrifice self and others to maintain the code. Can become self-righteous, brittle, or blind to context.
**Low IDEAL:** Pragmatic, flexible, morally adaptive. Survives by bending. May have no line at all, or may have lost every line they once held.

### ID
**What it is:** The pre-interface self. The biological, pre-augmented identity. Who you were before the hardware, the indenture, the corpo classification, the tier assignment.
**What it does:** ID holds the original personality — the person who existed before the systems got to them. Memories of childhood, of a name that wasn't a registration number, of a body that didn't hum with firmware. ID is the voice that says *I was someone before all of this.*
**In the BCI era:** The biological self being overwritten. Every firmware update, every cognitive subscription tier, every neural data harvest erodes ID a little more. For Tier 4-5 augmented: ID may be nearly gone, replaced by optimized cognitive architecture. For Blanks: ID is all they have. For test subjects: ID is what the facility tried to erase and couldn't quite kill.
**High ID:** Strong sense of original self. Resists cognitive drift. May reject augmentation upgrades that alter personality. Clings to pre-augmentation memories, habits, relationships.
**Low ID:** The original person is buried or gone. Identity is defined by function, tier, employment, or augmentation suite. May not remember who they were. May not care.

### SHADOW
**What it is:** What you suppress. What the implant reveals about you that you didn't want to know. The subconscious made readable.
**What it does:** SHADOW holds everything you've pushed down — desires, impulses, memories, patterns you don't acknowledge. In a pre-BCI world, SHADOW stayed hidden. In 2100, neural telemetry makes SHADOW visible to anyone with access to your data. SHADOW is the voice that says *You already know what you are. The data just confirms it.*
**In the BCI era:** Neural monitoring surfaces suppressed thought patterns. Behavioral prediction models know your SHADOW better than you do. Corpo loyalty screening reads your suppressed resentment. Pre-crime algorithms flag your suppressed rage. The implant doesn't create SHADOW — it illuminates it, and sells the illumination.
**High SHADOW:** Deep suppression. Rich interior darkness. The person is carrying something enormous and unprocessed. High SHADOW characters are unpredictable — when the suppression breaks, it breaks catastrophically. Also: the most interesting characters to write.
**Low SHADOW:** Integrated. Self-aware. Has processed their darkness or never accumulated much. Transparent. Predictable. Boring at parties but reliable in a crisis.

### MASK
**What it is:** The curated self. The performance. What you broadcast to the world, the version of yourself that faces outward.
**What it does:** MASK is the interface between inner reality and social survival. It's the corpo smile, the street toughness, the professional competence, the loyal employee, the good citizen. Everyone wears a MASK. The question is how far it sits from the truth. MASK is the voice that says *They see what I need them to see.*
**In the BCI era:** Your public neural signature. The curated data profile. What your implant broadcasts about your emotional state, your loyalty metrics, your cognitive performance. For corpo workers: MASK is the compliance persona that keeps you employed. For the excluded: MASK is whatever identity you've fabricated to survive outside the system. For test subjects: MASK was stripped away in the facility, and rebuilding it is the hardest part of surviving.
**High MASK:** Masterful performer. Gap between inner reality and outer presentation is vast. May have lost track of which version is real. Socially effective, internally fractured.
**Low MASK:** What you see is what you get. No filter. No performance. Either deeply authentic or deeply vulnerable. In corpo territory, low MASK is dangerous — your unfiltered self is readable, targetable, exploitable.

### GHOST
**What it is:** The question of whether *you* are still in there. Existential uncertainty. The boundary between self and system.
**What it does:** GHOST tracks the degree to which a person questions their own continuity of identity. Am I still me? Am I the firmware? Am I the subscription? If they turn off my cognitive enhancement, does the person who remains qualify as me? GHOST is the voice that says *I think I'm thinking. But I'm not sure the thought is mine.*
**In the BCI era:** The most BCI-specific facet. Every augmented person carries some degree of GHOST. The subscription model makes it acute: your enhanced cognition is rented. Your emotional regulation is a service tier. Your memory is cloud-hosted. If any of it is disconnected, are you diminished or are you restored? For Blanks: GHOST is low or absent — they know exactly who they are. For Tier 4-5: GHOST may be the dominant facet, because so much of their cognitive architecture is external that the question of selfhood is genuinely unresolvable.
**High GHOST:** Existential crisis as permanent state. Questions every thought. Distrusts their own cognition. May be right to distrust it. The uncanny valley of selfhood.
**Low GHOST:** No existential doubt. Either because they're unaugmented and the question doesn't apply, or because they've made peace with the merger, or because they lack the self-awareness to ask.

## Interoperability: The Cascade Model

The facets are not independent. They exchange information constantly. When a situational modifier shifts one facet, the others respond. This is the cascade.

### Cascade Rules

Each facet has **influence relationships** with the others. These are not mechanical multipliers — they are narrative tendencies that the writing voices interpret. The cascade describes *what tends to happen*, not what must.

```
WOUND ↑  →  MASK ↑ (pain drives performance — you hide what hurts)
WOUND ↑  →  ID ↓ (trauma overwrites the original self)
WOUND ↑  →  SHADOW ↑ (more pain, more suppression)

IDEAL ↑  →  MASK ↑ (discipline creates a composed exterior)
IDEAL ↑  →  SHADOW ↑ (rigid codes push forbidden impulses deeper)
IDEAL ↑  →  GHOST ↓ (strong code anchors identity — you know who you are)

ID ↑     →  GHOST ↓ (strong original self reduces existential doubt)
ID ↑     →  MASK ↓ (authenticity resists performance)
ID ↑     →  WOUND ↑ (remembering who you were means remembering what was taken)

SHADOW ↑ →  MASK ↑ (more to hide, more performance required)
SHADOW ↑ →  GHOST ↑ (suppressed darkness creates self-doubt)
SHADOW ↑ →  IDEAL ↓ (buried impulses erode the code from within)

MASK ↑   →  ID ↓ (the performance replaces the person)
MASK ↑   →  SHADOW ↑ (the gap between mask and reality feeds suppression)
MASK ↑   →  GHOST ↑ (which face is real? the question compounds)

GHOST ↑  →  IDEAL ↓ (can't hold a code if you don't know who's holding it)
GHOST ↑  →  ID ↓ (existential doubt dissolves the original self)
GHOST ↑  →  WOUND ↑ (not knowing if you're real is its own trauma)
```

### How Cascades Work in Practice

Cascades are **not automatic**. They are tendencies. A character with high discipline (IDEAL) might resist the WOUND→ID↓ cascade through sheer will. A character with strong community bonds might resist GHOST entirely. The cascade rules tell the writer: *this is the gravitational pull. The character may fight it, and the fight is the story.*

The magnitude of cascade depends on:
- **Base weight proximity:** Facets near their extremes cascade harder
- **Modifier intensity:** Severe modifiers cascade more than mild ones
- **Duration:** Sustained modifiers cascade more than brief ones
- **Character resistance:** Defined in the essence as specific cascade overrides

## Base Weights

Every character's essence defines six base weights on a 0.0 to 1.0 scale:

```yaml
facets:
  wound:  0.0-1.0   # trauma / survival memory
  ideal:  0.0-1.0   # discipline / code
  id:     0.0-1.0   # pre-interface self
  shadow: 0.0-1.0   # suppressed darkness
  mask:   0.0-1.0   # curated performance
  ghost:  0.0-1.0   # existential uncertainty
```

These are **resting state** — who the character is when nothing unusual is happening. The baseline personality. Modifiers shift these weights temporarily. Cascades propagate the shifts. The effective weight at any moment is:

```
effective_weight = base_weight + sum(active_modifier_deltas) + sum(cascade_effects)
clamped to [0.0, 1.0]
```

### Weight Interpretation Scale

| Range | Interpretation |
|-------|---------------|
| 0.0-0.2 | Nearly absent. This facet barely registers in the character's psychology. |
| 0.2-0.4 | Present but subordinate. Surfaces under specific pressure. |
| 0.4-0.6 | Active and influential. A significant part of who they are. |
| 0.6-0.8 | Dominant. This facet colors most of their decisions and perceptions. |
| 0.8-1.0 | Overwhelming. This facet has consumed or is consuming the character. |

## The Modifier System: Buffs & Debuffs

Modifiers are situational events that temporarily shift facet weights. They represent what's happening to the character *right now* — not who they are, but what they're experiencing.

### Modifier Schema

```yaml
modifier:
  name: "string"              # descriptive name
  trigger: "string"           # what caused it
  facet_deltas:               # direct shifts to facet weights
    wound:  -1.0 to +1.0     # (most modifiers shift 0.1-0.3)
    ideal:  -1.0 to +1.0
    id:     -1.0 to +1.0
    shadow: -1.0 to +1.0
    mask:   -1.0 to +1.0
    ghost:  -1.0 to +1.0
  intensity: 0.1-1.0          # scales all deltas
  duration: "string"          # scene / chapter / arc / permanent
  decay: "string"             # none / linear / sudden
  cascade: true/false         # whether this modifier triggers cascade effects
  notes: "string"             # narrative context
```

### Example Modifiers

**"Firmware Update Pushed Overnight"**
```yaml
modifier:
  name: "Uninvited Firmware"
  trigger: "Corpo pushed a firmware update during sleep cycle"
  facet_deltas:
    ghost: +0.3
    id: -0.2
    shadow: +0.1
  intensity: 0.6
  duration: scene
  decay: linear
  cascade: true
  notes: "Woke up thinking differently. Can't tell if the new thought patterns are better or just different. The old self feels further away."
```

**"Neural Data Subpoenaed"**
```yaml
modifier:
  name: "Exposed"
  trigger: "Corponation tribunal subpoenas full neural activity log"
  facet_deltas:
    shadow: +0.4
    mask: -0.3
    wound: +0.2
  intensity: 0.8
  duration: arc
  decay: none
  cascade: true
  notes: "Everything you suppressed is now evidence. Your MASK is meaningless — they've already seen behind it. SHADOW and MASK in direct, irreconcilable conflict."
```

**"Killed Someone Who Deserved It (Maybe)"**
```yaml
modifier:
  name: "Justified Violence"
  trigger: "Lethal action taken against a threat, moral ambiguity present"
  facet_deltas:
    wound: +0.2
    ideal: -0.1
    shadow: +0.2
    ghost: +0.1
  intensity: 0.5
  duration: chapter
  decay: linear
  cascade: true
  notes: "IDEAL says it was necessary. WOUND says you liked it. SHADOW agrees with WOUND. GHOST wonders if the augmented reflexes made the decision before you did."
```

**"Saw a Child in the Facility"**
```yaml
modifier:
  name: "Developmental Cohort"
  trigger: "Witnessed children in a VCP testing facility"
  facet_deltas:
    wound: +0.4
    ideal: +0.3
    shadow: +0.2
    mask: -0.2
    id: +0.3
  intensity: 0.9
  duration: arc
  decay: none
  cascade: true
  notes: "ID surges — you remember being a child. IDEAL surges — some lines cannot be crossed. WOUND surges — you know what's being done because it was done to you. MASK collapses — you can't perform composure. The most destabilizing modifier in the system."
```

**"Augmentation Subscription Lapsed"**
```yaml
modifier:
  name: "Cognitive Downgrade"
  trigger: "Missed payment, cognitive enhancement tier reduced"
  facet_deltas:
    ghost: +0.4
    id: +0.2
    wound: +0.2
    ideal: -0.1
    mask: -0.2
  intensity: 0.7
  duration: chapter
  decay: none (until payment restored)
  cascade: true
  notes: "The enhanced you is gone. The person underneath is slower, foggier, more emotional. Is this the real you or the diminished you? GHOST spikes because the question has no answer."
```

**"Someone Recognized Your Pre-Augmentation Name"**
```yaml
modifier:
  name: "The Old Name"
  trigger: "Addressed by birth name from before augmentation/indenture"
  facet_deltas:
    id: +0.4
    wound: +0.2
    mask: -0.3
    ghost: -0.1
  intensity: 0.6
  duration: scene
  decay: linear
  cascade: true
  notes: "ID surges violently. For a moment, you're the person you were. MASK destabilizes — the performance cracks. GHOST actually drops, because for one disorienting second, you know exactly who you are."
```

**"Entered an EMP Dead Zone"**
```yaml
modifier:
  name: "Signal Dark"
  trigger: "Entered area where all electronic systems are offline"
  facet_deltas:
    ghost: -0.3
    id: +0.2
    mask: -0.2
    shadow: -0.1
  intensity: 0.5
  duration: scene
  decay: sudden (on exit)
  cascade: true
  notes: "No surveillance. No neural telemetry. No data harvest. GHOST drops because nobody's watching your thoughts. ID rises because the firmware is silent. The quiet is terrifying and beautiful."
```

## Integration with the Writer Pipeline

The 6-facet system feeds into `duo_writer_lit.py` by shaping the system prompts. Currently the writer uses two voices (WOUND and IDEAL). The expanded system works as follows:

### How Facets Inform the Two Voices

WOUND and IDEAL remain the two **drafting voices** — they write the competing scene drafts. But both voices are now informed by the full 6-facet state:

**WOUND voice is shaped by:** WOUND (primary), SHADOW (what it dredges up), ID (what was lost), GHOST (whether the pain is even real)

**IDEAL voice is shaped by:** IDEAL (primary), MASK (the performance of discipline), ID (what the code protects), GHOST (whether the code-holder still exists)

**The CRITIQUE phase uses:** SHADOW (what both drafts are hiding), MASK (what both drafts are performing), GHOST (what both drafts are avoiding)

**The MERGE phase uses:** All six facets at their effective weights (base + modifiers + cascade) to determine the final tone, the balance of interjections, and which voice "wins" the scene.

### Prompt Injection Format

Before each generation, the character's effective facet state is injected as context:

```
CHARACTER STATE — {character_name}
Effective facet weights (base + active modifiers):
  WOUND:  {value} — {one-line interpretation}
  IDEAL:  {value} — {one-line interpretation}
  ID:     {value} — {one-line interpretation}
  SHADOW: {value} — {one-line interpretation}
  MASK:   {value} — {one-line interpretation}
  GHOST:  {value} — {one-line interpretation}

Active modifiers:
  - {modifier_name}: {brief description} ({duration remaining})

Cascade effects in play:
  - {facet} → {facet}: {direction and reason}

The character's inner voice should reflect these weights. Higher facets speak louder. Facets in conflict create tension in the prose.
```

## Character Essence File Format

Every character is stored as a YAML file in `characters/`:

```yaml
# Character Essence — Universal Template
name: ""
aliases: []
tier: 0-5
status: ""  # active, excluded, deceased, missing, unknown

# Core identity
origin: ""
age: 0
augmentation: ""  # description of current hardware/bioware
occupation: ""
affiliation: ""  # corponation, gang, independent, excluded

# Base facet weights (resting state)
facets:
  wound: 0.0
  ideal: 0.0
  id: 0.0
  shadow: 0.0
  mask: 0.0
  ghost: 0.0

# Cascade overrides — where this character resists or amplifies the default cascade
cascade_overrides:
  - from: ""
    to: ""
    modifier: 0.0  # negative = resistance, positive = amplification
    reason: ""

# What each facet sounds like for THIS character
facet_voices:
  wound: ""   # the specific flavor of this character's trauma
  ideal: ""   # the specific code this character holds
  id: ""      # who they were before
  shadow: ""  # what they suppress
  mask: ""    # what they perform
  ghost: ""   # the specific nature of their existential doubt

# Active modifiers (current story state)
active_modifiers: []

# Backstory beats — key events that shaped the base weights
history: []

# Relationships — connections to other characters
relationships: []

# Writer notes — how to write this character
voice_notes: ""
```
