# Corponation

**Corponation** (n.) [kor-poh-NAY-shun] — compound: corporation + nation

## Legal Definition

A corponation is a private commercial entity that has, through the accumulation of economic, technological, or infrastructural dominance, attained a degree of sovereign-equivalent authority that functionally supersedes or operates independently of the governing power of one or more recognized nation-states or subnational jurisdictions.

A corponation is distinguished from an ordinary corporation by the presence of all of the following qualifying characteristics:

### 1. Sovereign Override Capacity
The entity exercises regulatory, legislative, or enforcement authority within its operational domain that is recognized — whether formally or through practical non-interference — as taking precedence over applicable governmental law.

### 2. Standing Security Force
The entity maintains a permanent, organized, armed force under its direct command and charter, capable of enforcement, deterrence, detention, or defensive military action, not subject to the exclusive command authority of any national military or law enforcement body.

### 3. Proprietary Jurisdiction
The entity enforces a discrete body of internal law — including but not limited to codes of conduct, civil penalties, surveillance authority, and adjudicative processes — upon all persons physically present within any space it owns, operates, leases, franchises, or otherwise controls, regardless of that space's national or municipal location.

### 4. Extraterritorial Legal Continuity
The entity's internal legal code travels with it — applying to its personnel, contractors, customers, and visitors across jurisdictions — creating a portable legal environment not contingent upon the laws of the host territory.

## Distinguishing from Related Entities

- **vs. Private Military Company**: A corponation is primarily a commercial enterprise, with security force powers incidental to — rather than the primary product of — its operations.
- **vs. Company Town**: A corponation's jurisdiction is not geographically fixed but extends to all controlled spaces globally, and its authority is not merely administrative but coercive and enforceable.
- **vs. State-Owned Enterprise**: A corponation derives authority through market dominance rather than governmental delegation, and may operate in explicit tension with or supersession of sovereign governments.

## Usage Note

The corponation represents a post-sovereign legal category not currently recognized under international law, though historical and emerging precedents — including extraterritorial corporate security arrangements, private arbitration systems that supersede national courts, and corporate governance of critical infrastructure — suggest the category describes an increasingly observable real-world phenomenon rather than a purely theoretical one.
