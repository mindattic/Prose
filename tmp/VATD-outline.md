## Voice & Prose Laws (SS-A31 + Register - MANDATORY)

### Accessible Prose (SS-A31)
- Sentences: short-to-medium. Break any clause over ~20 words.
- Narration uses contractions naturally: he's, it wasn't, couldn't, didn't, hadn't.
- Vocabulary: Anglo-Saxon base words preferred. Avoid Latinate abstractions.
- No stacked subordinate clauses.
- No pseudo-profundity. Metaphors survive literal scrutiny.
- No filler-wit. No wry universal-truth narrator lines.

### Tomas Alvarado's Register - Blue-Collar Pragmatic (PRIMARY POV)
Close-third on Tomas. He is a family man with steady hands who does an ugly job because the work is reliable and he has a daughter and a therapy bill. The narration notices practical things: what his hands are doing, what the job requires, what it costs. He does not editorialize. He is not cynical about the work - he has compartmentalized it. When the compartment fails, the prose doesn't announce the failure; it shows his hands slowing down.

**His register:** procedural, blue-collar, no-nonsense. He fixes things. He maintains the Scarab. He does not make speeches. When he's angry at Levin, the anger comes out in efficiency - shorter movements, less checking his partner's work.

**The moral turn:** He fires the shot he would give a year of his life not to fire. The guilt doesn't appear as reflection - it appears as the way he cannot stop working after. Work is the thing his hands know.

### Ekow Ato's Register - Hunter (SECONDARY POV, 4 chapters)
He reads people the way a hunter reads ground. His register notices what is *carried* rather than stated: posture, weight distribution, the angle of someone's attention. His instrument is the look - "your lies written on you like a confession letter." This lands before he says anything. He is menacing in stillness, not in aggression.

His arc: the leashed dog who lights the match on the house that owns him. He doesn't announce the turn. He does it, efficiently, and then the work is done.

### BANNED in this strand
- Kyle's register: arithmetic/filing/parliament/geometry/math-thought - never in Tomas's or Ekow's narration.
- Sentimentality about the work. The story's power comes from when the compartment fails - not from Tomas feeling bad about it ahead of time.
- Over-explanation of the comedic irony. The Bram Osei situation is never commented on. The reader feels it.

## World Texture (GLMZ - ground every scene here)

**Sky communities (SS-A32/A33):** Aeropods — Aeropolis — Drifters. Altitude = class axis. The Low (30-320m ungoverned). "Little birdies"/"Harpies" = sky-dweller insults from below.

**Underwater communities:** Hermetic domes on the lakebed + repurposed port infrastructure. "Fishmen"/"Mermaids" = outsider insults. No Arcturus Civil Security below surface.

**Micro-schisms:** Pebble clusters (~2 inches) floating in complex orbits around apparently nothing. Street corners, gutters. Treated like weather. Nobody explains them.

**Nanite gutters (stopped):** Sanitation nanites stopped coming around. Streets grimier. Iridescent film in neglected zones. Corpo-adjacent districts still pristine - class marker.

**Job vocabulary:** Ghost/Channeler (Shell; Husk). Splicer. Tunneler. Scavs (lowercase).

**Infrastructure:** Pulse (Mach 6 vacuum tube). No trains. VTOLs = Tier 3+. No police - Arcturus Civil Security only.

**Φ** = QUANTA (never phi). Iowan Behemoths = machines, not life.

## Story - Vultures at the Door (VATD)

**Genre:** Dark comedy / noir. VATD-style black humor - the ironic gap between professional competence and moral squalor is never commented on. The reader feels it. Characters can't.

**The job:** Tomas Alvarado and Levin "Lev" Calder operate cardiac repos for the body-organ lending system. They repossess the financed hearts of people who default. It is legal. It is ugly. They do it because someone has to and the pay is steady.

**The inciting act:** They repo a heart from someone who turns out to be the Lotus Syndicate heir (Han Do-yun), at the wrong time. The IdentiScan confirms his identity moments after the shot, once the room's first shock passes - not instant, but still inside the same unbroken beat of the scene. The conscience arrives too late.

**The hunt:** The heart needs to get back to Do-yun alive. Han Jeong-suk (Do-yun's mother, Lotus Stem) sends Ekow after the "saboteurs."

**The black comedy engine:** Tomas and Levin convince Ekow to look the other way (earning: he's been the Syndicate's despised outsider dog - used, never admitted). Ekow flips and helps them get Do-yun home and stage the damning evidence. Bram Osei - whose silence the Vultures bought and whose arm Levin voided the recall on - is tracked down and interrogated to death by Ekow, working the same trail backward. Hours later, a Full Repossession order (the company's routine response to a dead debtor with no next of kin) sends the Vultures back to strip what's left of him, so they end up completing, unwittingly, what the hunter started: the man whose silence they'd purchased is stripped clean by the very people who paid for it. This is never commented on.

**The bookend:** The book opens and closes on a second, quieter thread - a contested-account kidney repo (an old man, daughter Daria Holme) that Tomas performs under an explicit litigation hold he cannot stop the Wagon from carrying him to. He never resolves it in the moment; the book's actual closing scene is Tomas quietly reversing that one wrong the next day, in the same 24-hour span as the Do-yun job. This frames the whole book, but is easy to miss since it never touches the Do-yun plot directly.

## Characters

**Tomas Alvarado (PRIMARY POV):** Cardiac repo technician. Family man - daughter Paz, therapy bill that never clears. Operates the Scarab. Steady hands - this is his identity and the thing that destroys him when those hands fire the round. NOT a narrator-voice character; a hands-and-work character.

**Levin "Lev" Calder (secondary; narration uses Levin):** Tomas's partner. Heartless-efficient in the opening - no flinch. Heavy Tears user. Has nothing to protect. He spooks Han Do-yun, his gun snags (Tears-slow), and Tomas has to fire. Tomas is furious. The hunt is how they mend fences.

**Ekow Ato:** Lotus hunter (Ekow Ato; Ghanaian NGRA vet; hunter register). "Your lies written on you like a confession letter." Menacing in stillness. Gets a real arc: figures it out, corners them, they earn his turn. He flips and helps.

**Maisy Achterberg (Soledad in entity record):** OD'd donor. Levin knew her (prom dates, sixteen years ago). She dies of a plain Velvet overdose - not arranged. Her rare-type heart becomes Do-yun's. Play it dry. Note (VATD-1, 2026-07-18): "Soledad" is entity-level lore only - it never surfaces in the Chapter 3 corneal-retrieval/tissue-scan prose, and stays off-page by design. Closed; no prose addition needed.

**Yuen:** Off-book surgeon Tomas and Levin bring the black-market transplant to - a converted loading-bay clinic in the lower Ward. Former North Tier Splicer; left that world once the line between a heart from a clinic and a heart from somewhere else blurred past seeing. Moral numbness played dry, never confessed. Recurring voice: clipped, procedural questions ("What's the phenotype?" / "How long has the donor been packed?"), and the callback phrase "I'm not asking about the window" - the old, unspoken shorthand between her and Tomas for *don't make me say it and I won't make you hear it*. Performs Do-yun's transplant (with the Scarab/Mama closing behind her); in the same scene she warns Tomas of a watcher casing her door, seeding the sense that the hunt is closing in. Not romantic, not comic relief - a mirror of Tomas's own compartmentalization, one door further down the same hallway.

2. Levin's opening repo: NO FLINCH - no emotional or moral hesitation, ever. (This is distinct from the Tears-slowed holster snag later in the same scene, which is a mechanical drug-impairment failure, not a flinch.) If he flinches, the story collapses.
3. Tomas fires the shot (RETCON 2026-06-28) - Levin spooks the heir, his gun snags; Tomas fires.
4. Maisy dies of a plain Velvet overdose - NOT arranged.
5. The reinstall is a procedure, not a speech. Do not underline it.
