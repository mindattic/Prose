The array wrote one line to its deepest diagnostic layer and then hid the line from every system Kyle knew how to read.

No alert. No new voice. Only a cold, precise change at the seam where the governed hardware met the ungoverned lattice, as if something on the far side of the Weather had tied off a thread and left it slack.

Kyle did not feel it. He was too busy keeping Imani breathing and the bike pointed at the hospital.
