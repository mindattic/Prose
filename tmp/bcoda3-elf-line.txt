Ledger called it a dormant E.L.F., a carrier-facing mechanism waiting for a frequency that might never surface again. Kyle heard the word *mechanism* and thought trap.

But the printout's deepest line did not look built to receive anything. It looked, absurdly, like a route out: one end buried in the scar left by Cinderfall, the other reaching toward a place no map could name. He folded the thought away before it could become hope. A line was not proof of a way home. It was only proof that something had expected him to need one.
