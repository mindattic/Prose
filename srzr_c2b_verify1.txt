The node was a maintenance junction box bolted to the underside of the bridge's eastern footing — the kind of housing that held nothing worth stealing, which was exactly why someone had used it.

She found it hand over hand along the footing, patient, the way you find anything down here without a schematic - until the housing gave itself away, a hand-width of green-corroded steel that didn't belong to the bridge's own hardware.

The service hatch had a panel with no business reading her thumb. It read her thumb anyway. The hatch sighed open.
