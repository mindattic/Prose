The contractor brings his Gauss rifle up, and Kyle already knows how the next second goes. Thirty seconds ago this was a conversation — an Arcturus contractor deciding whether to shoot Kyle tonight. He decided yes, and he brought a rifle to put an exclamation point on it: one hypersonic slug, dead straight, nothing in the world to argue with it. He fires it at Kyle's chest. Kyle drops. The slug finds nothing but the air he just left — and it does not stop. It crosses the intersection in a flat line, punches through the windshield of an ambulance running its lights north, and kills the driver where he sits. Steered by no one now, the ambulance slews into a stalled hauler and T-bones it, and the impact throws the back doors wide. And there in the wreck-light, strapped to a gurney inside, is a small girl — delivered to Kyle's feet by a shot that was meant for him, like the universe went to great trouble to make the introduction. Kyle comes up with Chorus already speaking; one round takes the contractor's trigger hand apart at the wrist, and the rifle clatters off to begin a quieter life on the pavement. Then Kyle looks at the girl, and the girl waves. He waves back.
