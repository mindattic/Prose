Vance is already in the lane with the gurney — tall, brown skin, hair tied back, scrubs over a body that has run a lot of miles today — and doesn't waste a syllable. "On the gurney." Kyle is already lowering her. The harness comes off in two practiced motions. Imani's hand stays in his coat a half-second after the rest of her is on the gurney, and when he works it loose she asks the question every kid eventually has to put to somebody. "Will I see you again?" Kyle doesn't lie to her; he's been lied to by kinder people than himself, and it never once helped. "No," he says. "Probably not." She takes it the way she's taken everything tonight — straight, no flinch, filed somewhere for later. "Thank you for the loud," she says. "Anytime, kid," he says — the one gentle lie he lets himself, and the only one she lets him have. "Tell the heart I said hi."
