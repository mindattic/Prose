Here is how Kyle knows it's sixty minutes and not sixty-one — the same way he knew the contractor's trigger a beat early. Everyone in the GLMZ — the drowned-Midwest sprawl that stopped being a dozen cities and started being one long vertical argument — runs Neuretics: compute grown into the skull and worked by thought, no jack, no screen, just knowing. Most people's stay inside the safe envelope. Kyle's is a military NeoCortex tuned so far past it that it is, slowly and without drama, killing him, and he is at peace with that. Somebody built him into a weapon when he was too young to be asked, and he has spent a long time deciding the one thing he actually gets to decide: who the weapon is for. Tonight it's for something that weighs less than his jacket and wants to know how fast the bike goes.
