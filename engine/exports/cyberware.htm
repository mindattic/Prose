description The Understudy is a subdermal musculoskeletal augmentation system consisting of a network of shape-memory alloy microfibers implanted in parallel with the body's major muscle groups, connected by a distributed processing harness anchored at the thoracic spine. The Understudy was developed by Arcturus for heavy infantry and breaching specialists who needed sustained force output without the joint degradation of cruder exo-augmentation. It has since migrated into the private security market and, inevitably, into the fighting circuits. The street version, manufactured from inferior alloy stock by unlicensed fab shops, is prone to a failure mode operators call 'overclock echo' — the harness misreads a signal and pre-fires, causing an involuntary, full-force muscle contraction. Groundwork is less common than other augmentations because its benefits are subtle and require significant training to exploit.
